                           The Board holds the ultimate responsibility for setting the strategic
                           direction on sustainability issues, with dedicated oversight provided by
                           Board committees whose mandates now encompass sustainability. The
                           Board is supported by an executive-led Sustainability Steering Committee
                           (SSC), which offers strategic guidance on major sustainability matters.
                           Day-to-day implementation of sustainability strategies and initiatives is
                           managed by a cross-functional Committee and Working Group (CWG),
                           consisting of management representatives from business units and
                           led by the Sustainability Department (SD). This integrated governance
                           structure is designed to ensure that sustainability is deeply embedded
                           throughout all levels of our organisation.

                           The Board recognises the crucial role of sustainability in today’s business
                           landscape and is committed to cultivating knowledgeable and capable
                           leadership to provide stringent oversight of sustainability matters. Board
                           members are committed to staying informed about sustainability issues,
                           including climate-related risks and opportunities. Additionally, the Board
                           actively works to continuously assess and enhance its sustainability
                           expertise through ongoing leadership development programmes.

                           Our commitment to long-term value creation is integral to our evaluation
                           of Board and senior management performance. This commitment
                           includes our efforts to cultivate sustainable business practices that
                           positively impact society and the environment. We have also integrated
                           the principle of “shared growth” into our approach, ensuring that our
                           pursuit of business and operational targets aligns with stakeholder
                           expectations, environmental stewardship, and the well-being of the
                           communities we serve. This holistic approach enables the Group to
                           achieve growth and profitability while simultaneously contributing to
                           societal and environmental betterment.

         Water Conservation

                          SUSTAINABLE MANAGEMENT OF WATER AND EFFLUENTS

  WHY IS THIS IMPORTANT?

  Water scarcity presents a material risk to cocoa processing operations, where water serves as a critical input for
  multiple production stages including processing and cooling systems. Water stewardship also carries significant
  regulatory implications, with evolving environmental standards and increasing compliance requirements
  across our operating regions.

  Concious of our water usage, responsible water management demonstrates our commitment to local
  environmental stewardship while supporting our social license to operate. This multilayered importance—
  spanning operational necessity, regulatory compliance and environmental responsibility—makes water
  management fundamental to our long-term business resilience.

  OUR APPROACH

Effective and sustainable water management is a core component of our dedication to water conservation. To
support this, we have incorporated our robust commitments and operational practices into comprehensive Group-
wide Environmental and Sustainable Development policies that span the entire Group.

Water Conservancy
In our dedication to responsible water management across the Group, we maintain a vigilant approach to
monitoring water utilisation in our manufacturing processes, benchmarking optimal water usage as a key standard
for achieving operational excellence.

We have established strategies to enhance water conservation within our operational processes and facilitate
increased water conservation, underscored by improvements made on our innovative automation systems
and machinery to minimise water usage in our factories. Furthermore, our ongoing efforts extend to promoting
responsible practices among our workforce.

Water Discharge Management
We are deeply committed to responsible wastewater treatment, demonstrating our dedication to environmental
stewardship through stringent management practices. Beyond merely meeting regulatory requirements, we strive
to exceed them by actively reducing effluent contaminants, reflecting our proactive approach to sustainability and
community well-being.
We are committed to efficient resource management by reusing treated wastewater for non-critical purposes, such
as cleaning outdoor areas. To enhance our wastewater management, we have invested in new equipment that
improves our treatment processes. Additionally, we have prioritised employee development by sending our staff
to competency courses in wastewater treatment, ensuring they are well-equipped with the necessary skills and
knowledge to effectively manage our wastewater initiatives.

We simultaneously conduct regular monitoring of our treated wastewater, employing recommended water quality
tests to assess its compliance. We submit regular reports outlining the results of these tests to the appropriate local
authorities, including the Department of Environment (DOE).

  operational resilience and creates new opportunities for value creation through waste-to-value initiatives.

  OUR APPROACH

Advancing Waste Circularity

establishing a dedicated Circular Economy workstream within our sustainability governance. We are also
continuously improving our waste management approach, focusing on the entire lifecycle of materials to promote
greater circularity.

Our efforts aim to reduce overall waste generation, improve consumption patterns, encourage better production
methods, and enhance waste management practices, with a strong emphasis on recycling as part of our broader
circular economy framework, which establishes a clear waste management hierarchy: prevention, reduction, reuse,
recycling, and energy recovery.

repurposing, where applicable. These initiatives exemplifies our dedication to innovative practices that align with
our climate action objectives.

Furthermore, we’re expanding our scope to address challenges like electronic waste management, integrating it
into our scheduled waste processes.

                        DRIVING CONTINUAL IMPROVEMENT IN HEALTH AND SAFETY

                   WHY IS THIS IMPORTANT?

                   Workplace safety and employee wellbeing are fundamental to our operational excellence. As a global leader in
                   the food industry, we are committed to fostering safe and healthy work environments across all our facilities. Our
                   efforts beyond compliance, building a safe place to work directly impacts operational continuity, productivity,
                   and talent retention. By encouraging continuous development, we empower our team to perform at their best,
                   contributing meaningfully to the overall success of the organisation.

                     OUR APPROACH

                   We are committed to fostering a supportive working environment that promotes continuous improvements in
                   workplace safety practices by adhering to Safety and Health Acts and international industry standards.

                   Establishing a Culture of Workplace Safety

                   We prioritise workplace health and safety through a collaborative approach led by Health and Safety
                   Committees at each factory, comprising employee and management representatives. These committees
                   hold regular meetings to address safety concerns, analyse incidents, and recommend enhancements, with
                   employees encouraged to report issues directly with the committee or via a whistleblower channel.

                   To maintain good health and safety standards, we have designated Safety Officers at each subsidiary, who
                   serve as key points of contact for the Health and Safety Committee at the ground level. We conduct routine
                   hazard assessments and operability analyses to identify potential risks in our operations. Using a hierarchy of
                   controls, we work to minimise or eliminate these risks. In the case of work-related incidents, Safety Officers lead
                   investigations to identify the root causes and implement corrective actions. The effectiveness of these actions
                   is reviewed regularly by the committee, ensuring continuous improvement.

                   Staying Safe, Staying Informed: Monthly Safety Insights

                   To keep employees informed and updated on safety and health guidelines, we publish a monthly Safety Newsletter.
                   Delivered via email, it covers essential topics such as First Aid, Machine Guard Safety, and the proper use of Personal
                   Protective Equipment (PPE).

                   Ongoing Enhancement of Our Health and Safety Protocols

                   We focus on providing our employees with the necessary tools and knowledge to effectively manage health
                   and safety concerns. Safety training is compulsory for relevant employees during their GCB induction, and we
                   also offer ongoing training for Safety Officers and our emergency response teams to keep them aligned with the
                   latest regulatory and industry standards. For employees in high-risk roles, we offer specialised safety training as
                   part of their onboarding process, ensuring they are equipped with the required protective equipment.

                   Promoting Worker Health

                   As a global business, we prioritise compliance with regional health and safety regulations while going beyond these
                   requirements to protect our employees. All our entities offer access to various non-occupational medical services,
                   including dental and general practitioner care, along with hospitalisation support through insurance schemes.

                   We also fulfill mandatory regional obligations, such as social security contributions. Given our global presence, the
                   specific benefits may vary by region but are designed to meet similar health and welfare goals.

Corporate Governance Overview Statement

The Board of Directors of the Company (“the Board”) recognises the importance of good corporate governance
in ensuring that the interest of the Company, shareholders and other stakeholders are protected. The Board is
committed to the establishment and implementation of a proper framework for governance and controls that
are consistent with the principles recommended in the Malaysian Code on Corporate Governance (“MCCG”)
and other applicable laws, regulations, directives and guidelines.

This corporate governance overview statement (“Statement”) sets out the adoption and practices of the
principles as set out in the MCCG and the relevant chapters of the Main Market Listing Requirements (“Listing
Requirements”) of Bursa Malaysia Securities Berhad (“Bursa Securities”) on corporate governance.

The manner in and the extent in which the corporate governance framework is applied throughout the financial
year ended 31st December 2024 is summarised as follows:

PRINCIPLE A: BOARD LEADERSHIP AND EFFECTIVENESS

   I. Board Responsibilities

       Principal Responsibilities of the Board of Directors

       The Board directs the risk assessment, strategic planning, resource allocation, succession planning and
       financial and operational management of the Company and each of its subsidiaries (collectively referred
       to as “the Group” or “GCB Group”) to ensure that obligations to shareholders and other stakeholders
       are understood and met. The Board provides the leadership necessary to enable the Group’s business
       objectives to be met within the framework of internal controls described in this Statement.

       The Board, together with the management, takes responsibility for the governance of sustainability in the
       Group in accordance with Practices 4.1, 4.2, 4.3 and 4.4 in the MCCG. The Board oversees the Sustainability
       Steering Committee (“SSC”), which in turns governs the Sustainability Department (“SD”). The SD
       collaborates with the Committee and Working Group (“CWG”) in implementing sustainability strategies
       across the Group’s day-to-day operations. The Board takes into account sustainability considerations
       when developing and implementing company strategies, business plans, major plans of action and risk
       management. The Board ensures that it constantly stays abreast with sustainability issues relevant to
       the company and its business and that sustainability initiatives undertaken by the Group are effectively
       communicated to stakeholders. In preparing for the Sustainability Reporting, the SD performs materiality
       assessment every three (3) years, which is reviewed by the SSC and endorsed by the Board. Sustainability
       Reporting is reviewed by the SSC and reported to the Board.

       As set out in the MCCG’s Practice 1.2, the Board is led by a Chairman, who is responsible for:

     • Representing the Board to shareholders and ensuring effective communication with shareholders;
     • Ensuring effective operation of the Board and its committees in conformity with the highest standards

           of corporate governance;

     • Setting the agenda, style and tone of Board discussions to promote constructive debate and effective

           decision-making;

     • Ensuring that all Board committees are properly established, composed and operated;
     • Ensuring comprehensive induction programmes for new directors and updates for all directors as and

           when necessary;

     • Supporting the Chief Executive Officer (“CEO”) in the development of strategy and, more broadly, to

           support and advise the CEO; and

     • Establishing a harmonious and open relationship with the CEO.

       In compliance with Practice 1.4 in the MCCG, the Chairman of the Board is not a member of any board
       committees nor attended their meetings, ensuring his objectivity in oversight.

       The Board is supported by two (2) suitably qualified and competent Company Secretaries, who provide
       sound governance advice, ensure adherence to rules and procedures and advocate for the adoption of
       corporate governance best practices, in compliance to the MCCG’s Practice 1.5.

Paragraph 15.26(b) of the Main Market Listing Requirements requires the Board of Directors (the “Board”) of any
given Listed Issuer to include in its annual report a Statement on Risk Management and Internal Control. The
Board is pleased to provide the following statement that is prepared in accordance with the Statement on Risk
Management and Internal Control: Guidelines for Directors of Listed Issuers (the “Guidelines”) endorsed by Bursa
Malaysia Securities Berhad (“Bursa Securities”) which outlines the nature and scope of the risk management
and internal controls of the Group during the financial year under review until the date of approval.

BOARD’S RESPONSIBILITY                                                                                               CORPORATE GOVERNANCE

The Board is committed to the continuous improvement of internal controls and risk management practices
within the Group to meet its business objectives. The Board affirms its overall responsibility to maintain a sound
system of internal controls and effective risk management, and for reviewing the adequacy, integrity and
effectiveness of these systems to safeguard shareholders’ investment and the Group’s assets. It covers not only
financial controls but operational and compliance controls, and risk management.

However, such systems, by their nature, can only provide reasonable, but not absolute, assurance against
material misstatement, losses or fraud. These systems were designed to manage, rather than eliminate, the risk
of failure to achieve business objectives of the Group.

RISK MANAGEMENT FRAMEWORK

In dealing with its stewardship responsibilities, the Board recognises that an effective risk management is part
of good business management practice. The Board acknowledges that all areas of the Group’s activities involve
some degree of risk and is committed to ensuring that the Group has an effective risk management framework
which will allow the Group to be able to identify, evaluate, monitor and manage risks continuously that affect
the achievement of the Group’s business objectives.

This process is annually reviewed by the Board. It is intended that any key risk or significant control failings or
weaknesses shall be identified and discussed in these reports including the impact they have had or may have
on the Group and the actions to rectify them.
