As Malaysia’s economy reeled under the impact of the COVID-19 crisis, Dutch Lady
Milk Industries Berhad harnessing innovative solutions and the resilience of our people
to continue enhancing our operations, despite movement restrictions.

Looking back, amidst having to make changes to the way we carry out our day to day
operations, we are pleased to have achieved uninterrupted supply of our products to
shelves nationwide while going the extra mile to appreciate and serve frontline heroes,
