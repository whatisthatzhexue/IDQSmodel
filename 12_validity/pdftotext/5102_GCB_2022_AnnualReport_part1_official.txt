TODAY

The incorporation of GCB provided the impetus for our
ascension from a humble Malaysian manufacturer to the top
cocoa grinder in Asia and the world’s 4th largest manufacturer
of cocoa-derived food ingredients. We recognised the potential
we could unlock and set our sights on establishing our position
as a competitive multinational player in the global cocoa and
chocolate industry today.

From working with small groups of trading companies in the 1980s, we began
engaging with multinational chocolate manufacturers and expanded our sales
footprint to more than 80 countries. As the global demand for GCB products
increased, we were empowered to invest in product development, steadily
expanding our portfolio from minor commodity products to now producing a
wide range of cocoa-derived food ingredients, industrial chocolate and even
gourmet products.

Looking to the future, we will sustain our investment in the latest technologies,
new machineries, automation enhancements and a range of sustainability
initiatives to maintain and enhance our global competitiveness, while at the
same time reducing our carbon footprint and environmental impact. We will also
focus on nurturing our human capital by investing in our employees’ physical
and mental wellbeing in their working environment, while continuing to support
their personal and professional development and growth.

WHERE WE OPERATE

Our production facilities span four continents and collectively contribute to an annual bean grinding
capacity of 337,000 MT, annual value-added capacity of 99,000 MT and annual industrial chocolate
capacity of 95,000 MT across our various cocoa processing and chocolate production operations.

With our new UK production facility commencing operations during 2023 and additional machineries
also set to be installed at our SCHOKINAG plant in Germany next year, our annual industrial chocolate
capacity is set to rise by a further 21,000 MT.

Products Introduction

We offer a wide range of cocoa-derived ingredients and chocolates in a multitude of
varieties that cater to a range of applications across the cocoa industry, both within
food production as well as across popular consumer categories.

                         COCOA
                         MASS

                                                   Produced by roasting and grinding cocoa nibs from
                                                   solids into a more permeable and fluid form. Cocoa
                                                   mass is also known as cocoa paste or cocoa liquor,
                                                   and is complementary to the production of all
                                                   types of chocolates, especially dark chocolate, due
                                                   to its smooth texture and can be further processed
                                                   to create cocoa cake and cocoa butter for white
                                                   chocolate, milk chocolate or other applications.
GUAN CHONG BERHAD Integrated Annual Report 2022                                                                                 INTRODUCTION

                                                                         15  Products Introduction

COCOA
BUTTER

Cocoa butter is extracted from the pressing of
cocoa mass and is used as an intermediary product
in the production of chocolates, personal care
products, ointments and pharmaceutical products.

COCOA
CAKE

Derived via the cocoa mass pressing process,
cocoa cake consists of the solid portion of cocoa
nibs that are then broken into smaller pieces. It is
primarily used in the industrial grinding process
that creates cocoa powder.

COCOA
POWDER

Produced by grinding cocoa cake, cocoa powder
is used to manufacture chocolate confectioneries
and chocolate pastes, which are essential to the
concoction of desserts.
GUAN CHONG BERHAD Integrated Annual Report 2022                              INTRODUCTION

                                                                         16

Products Introduction

CHOCOLATE
BEVERAGES

Our chocolate beverages are produced via a
careful blend of various cocoa materials with
sugar, milk powder and other ingredients, and
may be mixed with water for consumption.

CHOCOLATE
CHIPS & CHUNKS

Chocolate chips and chunks are used in
the baking of cakes and biscuits and for the
decoration of desserts and other sweets. Our
chips are designed to be easy to dose, quick to
melt and well-tempered to enhance the melting
process.

LIQUID
CHOCOLATE

Produced by processing cocoa butter together
with cocoa mass, our liquid chocolate is rich in
flavonoids and antioxidants. Through continuous
refinement of the complex processes involved,
we have been able to achieve high consistency
in producing superior quality liquid chocolate.

BUILDING BUSINESS SUSTAINABILITY THROUGH ENVIRONMENT, SOCIAL & GOVERNANCE

"Caring Beyond Cocoa" is GCB’s corporate sustainability program that aims to achieve sustainable development in four
pillars, namely "Healthy Planet", "Happy Employees", "Thriving Communities", and "Sustainable Supply Chain".

Healthy Planet

The Group is committed to maintaining high standards of environmental responsibility in all
our operations, with focus on climate resilience and encouraging sustainable agricultural
production. We also enforce environmental compliance across all our subsidiaries. The Group
also closely monitors our performance on issues such as energy, emissions, waste and water,
while keeping a keen eye on the potential impact of climate change on our operations.

We also continued to make good progress in bringing our sustainability development ahead.
In FY2022, we installed solar panels at our facilities in Pasir Gudang, Malaysia. This goes a long
way in reducing carbon footprint for our Group. We are currently installing solar panels in our
new production facilities in UK and Côte d'Ivoire.

Apart from that, we are also installing biomass boilers at our production facility in Côte
d'Ivoire, which converts our internally generated cocoa shell waste to steam energy for heat
requirements of our processes. Our waste-to-energy generation currently accounts for up
to 105,547 GJ of the fuel we use for steam generation at our Malaysia production facilities,
significantly reducing our consumption of natural gas.

Happy Employees

The Group places great emphasis on nurturing a committed and motivated workforce and
ensuring a healthy and happy working culture across all our subsidiaries. We invest in the
personal and professional development of our employees and maintain high standards of
business conduct and integrity.

Overall, we aim to create corporate awareness by providing local employment opportunities
and fair wages in regions where we operate. Not only that, the Group is also committed to
empower our local talents by offering career training and development opportunities.

Thriving Communities

The Group advocates for ethical and conscientious business practices and supports
philanthropic projects and dedicated CSR activities that focus on enhancing the standard of
living and building knowledge-based communities. We also provide assistance during times of
need and safeguard the individuals and communities impacted by our suppliers.

The Group carried out activities such as supporting local community in times of need through
Mercy Malaysia Flood Respond & Recovery donation, as well as nurturing youths in community
through educational programs such as our support for the AIESEC Speak Up Project.

Sustainable Supply Chain

The Group is committed to ensuring that all our products are sourced and produced in a
responsible way that meets or surpasses the expectations of its customers and reflects the
environmental responsibility that we have set for ourselves. In our efforts in adopting ethical
cocoa trading principles, we have established our GC&B Program to show our commitment in
putting sustainability at the core of our business practices.

This program comprises four pillars of sustainability that focus on: -
1. Improving cocoa farmer income;
2. Benefitting farmers and their community;
3. Protecting environment; and
4. Providing responsible cocoa.

By addressing the issues underlying each pillar, we believe this will enable us to achieve our
final goal of delivering 100% traceable and sustainable cocoa ingredients in our direct supply
chain by 2030.

In 2022, the program benefitted 7,816 farmers reached by GAP training programs and
conducted training that promotes IGA to 1,230 farmers.

GCB is committed to practicing good corporate governance to ensure our business practices
are operating in an ethical and transparent manner, with a goal to creating long-term value for
our stakeholders, including customers, employees, shareholders, and the community.
