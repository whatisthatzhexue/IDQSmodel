INTRODUCTION  PRODUCT
              INTRODUCTION

              Our Diverse
                    Product Range

                COCOA MASS

                Cocoa mass, also known as cocoa paste or cocoa liquor, is produced
                through a meticulous process of roasting and grinding cocoa nibs
                to achieve a smooth, fluid state. This ingredient is crucial in crafting
                different types of chocolate, particularly dark chocolate.

                COCOA BUTTER

                Pressing cocoa mass creates cocoa butter, a key intermediary
                ingredient used in a variety of sectors, including chocolate production,
                personal care products, ointments, and pharmaceuticals.

                COCOA CAKE

                Derived from the pressing of cocoa mass, cocoa cake consists of
                solid fragments of cocoa nibs that are subsequently broken down
                into smaller pieces. It plays a vital role in industrial grinding and is
                essential for producing finely ground cocoa powder.

                                       INTRODUCTION

We offer an extensive range of cocoa-derived ingredients and chocolates, tailored for various applications
within the cocoa industry, encompassing both food production and popular consumer categories.

COCOA POWDER

Cocoa powder is made by grinding cocoa cakes and is a key
ingredient in chocolate confectioneries, as well as various dessert
recipes and pastes.

LIQUID CHOCOLATE

Our liquid chocolate, rich in flavonoids and antioxidants, is
produced by expertly combining cocoa butter and cocoa mass.
Through rigorous refinement, we achieve remarkable consistency in
delivering liquid chocolate of superior quality.

CHOCOLATE CHIPS & CHUNKS

Ideal for baking, our precisely crafted chocolate chips and chunks
simplify measurements, melt swiftly, and temper perfectly,
enhancing the chocolate melting process and elevating baking
experiences.

CHOCOLATE BEVERAGES

We create a variety of powdered chocolate beverages by carefully
blending cocoa components with sugar, milk powder, and other
ingredients. These beverages are easily mixed with water to provide
a rich and satisfying cocoa flavor.

       Competitive Landscape

Côte d’Ivoire Expansion
Establishing operations in the world’s largest cocoa bean-producing country positions us strategically for
a competitive edge. Our cocoa processing facility in Côte d’Ivoire, commissioned in September 2022 and
inaugurated in July 2023 by the country’s vice president, became fully operational in November 2022. It
completed its maiden export shipment in February 2023, marking a significant milestone for our business. With
a potential capacity of 240,000 MT, we have been proactively exploring ways to optimise capacity utilisation
and unlock its full potential since its commissioning.

Equipped with cutting-edge machinery and the latest technology, the facility allows us to source premium
cocoa beans directly from the origin, significantly reducing the beans-to-ingredients cycle. This strategic
expansion also enhances our supply chain resilience, providing greater control over production and shipping
schedules. By supplying cocoa-derived ingredients directly to our operations in Germany and the UK, we
anticipate improved cost efficiencies, strengthening our competitive position in these critical markets and
beyond.

Looking ahead, we are actively exploring further opportunities to expand the processing capacity at our Côte
d’Ivoire facility, positioning us to tap into additional strategic consumer markets.
