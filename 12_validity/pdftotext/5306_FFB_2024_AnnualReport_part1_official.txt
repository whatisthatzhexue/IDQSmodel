As the leading homegrown dairy company in Malaysia, we are committed to meeting the
evolving needs of our customers through ongoing innovation while staying true to our brand

                           promise of providing fresh, nourishing goodness for everyone, every day.

Our diverse product range includes various dairy products such as fresh milk, flavoured milk,
yoghurt and yoghurt drinks, and our new growing-up milk for children. We also offer plant-
based alternatives such as soy, almond and oat milk, and product extensions through Jom

                  Cha, which features bubble tea, soft-serve yoghurt and other milk tea products.

To enhance our offerings and increase value for stakeholders, we introduced several new
products during the last financial year, including Farm Fresh Grow in whole powder format,

           100% Australian organic milk, chocolate mint milk, riceberry milk and cooking cream.

Through large format retailers, mini marts, convenience stores and our unique home dealer
network – encompassing 58 stockists, 762 home dealers and 971 agents nationwide – we are
consistently expanding our reach to ensure product accessibility across the whole of Malaysia.
Our products are also currently sold in Singapore, Australia and soon, the Greater Manilla

                                                                                  market of neighbouring Philippines.

We extend our heartfelt gratitude to our customers, partners and stakeholders for their
unwavering support over the past year. Together, we will continue to grow, innovate and

                                                              bring the best of Farm Fresh to every household.

WHO WE ARE

 Our Journey of
 Growth & Innovation

      Since our founding in 2009, we have steadily expanded across the entire grass-
      to-glass value chain, becoming one of Malaysia’s most iconic homegrown brands
      and establishing a robust international presence.

      Our journey began 15 years ago when our visionary founder, Loi Tuan Ee, imported 60
      Holstein Jersey cows from Australia and set up a farm in Mawai, Johor. His mission was
      ambitious: to offer pure dairy products to the Malaysian market, free from preservatives
      and artificial additives, delivered fresh to supermarket shelves.

      This commitment to freshness and quality quickly became our unique selling point, setting
      us apart from imported dairy companies that relied on powdered or reconstituted milk.
      With enthusiastic customer response and support from Khazanah Nasional
      Berhad, Malaysia’s sovereign wealth fund, we were propelled on a
      transformative path, expanding our production capacity and diversifying
      our product range.

      A significant breakthrough came with the creation of our innovative
      home dealer network. This initiative allowed micro-entrepreneurs in
      urban and rural Malaysia to become stockists, dealers and agents
      of our products, providing income opportunities to local
      communities while enabling us to reach previously untapped
      markets.

      Today, we are a fully integrated dairy producer, operating seven
      dairy farms and four processing facilities across Malaysia and
      Australia. Our diverse brand portfolio includes Farm Fresh, Yarra
      Farm, Yarra by Farm Fresh, Master Barista, Henry Jones, Nubian
      Goat’s Milk, Jom Cha by Farm Fresh, St David Dairy (Australia) and
      our most recent acquisitions, Inside Scoop and Sin Wah Ice Cream.

      8 Farm Fresh Berhad
Under these brands, we offer a comprehensive range of 192 SKUs to a broad customer base across Southeast Asia and
Australia. Our robust market presence extends across various product segments, from ready-to-drink (RTD) chilled and
ambient products to yoghurts, plant-based alternatives, sauces and ice cream. Our growth has been fuelled by strategic
investments in research and development (R&D), enabling us to introduce innovative products such as organic milk,
lactose-free milk and almond milk, as well as Farm Fresh Grow for children, to meet the evolving needs of consumers.
During FY2024, we introduced various new products, including a powder variant of Farm Fresh Grow, Australian organic
milk, chocolate mint milk, riceberry milk and cooking cream. Looking ahead, we are excited to launch consumer packaged
goods (CPG) ice cream, chocolate malt beverage, plant-based yoghurt and butter products over the next twelve months.
Having ascended to the position of Malaysia’s largest integrated producer of dairy products, we are poised to further
expand our presence within the Asia Pacific region. Our latest factory in the Philippines is set to commence operations
in the third quarter 2024, enabling us to provide chilled and UHT dairy products, as well as growing up milk powder, to
the Greater Manila market.
As we continue to elevate our production capacity and diversify our portfolio with innovative dairy and plant-based
offerings, we remain deeply committed to upholding the brand promise cemented at our founding – to prioritise
sustainability, honesty and the well-being of consumers in everything we do.

                         OUR PROMISE (VISION)

      We achieve our brand promise of delivering nutritious dairy and plant-
      based products by:
      • Diversifying our product portfolio to capture a wide, addressable

         market with different functional and nutritional needs, supported by a
         culture of innovation whilst being honest and meticulous with our
         ingredients based on the functional and nutritional needs of customers.
      • Being ethical and truthful in our labelling and marketing practices to
         foster strong brand trust.
      • Practising certified-humane farming practices at our dairy farms and
         achieving high-yielding tropical dairy farming with our Australian
         Friesian Sahiwal (AFS) breed, including via continuous breed improvement
         utilising in vitro fertilisation (IVF).
      • Enhancing our environmental stewardship by implementing
         regenerative agriculture processes that treat dairy manure for reuse
         in our farm operations.
      • Championing socially inclusive efforts such as the home dealer
         network and creating rural employment.
