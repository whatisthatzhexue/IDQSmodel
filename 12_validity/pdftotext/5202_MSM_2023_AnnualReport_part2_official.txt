OVERVIEW

The global recovery continues to be sluggish and inconsistent. Several factors are hampering the
recovery, including the long-term effects of the pandemic, the ongoing war in Ukraine, increasing
geoeconomic division, the impact of tighter monetary policies aimed at reducing inflation,
the withdrawal of fiscal support amidst high debt levels, and severe weather events.

Global prices for raw and refined sugar have soared, influenced by global shortages, increasing energy prices, and the El Nino weather phenomenon.
These factors combined to create a challenging business climate in FY2023/FY2024, both domestically and internationally.
