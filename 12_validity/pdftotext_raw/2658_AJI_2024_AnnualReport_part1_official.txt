History & Milestones
Ajinomoto (Malaysia) Berhad (“the Company”), established in 1961, began its journey in Malaysia as a distributor of
monosodium glutamate (“MSG”) under the renowned AJI-NO-MOTO® brand. By 1965, the Company’s business rapid
growth led to the commencement of local production of MSG under the AJI-NO-MOTO® brand at its then Kuala Lumpur
factory. The Company then implemented its strategy to make available a range of AJI-NO-MOTO® Umami Seasoning
and food products to meet the varied needs of consumers, viz households, restaurants and other food service providers,
and food manufacturers. These key developments firmly established the Company’s reputation as a trusted food
manufacturer and distributor in Malaysia.
The Company achieved a new milestone with the commissioning of its state-of-the-art plant in Bandar Enstek in 2022,
signifying a paramount commitment to quality and innovation. The new smart and eco-friendly plant marks a significant
step in its thrust into global markets for food seasoning products.

The Company is steadfast in its vision of becoming a global customer-centric halal food company operating under
food manufacturer compliance standards and adhering to all relevant international and local laws and regulations.
It is committed to enhance people's well-being through its Specialties and Innovative Solutions, in order to uphold the
Ajinomoto Group's corporate slogan, "Eat Well, Live Well". Being a responsible corporate citizen, the Company is actively
implementing strategies to meet Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) requirements and standards in its business
plans and policies. As a responsible employer, policies are regularly reviewed and shared with employees to uphold its
ongoing commitment to be an employer of choice.
In March 2024, the Company achieved FSSC22000 certification in respect of its compliance to manufacturing processes
and food safety management systems, including hazard analysis, risk assessment, and stringent operational controls
across its entire production and supply chain. This significant accomplishment reflects its commitment to the highest
standards of food safety and quality in operations.
Our Product Range
Consumer Products
The Company continued its dedication to develop products that cater to a diverse range of consumer needs. Its extensive
range of seasoning and food products, including chicken stock, menu seasoning, pepper, sweetener, and jelly drink with
amino acids, showcases its commitment to offer a variety of quality food products.
In 2023, the Company introduced two new variants of Seri Aji seasoning: Seri-Aji® Curry Fried Rice and Chicken Soup
Seasoning Powder, offering delicious, simple and convenient cooking solutions for household meals. Additionally, the
Company expanded its product line with “Ajinomoto” Chicken Gyoza, bringing the taste of authentic Japanese cuisine
to consumer as well as food service markets. These new additions highlight the Company’s commitment to deliver
products meeting diverse consumers’ needs.
The Company’s range of products are distributed across Malaysia through direct sales forces and distributors, retailers,
wholesalers and e-commerce channels and agents in overseas markets. Furthermore, the Company’s marketing efforts
include social media and digital platforms, effectively reaching consumers of food seasoning and food products. Using
such platforms facilitates continuous improvement in interactions with the market and consumers to better understand
and respond to their needs.

Industrial and Food Service Products
In addition to its consumer product line, the Company also offers a comprehensive range of industrial products for the
food manufacturing and food service industries. The product portfolio spans diverse categories, including taste and
flavour-enhancing products, binders, and texture improvers. They are marketed under the brand names AJIMATE®, AJI-
AROMA® (referred to as “TENCHO” product category) and ACTIVA®, and are widely utilized in various processed foods,
such as instant noodles, soups, snacks, sauces, dairy products, processed meats, and processed seafood.
With a robust global presence, the Company serves a diverse clientele across Asia, Central and South America, Middle
East, Oceania and Africa. Leveraging multiple distribution and export channels, the Company efficiently distributes and
exports its products, meeting the needs of food service and industrial clients both locally and internationally.
As the Company continues to address the growing demand for simple-to-use seasoning and texture solutions, the
Company in 2023 introduced AJIRISE™ - a series of yeast extract products which help to enhance original flavours and
also adds a savoury touch. Alongside that, it also launched two additional products: AJIezi™ Rice Fresher, employing
advanced texture control to extend steamed rice freshness; and AJIezi™ Meat Juicy Up, improving meat juiciness while
maintaining texture integrity for consistently delicious results.

Operating Environment
The Malaysian economy grew moderately in 2023 amid a challenging external environment due to weak exports,
geopolitical tensions and tighter monetary policies. Bank Negara projected our economic growth to improve in year 2024
mainly driven by continuous domestic demand expansion and improvement in external demand. There are downside risks
to growth arising from weaker-than-expected external demand, escalation of geopolitical conflicts and lower production
of commodity due to adverse weather conditions which could impact the cost of our raw materials and energy.
Financial Review
In the financial year ended 31 March 2024 (“FYE 31.03.2024”), the Company achieved a revenue of RM636.4 million,
increased by 5.4% as compared to the revenue of previous financial year of RM603.7 million. Sales volume in the current
financial year decreased mainly from AJI-NO-MOTO® against the sales volume in the previous financial year. However,
this reduction was compensated by the increase in prices of AJI-NO-MOTO® AND "TENCHO". Both better revenue and
lower key material cost of AJI-NO-MOTO® contributed to a strong profit from our business operations. In addition, the gain
on disposal of assets held for sale including the lands and property, plant and equipment located in Jalan Kuchai Lama,
Kuala Lumpur of RM394.2 million increased the Company’s profit before tax to RM452.6 million.
SEGMENT INFORMATION
Consumer Business
Revenue in Consumer Business was RM484.2 million as compared to the revenue of RM462.1 million in the previous
financial year. Consumer business recorded lower sales volume from AJI-NO-MOTO® mainly in the export market. The
decrease in the AJI-NO-MOTO® export sales was due to the demand fluctuation arising from the geopolitical issue. The
sales volume of TUMIX®, AJI-SHIO®, aminoVITAL® and "Ajinomoto" Chicken Gyoza had shown positive expansion. Despite
lower sales volume, the revenue in the Consumer Business improved due to better selling price of AJI-NO-MOTO® and
foreign exchange gain from export sales. Profit was 43.9 million, 13.3 times of the profit of RM3.3 million in the previous
financial year. The main contributors were higher revenue, lower cost of key material AJI-NO-MOTO® and staff costs.
Industrial Business
Revenue from Industrial Business increased by 7.4% at RM152.2 million as compared to the previous financial year’s
revenue of RM141.7 million. The rise in revenue was attributed to higher selling price of "TENCHO" and foreign exchange
gain from export sales with the sustained sales volume. However, the increase in revenue was eroded by higher costs of
raw materials and factory overheads including depreciation. As a result, profit was RM15.5 million against the previous
financial year’s profit of RM15.1 million.

FINANCIAL CONDITION
As at FYE 31.03.2024, there was a spike in Total Assets and Total Net Assets as compared to the previous financial year
due to higher operating profit and gain on disposal of assets held for sale. Net cash generated in operating activities
for the current financial year was RM66.4 million while the net cash used in operating activities in the previous financial
year was RM10.4 million due to the increase in net profit from our business operations. Net cash generated from investing
activities was RM69.5 million during the current financial year due to lower payment of capital expenditure incurred
in manufacturing facilities and proceeds received from the disposal of assets held for sale. Net cash used in financing
activities was RM70.5 million for the current financial year after the repayment of borrowing under Islamic financing and
advance from ultimate holding company as well as dividend payment to shareholders.
DIVIDEND
The Board of Directors continue to maintain the policy of stable and sustainable dividend payout to shareholders. The
Directors, as announced by the Company on 27 June 2024, declared a first and final single-tier dividend of 38.40 sen per
ordinary share in respect of FYE 31.03.2024 to be paid on 25 September 2024.
In addition to the above, the Board of Directors, as announced on 2 May 2024, declared and paid a special one-off
single-tier dividend in respect of the financial year ending 31 March 2025 of RM2.12 per ordinary share, amounting to
RM128,892,892 on 30 May 2024 subsequent to the completion of the disposal of assets held for sale.
OPERATIONAL RISKS
(1) Operating Environment Risks
Exchange Rate Fluctuations
The Company’s exports and imports of raw materials are denominated in USD. Therefore, the Company is exposed
to currency fluctuation risk, especially between the USD and the Malaysian Ringgit and this has impacted the
Company’s financial results.
Unforeseen Adverse Economic or Political Factors
The Company conducts business globally, and various potential economic, political and legal impediments overseas
such as political instability may have an adverse impact on business results.
Price Fluctuations for Raw Materials and Fuel
The prices of certain raw materials and energy resources used by the Company fluctuates according to market
conditions. These may result in higher manufacturing costs which will impact the Company’s business results.
(2) Risks Related to Business Activities
Food Safety Matters
The Company takes extensive care and effort to manage factors that impact on food safety, which is one of the
very important foundation upon which the Company’s business is built. However, in the event unforeseen issues
affecting food safety arise, which are beyond anyone’s control, such unforeseen events may have an adverse
impact on the Company’s business results.
Laws and Regulations, Litigation, etc.
As the Company conducts business on a global basis, it undertakes to comply with the laws, rules and regulations of
Malaysia and the countries that the Company exports its products to and/or purchases its raw materials from. The
possibility exists, however, that legal changes may be introduced and complying with such changes may restrict the
Company’s operation which may impact on business results.

OUTLOOK
Throughout the year, amidst ongoing challenges in the global economy, including dynamic market conditions,
inflationary pressures, and slower economic growth due to the Ukraine and other geopolitical conflicts, the Company
stays committed and confident by focusing on innovation and excellent service to sustain growth momentum.
Notable achievements have been made in key areas such as revenue growth, market expansion, product innovation.
These successes were made possible by the dedication and hard work of the Company’s employees, as well as strategic
decision-making during evolving market dynamics.
In the consumer sales segment, the Company foresee sustained growth driven by increasing demand and a growing
interest in healthy eating. To reinforce its corporate message of "Eat Well, Live Well," it introduced initiatives such as the
Smart Salt logo and continued sponsorship of Nutrition Month Malaysia. Throughout 2023, the Company focused on
enhancing wellness and brand awareness through multifaceted initiatives and activities aimed at reaching a broader
audience. These included the launch of the Smart Cooking Website and the Hi Ajinomoto MY page on TikTok, along
with the introduction of the Seri-Aji® Sahur campaign to simplify meal preparation for breaking fast during Ramadhan.
Other efforts included the AJI-NO-MOTO® Smart Salt Campaign for consumers and participation in scientific exhibitions
to raise awareness about the concept of delicious salt reduction. Additionally, the Company collaborated with AIESEC
JB’s “Clean Our Plate” campaign to advocate for sustainable consumption habits.
In addition to its commitment to promoting health and active living through aminoVITAL® events, the Company also
launched its Sports Nutrition Awareness Project at Sabah Malaysian Sports School (SSMS). This initiative aims to continuously
promote the health and well-being of student athletes through sports nutrition education programs and balanced set
meal serving while supporting Malaysian sportsmanship.
The Company remains steadfast in its commitment to expanding its industrial products to the export market and diversifying
its product portfolio. Efforts to strengthen networks with overseas affiliates will bolster the global presence of “TENCHO”
products. A recent milestone in this commitment is the launch of the Food Service Business Department, which aligns with
the objective of better and efficiently serving food service customers. The Company is well-positioned to meet evolving
trends such as the growing emphasis on alternative raw materials, plant-based proteins, and the "CLEAN LABEL" concept.
This strategic direction is further supported by the successful relocation and full operation at the new plant in Bandar
Enstek, highlighting the Company's dedication to operational excellence and efficiency.
Throughout FYE 31.03.2024, the Company demonstrated its commitment towards social responsibility and community
welfare through various initiatives. These efforts focused on promoting healthy living, empowering communities, and
addressing societal challenges, reflecting the Company's dedication to making a positive difference in the lives of those
it serves. The Company partnered with the Malaysian Recycling Alliance (MAREA) to advance sustainable practices in
Malaysia, emphasizing environmental stewardship and its commitment to a sustainable future. Additionally, collaborations
with Leave a Nest Malaysia to sponsor the Science Castle Grant 2024 underscored the Company's commitment to
nurturing scientific curiosity and empowering young minds. These initiatives align with the Company's ESG initiatives,
reinforcing its dedication to driving positive environmental, social, and governance outcomes.
With the adoption of the Ajinomoto Group’s Creating Shared Value (ASV) framework, which guides its efforts to create
positive corporate culture impacts while empowering its employees to take on challenges and fulfil the Company’s
overall goals, the Company is dedicated to continually creating both social and economic value. As the Company’s
work towards its 2030 roadmap, these initiatives remain crucial for sustaining values creation, contributing to growth of a
more sustainable future for all stakeholders and shareholders.

DIRECTOR’S PROFILE
Tan Sri Dato' (Dr.) Teo was appointed to the Board of Ajinomoto (Malaysia) Berhad on 28 June 2001 as an Independent Non-
Executive Director. On 4 June 2020, he was appointed as the Chairman of Ajinomoto (Malaysia) Berhad. At the same time,
Tan Sri Teo was re-designated as a member of the Audit Committee from his previous position as the Chairman, and as the

FOR THE FINANCIAL YEAR ENDED 31 MARCH 2024
INTRODUCTION
The Board of Directors (“the Board”) of Ajinomoto (Malaysia) Berhad is pleased to present its Statement on Risk
Management and Internal Control for the financial year ended 31 March 2024, which has been prepared pursuant to
paragraph 15.26(b) of Bursa Malaysia Securities Berhad (“Bursa Securities”) Main Market Listing Requirements (“Main LR”)
and in accordance with the principles and recommendations relating to risk management and internal controls provided
in the Malaysian Code on Corporate Governance and as guided by the Statement on Risk Management and Internal
Control: Guidelines for Directors of Listed Issuers. The statement below outlines the nature and scope of risk management
and internal control of the Company during the financial year under review.
BOARD RESPONSIBILITY
The Board acknowledges its responsibility and re-affirms its commitment to maintaining a sound system of internal control
to safeguard shareholders’ investments and the Company’s assets as well as reviewing the adequacy and integrity of
the system of internal control. The responsibility of reviewing the adequacy and integrity of the Company’s system of
internal control is delegated to the Audit Committee, which is empowered by its terms of reference to seek assurance on
the adequacy and integrity of the internal control system through independent reviews carried out by the internal audit
function.
However, as there are inherent limitations in any system of internal control, such a system put into effect by Management
can only reduce but cannot eliminate all risks that may impede the achievement of the Company’s business objectives.
Therefore, the internal control system can only provide reasonable and not absolute assurance against material
misstatement or loss.
KEY FEATURES OF THE COMPANY’S INTERNAL CONTROL SYSTEM
1. CONTROL ENVIRONMENT
• Organisation Structure and Authorisation Procedures
The Company maintains a formal organisation structure with a well-defined delegation of responsibilities and
accountability within the Company’s Senior Management. It sets out the roles and responsibilities, appropriate
authority limits, and review and approval procedures in order to enhance the internal control system of the
Company’s various operations.
• Periodic and Annual Budget
The Company has a comprehensive budgeting and forecasting system. The annual business plan and budget
are approved by the Board and the Holding Company. Budgetary control is in place for every operation of the
Company, where actual performance is closely monitored against budgets to identify and address significant
variances.
• Company’s Policies and Procedures
The Company has documented policies and procedures that are reviewed and updated to ensure that
it maintains its effectiveness and continues to support the Company’s business activities at all times as the
Company continues to grow.
• Human Resource Policy
Comprehensive guidelines on employment, performance appraisal, training and retention of employees are
in place to ensure that employees of the Company are well-trained and equipped with all the necessary
knowledge, skills and abilities to carry out their responsibilities effectively.
• Quality of Product
Quality of product is of prime importance to the Board. Compliance with procedures outlined in ISO9001:2015
and “Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Point” (HACCP) accreditation to underpin quality assurance and
control are strictly adhered to via regular internal and external quality audits.

2. RISK MANAGEMENT FRAMEWORK
The Board acknowledges that the Company’s business activities involve some degree of risks and key management
staff and Heads of Department are responsible for managing identified risks within defined parameters and standards.
The Risk Management Committee which adopts the risk framework from the parent company’s Risk Management
Guideline System is chaired by the Managing Director/Chief Executive Officer and includes other key management
staff of the Company. Identifying, evaluating and managing the significant risks faced by the Company is an ongoing
process which is undertaken at each department. During the year under review, this process was carried out through
periodic management meetings held to communicate and deliberate key issues and risks amongst Management
team members and where appropriate, controls were devised and implemented.
The abovementioned practices/initiatives by the Management serve as the ongoing process used to identify, assess
and manage key business, operation and financial risks faced by the Company.
Significant risks identified are escalated to the Board for their attention by the Managing Director/Chief Executive
Officer of the Company. The Board views the key risks that will have a significant impact on the Company’s results are
delays in obtaining authority approvals, fluctuations in material prices and compliance-related issues with legal and
regulatory requirements. Some of the other significant risks that were brought to the attention of the Board during
the financial year include Supply Chain Management challenges, key machinery and facilities breakdown, new
information technology system failures, quality control issues, recruitment and retention of talents and information
security risks. Action plans were being developed and taken to manage and monitor the identified risks.
Following the introduction of the anti-corruption acts and integration of corporate liability provisions into existing
legislation, the Management has taken initiatives not only to ensure compliance but to also inhibit corrupt practices
or actions stemming from within the Company. Control measures implemented included systematic reviewing and
enforcement of the Company’s policies, communication of policies through town hall briefings and the provision of
training and workshops to the Company’s staff.
3. INTERNAL AUDIT FUNCTION
The Company’s internal audit function, which is outsourced to a professional service firm, assists the Board and
the Audit Committee in providing independent assessment of the adequacy, efficiency and effectiveness of the
Company’s internal control system. The Internal Auditors report directly to the Audit Committee and the internal
audit plan is tabled to the Audit Committee for review and approval to ensure adequate coverage.
On a quarterly basis, the results of the internal audit reviews and the recommendations for improvement are
presented to the Audit Committee. In addition, the status of the implementation of corrective actions to address
control weaknesses is also followed up by the Internal Auditors to ensure that these actions have been satisfactorily
implemented. Senior Management will continue to ensure that appropriate actions are taken to enhance and
strengthen the internal control environment.
Based on the internal audit reviews carried out, none of the weaknesses noted have resulted in any material losses,
contingencies or uncertainties that would require separate disclosure in this annual report.
