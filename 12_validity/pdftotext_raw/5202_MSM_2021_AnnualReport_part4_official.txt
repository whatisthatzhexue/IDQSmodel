businesses, risk management, succession planning of Senior

information system are adequate and effective.

Board of Directors

shall not be less than two (2) and not more than twelve (12).

Paragraph 15.02 of the Listing Requirements. As at the date
of this statement, there are nine (9) members on the Board,
comprising:
• Six (6) Independent Non-Executive Directors; and
• Three (3) Non-Independent Non-Executive Directors.

Nomination and Remuneration Committee (NRC) to ensure
effective balance diversification of gender, skill-sets, experience

on pages 186 to 194 of this Annual Integrated Report.

transparent process, premised on meritocracy and taking into
account objective criteria such as competencies, experience,
character, integrity, diversity and the ability to devote time as
needed.
