RiskManagement
Risk management is an important aspect of the Group’s sustainability strategy, which helps us to identify, assess
and mitigate potential challenges relating to the EESG factors. The Board has the overall responsibility for the risk
management process within the Group to ensure that the Group identifies all of its risks, recorded in the risk register
and adequately mitigate action to be taken by the risk owner to achieve a proper balance between risks incurred
and potential returns to the shareholder.
The RMC is established to review and oversee the Group’s risk management framework and policies, and processes,
which include identifying, managing, monitoring, treating and mitigating significant risks of the Group including
looking at those risks that have an impact on the EESG matters and recommend them for approval by the Board.
The RMC is assisted by the risk management team, comprising members of the Management, nominated by the
Group CEO. With guidance from the RMC and Management, the Board is kept abreast with the material EESG-
related risks and opportunities disclosed in this Statement.
Further information on the Group’s risk management is outlined in the Statement on Risk Management and Internal

ClimateChangeandEmissionsManagement
The Group is committed to enhance its operational efficiencies to mitigate environmental impact, including pollution
and GHG emissions. Acknowledging the necessity of transitioning to a low-carbon economy, we actively assess the
adverse impacts of climate change that could disrupt production and supply chains. We aim to eventually extend
our efforts beyond our emission reductions within our operations to include mitigating the impacts of climate
change on surrounding communities.
For this Statement, the Group has collated fuel consumption data from its transportation vehicles, which will enable
Wellspire to report on its direct Scope 1 GHG emissions for the first time. Scope 2 GHG emissions are mainly
contributed from the consumption of purchased electricity from the warehouse facility, the offices in Thailand and
the head office in Malaysia. We will continue to review other activities in our operations across the value chain to
assess the impacts of GHG emissions.
To enhance the Group’s environmental transparency, the Group began tracking 2 key Scope 3 GHG emissions
categories in FYE 2024, specifically business travel by air and employee commute across the Group’s operational
regions. We conducted an employee survey to collect data on travel modes and distances. Consequently, the
Group’s overall GHG emissions have shown an increase compared to the previous year’s disclosure, reflecting
this initial inclusion of the Scope 3 GHG emissions data. Notably in Thailand, a significant portion of our workforce
relies on motorcycles for their daily commute, which contributes substantially to the reported employee commuting
emissions.
The Group will continue to assess other categories under Scope 3 GHG emissions that may be material for the
Group, to enable the Group to further refine the extent of the GHG emissions disclosures.
