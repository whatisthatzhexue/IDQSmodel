CORPORATE PHILOSOPHY
INTRODUCING MAGMA
Magma Group Berhad builds on the distinguished legacy of Impiana Hotels Berhad, driving a new era of
transformation, growth, and diversification. Incorporated as Bio Osmo Sdn Bhd in 2006 and listed on the Main Market
in 2007, we entered the hospitality sector in 2019, establishing a leading presence in Malaysia’s regional market. Our
strategic rebranding in December 2023 was more than a name change; it symbolised our evolution into a forward-
thinking conglomerate, expanding beyond hospitality to pioneer sustainable success across diverse sectors while
remaining rooted in our core strengths.
OUR BELIEFS
At Magma Group, we believe that true success stems from partnership. By uniting with clients, communities, and
industry allies, we harness collective strengths to deliver exceptional experiences, drive growth, and elevate hospitality
standards.
OUR VISION
We aspire to lead the hospitality industry into a new era defined by innovative, sustainable, and inspiring experiences.
Magma Group aims to set benchmarks through bold initiatives and strategic alliances, proactively adapting to
tomorrow’s opportunities.
OUR MISSION
We are dedicated to creating long-term value through every endeavour, delivering world-class experiences rooted in
collaboration, integrity, and excellence. As we grow, we remain committed to reshaping the industry while ensuring
tangible benefits for our customers, partners, and communities.

(Overview of Group Business)
Magma Group Berhad (“.BHNB” or “the Group”) is actively evolving from a traditional hospitality company into an
innovative, experience-focused property and lifestyle group. Anchored in Malaysia’s hotel industry, Magma manages
a portfolio of distinctive assets under the WOLO and Impiana brands, delivering culturally immersive hospitality across
strategic urban and regional locations.
As of the financial year ended 31 December 2024 (“FYE2024”), the Group’s portfolio includes WOLO Kuala Lumpur,
Impiana Hotel Ipoh, Impiana Hotel Senai, and an associate stake in Impiana KLCC Hotel. The full consolidation of
WOLO Kuala Lumpur under Magma Bintang Sdn Bhd has notably enhanced the Group’s hospitality performance,
fueling robust segmental growth.
Magma’s strategic focus on high-impact locations reflects its commitment to delivering exceptional guest experiences.
Through innovative offerings and targeted market positioning, the Group consistently sets industry standards for
service, quality, and operational excellence.
The Group’s foray into real estate leverages its hospitality strengths to deliver elevated living experiences and long-
term value. As a cornerstone of its growth strategy, property development is set to play a significant role in Magma’s
future trajectory.
Looking ahead to 2025, Magma is well-positioned to seize emerging opportunities in the dynamic hospitality
and tourism sectors. With a strong asset base, proven operational excellence, and an unwavering focus on guest
satisfaction, the Group is poised to lead with innovation and sustainable growth.

Assets
Total assets increased modestly by 1.5%, from RM245.21 million to RM248.76 million. This was mainly due to a 9.5%

ENERGY MANAGEMENT
ELECTRICITY CONSUMPTION
Energy management is a cornerstone of Magma Group’s sustainability initiatives, reflecting our commitment to
environmental stewardship. Our electricity needs are primarily sourced from the Tenaga Nasional Berhad (TNB) grid,
with consumption driven by the operational demands of our hotels and corporate offices. By integrating energy-efficient
practices, we aim to reduce our environmental footprint and support Malaysia’s shift toward sustainable energy.
In FY2024, Magma Group recorded electricity consumption of 5,135.9 Megawatt-hours (MWh), representing a 56.78%
increase from FY2023. This rise is attributed to the inclusion of WOLO Kuala Lumpur hotel in our portfolio, coupled
with its high occupancy rates and operational scale. Additionally, the recovery of the hospitality sector, driven by
economic improvement and increased guest demand, contributed to this trend. These factors underscore the Group’s
growth trajectory while highlighting the need for robust energy management strategies.

The Board of Directors (“the Board”) of the Company recognizes the importance of upholding high corporate

This Corporate Governance Overview Statement (“CG Statement”) provides a summary of the corporate governance
practices adopted by the Company throughout the financial year ended 31 December 2024 (“FYE 2024”). The CG
Statement has been prepared in compliance with the Malaysian Code on Corporate Governance (“MCCG”) and the
Main Market Listing Requirements (“Listing Requirements”) of Bursa Malaysia Securities Berhad (“Bursa Securities”).
It should be read in conjunction with the Company’s Corporate Governance Report for FYE 2024 (“CG Report”),

The Board regards risk management as an integral part of the Group’s business operations and has oversight over
this critical area through ARC. ARC, supported by the Internal Auditor, provides an independent assessment of the
effectiveness of the Group’s ERM framework and reports to the Board. The Group’s ERM is consistent with the
ERM Framework and involves systematically identifying, analysing, measuring, monitoring and reporting on the risks
that may affect the achievement of its business objectives. This ERM framework helps to reduce the uncertainties
surrounding the Group’s internal and external environment.
The ERM process is based on the following principles:
z Consider and manage risks enterprise-wide;
z Integrate risk management into business activities;
z Manage risk in accordance with the Risk Management framework;
z Tailor responses to business circumstances; and
z Communicate risks and responses to the Management.
All identified risks are displayed on a risk matrix based on their risk ranking to assist the Management in prioritising
their efforts and appropriately managing the different classes of risks. There is no dedicated ERM department,
however, the Management work closely with the respective operational managers to continuously strengthen the
risk management initiatives within the Group so that the Management could respond timely and effectively to the
constantly changing business environment and is thus able to protect and enhance shareholders value.
The Board recognises the importance of effective ERM in enhancing shareholders value while upholding a high
standard of corporate governance. Combining a strong and sustained commitment from the Board and Senior
Management with a clear direction and oversight from all levels of leadership, the Group embraces a holistic risk
management approach to achieve its business targets with minimal surprises.
