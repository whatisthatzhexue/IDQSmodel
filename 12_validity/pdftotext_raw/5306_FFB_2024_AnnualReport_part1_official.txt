As the leading homegrown dairy company in Malaysia, we are committed to meeting the
evolving needs of our customers through ongoing innovation while staying true to our brand
promise of providing fresh, nourishing goodness for everyone, every day.
Our diverse product range includes various dairy products such as fresh milk, flavoured milk,
yoghurt and yoghurt drinks, and our new growing-up milk for children. We also offer plant-
based alternatives such as soy, almond and oat milk, and product extensions through Jom
Cha, which features bubble tea, soft-serve yoghurt and other milk tea products.
To enhance our offerings and increase value for stakeholders, we introduced several new
products during the last financial year, including Farm Fresh Grow in whole powder format,
100% Australian organic milk, chocolate mint milk, riceberry milk and cooking cream.
Through large format retailers, mini marts, convenience stores and our unique home dealer
network – encompassing 58 stockists, 762 home dealers and 971 agents nationwide – we are
consistently expanding our reach to ensure product accessibility across the whole of Malaysia.
Our products are also currently sold in Singapore, Australia and soon, the Greater Manilla
market of neighbouring Philippines.
We extend our heartfelt gratitude to our customers, partners and stakeholders for their
unwavering support over the past year. Together, we will continue to grow, innovate and
bring the best of Farm Fresh to every household.

As we uphold our brand promise of delivering nutritious
dairy and plant-based products, we remain focused on
bolstering our regenerative agriculture practices. Since
embarking on this journey in 2014, our efforts in
responsibly treating and efficiently recycling solid and
liquid animal waste have yielded valuable resources for
animal bedding, crop irrigation and soil fertilisation.
These practices not only benefit the environment but
also enhance the welfare of our dairy herd, resulting in
healthier and more nutritious products.
We have also made substantial progress in transitioning
to renewable energy sources, in line with our role as
leaders in climate action. Successfully implementing
photovoltaic (PV) technology at three farms – Muadzam
Shah, Larkin and Taiping – has enabled us to harness
the vast potential of solar power in tropical climates to
generate clean energy. Additionally, the commencement
of our first biogas plant in Muadzam Shah this year is
anticipated to significantly reduce our greenhouse gas
(GHG) emissions and diesel consumption.
To ensure a concerted and conscientious approach to
quantifying and mitigating our GHG emissions, we
regularly conduct comprehensive carbon inventory
exercises. Our latest assessment in FY2024 reaffirmed
that our farms maintain significantly lower carbon
emissions intensity compared to regional peers, a
distinction we have held since 2019. Building on this, we
have maintained our reduction in carbon emissions
intensity per litre of milk compared to previous year
(excluding new businesses from The Inside Scoop, Sin
Wah and Jom Cha).
Concurrently, we drive sustainable socioeconomic
progress in our surrounding local communities. Through
our “local first” hiring policy, we prioritise the hiring of
talent from rural and underserved areas, alongside local
sourcing, to uplift local economies where our business
operates. We also support government programmes
aimed at providing nutritional dairy products for the
younger generation, while undertaking educational
outreach programmes to broaden the career prospects
of marginalised youth. Meanwhile, our innovative home
dealer network, established in 2016, empowers local
micro-entrepreneurs to earn a decent income as
stockists, agents or dealers of our products.
For further details on our sustainability approach and
the outcomes we have achieved so far, refer to the “Our
Sustainability Journey” section of this report from pages
122 to 179.

Our Journey of
Growth & Innovation
Since our founding in 2009, we have steadily expanded across the entire grass-
to-glass value chain, becoming one of Malaysia’s most iconic homegrown brands
and establishing a robust international presence.
Our journey began 15 years ago when our visionary founder, Loi Tuan Ee, imported 60
Holstein Jersey cows from Australia and set up a farm in Mawai, Johor. His mission was
ambitious: to offer pure dairy products to the Malaysian market, free from preservatives
and artificial additives, delivered fresh to supermarket shelves.
This commitment to freshness and quality quickly became our unique selling point, setting
us apart from imported dairy companies that relied on powdered or reconstituted milk.
With enthusiastic customer response and support from Khazanah Nasional
Berhad, Malaysia’s sovereign wealth fund, we were propelled on a
transformative path, expanding our production capacity and diversifying
our product range.
A significant breakthrough came with the creation of our innovative
home dealer network. This initiative allowed micro-entrepreneurs in
urban and rural Malaysia to become stockists, dealers and agents
of our products, providing income opportunities to local
communities while enabling us to reach previously untapped
markets.
Today, we are a fully integrated dairy producer, operating seven
dairy farms and four processing facilities across Malaysia and
Australia. Our diverse brand portfolio includes Farm Fresh, Yarra
Farm, Yarra by Farm Fresh, Master Barista, Henry Jones, Nubian
Goat’s Milk, Jom Cha by Farm Fresh, St David Dairy (Australia) and
our most recent acquisitions, Inside Scoop and Sin Wah Ice Cream.

FARMING
Implementing systematic farm management, animal
husbandry and milking practices across seven farms
in Malaysia and Australia
• Incorporating a systematic breeding process using the
AFS breed has resulted in high milk yields from our
dairy herd in Malaysia.
• Adopting a total mixed ration feeding regime ensures
our cows receive a chemically and nutritionally balanced
diet.
• Providing easy access to food and water in our purpose-
built barns, with comfortable dry sand-bedded areas,
promotes the well-being of our herd while contributing
to increased raw milk yield and herd longevity.
• Prioritising strict biosecurity measures, overseen by a
qualified team in veterinary science, animal science and
animal husbandry, ensures the health and safety of our
herd.
• Optimising our milking schedules and utilising advanced
milking stations maximises efficiency and productivity.
