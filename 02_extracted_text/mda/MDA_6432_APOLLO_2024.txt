OVERVIEW OF THE GROUP’S BUSINESS AND OBJECTIVES

[MDA_P003]
Apollo Food Holdings Berhad is principally an investment holding company and currently it has two (2)
wholly owned subsidiaries as below:

[MDA_P004]
(i) Apollo Food Industries (M) Sdn Bhd is principally engaged in manufacturing and trading in
compound chocolates, chocolate confectionery products and layer cakes in overseas market.

[MDA_P005]
(ii) Hap Huat Food Industries Sdn Bhd is principally engaged in distributing and marketing of compound
chocolates, chocolate confectionery products and layer cakes in local market.

[MDA_P006]
Over the years, the Group has recognised itself as one of the leading manufacturers of chocolate
confectionery products and layer cakes in Malaysia.

[MDA_P007]
The aim of the Group is to always fulfil the customer needs and requirements by using the latest
equipment and technology. To accomplish this goal, the Group uses only the finest natural ingredients
sourced from a select panel of suppliers and employs state-of-art technology in manufacturing its
products.

[MDA_P008]
The Group firmly believes that quality sells. Holding this belief, the Group strives to ensure that the
following principles are taken to improve its market position:

[MDA_P009]

Implement and maintain quality management system and continually improve its effectiveness;

Produce products using premium raw and packaging materials;

Use world class wafer and layer cake manufacturing machinery from Europe and constantly
upgrade to improve the quality of products and competitiveness of the Group;

Enhance customer satisfaction by meeting customer requirements;

Recognise customers’ needs by introducing individual packaging; and

Implement quality assurance procedures such as HALAL, GMP and ISO accreditations.

[MDA_P010]
FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE

[MDA_P011]
Revenue

[MDA_P012]
The Group recorded revenue of RM255.27 million for the financial year ended 30 April 2024, a decrease
of 0.67% as compared to RM257.00 million in the previous financial year ended 30 April 2023. A large
proportion of revenue was contributed by local market, accounting for 74.16% of total revenue. There
were no material changes on the sales proportion from previous financial year.

[MDA_P013]
Profit for the Financial Year

[MDA_P014]
The profit after taxation had increased by 71.03% from RM31.65 million as recorded in the previous
financial year to RM54.13 million. During the financial year, the Group recorded a one-off gain (net of
tax) on the disposal of investment properties of RM18.66 million, accounting for 34.47% of profit after
taxation. Excluding the one-off gain, the Group still recorded a commendable 12.08% year-on-year
growth in profit after tax on the back of improved gross profit margins.

[MDA_P015]
The Group’s earnings per share increased from 39.56 sen to 67.66 sen for the financial year.

RISK OVERVIEW

[MDA_P023]
The Group is currently facing a few risk factors that might affect the Group’s profitability. Those risks
include the following:

[MDA_P024]
(i)
Fluctuation of Raw Material Prices

[MDA_P025]
Any fluctuation of raw material prices will have a direct impact on the Group’s gross profit
margin. The Group has taken measures to reduce the risk of price fluctuation by working closely
with suppliers to get reasonable price without compromising quality. The Group will monitor the
price closely and assures that the quality of raw materials is maintained.

[MDA_P026]
(ii)
Fluctuation of Foreign Currency Exchange Rate

[MDA_P027]
Significant fluctuation of foreign currency exchange rate will impact on the Group’s cash flow
movement and profitability. The movement of the foreign currency exchange rate is closely
monitored by the Group’s management.

[MDA_P028]
(iii)
Changes in Consumer’s Preferences

[MDA_P029]
The Group is sensitive to the evolving consumer preferences and will continue to invest in
product innovation to remain relevant to our customer base.

[MDA_P030]
(iv)
Quality of the Products

[MDA_P031]
Poor quality products can harm the Group’s image and reputation. The Group has implemented
quality management systems and quality assurance procedures via HALAL, GMP and ISO
accreditations and sources raw materials from reputable and reliable suppliers to maintain the
quality of the product.

[MDA_P032]
(v)
Labour Market

[MDA_P033]
A large proportion of the Group’s workforce comprises of foreign workers to overcome the
difficulties in employing local employees. Any future changes to the policies imposed by the
Malaysia Government on employment of foreign workers will affect the Group’s production and
consequently, the profitability of the Group.

[MDA_P036]
OPERATION REVIEW

[MDA_P037]
In this challenging operating environment where inflationary pressures remain a key concern impacting
consumers and businesses across the region, the Group continues to deliver commendable overall
performance for the current financial year, a good indication of solid demand for the Group’s products.

[MDA_P038]
In order to remain competitive going forward, the Group will continue its’ efforts to enhance its core
operations. Focus remains on increasing efficiency and leveraging on the Group’s strong brand to
develop new export market and customer segments. Initiatives such as manufacturing space
rationalisation, upscaling automation and digitalisation to optimise overall productivity and efficiency are
envisaged to strengthen the Group’s future growth prospects. Additionally, the Group will proactively
improve it’s distribution and marketing efforts to achieve deeper market penetration while still
maintaining stringent quality control on the Group’s products.

[MDA_P039]
BUSINESS PROSPECT

[MDA_P040]
We remain cautious on the outlook for financial year ending April 2025 for both domestic and
international markets due to uncertainty in global macroeconomics, trade relations, geopolitical events
as well as rising inflationary environment in which the Group operates in.

[MDA_P041]
Price fluctuation of key raw materials supply chain coupled with the weakened Malaysia Ringgit remains
a key challenge for the Group. Having said that, the Group remains committed to maintaining
satisfactory results by continuing to enhance operating efficiency and implementing cautious measures
without compromising product and service quality.

[MDA_P042]
DIVIDEND POLICY

[MDA_P043]
The Group does not have a fixed dividend policy. During the financial year, the Board of Directors
approved a First Interim Dividend of 20 sen per ordinary share and a Second Interim Dividend of 50
sen per ordinary share under the single tier system (tax exempt) for the financial year ending 30 April
2024 that were paid on 03 November 2023 and 24 April 2024 respectively.

[MDA_P044]
In recognition of shareholders’ continuous support, the Board of Directors is pleased to recommend a
Final Dividend of 5 sen per ordinary share under the single tier system (tax exempt) for the financial
year ended 30 April 2024. The dividend will be subject to shareholders’ approval at the forthcoming
Annual General Meeting to be held on 23 October 2024.
