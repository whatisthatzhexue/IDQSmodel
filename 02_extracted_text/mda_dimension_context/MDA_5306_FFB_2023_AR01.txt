While the latest trends in the International Monetary Fund (IMF)’s
Global Uncertainty Index indicate that uncertainty due to factors
such as the COVID-19 pandemic and international trade tensions
have contracted compared to last year, the Russia-Ukraine conflict
remains the dominant contributor for global economic insecurity1. The ongoing war is likely to continue to cause volatility in
commodities, materials and logistics costs. Meanwhile, the World Bank recently softened its stance on
impending global recession, but forecasted global GDP growth
of just 2.1% in 2023, well below the 3.1% in global growth recorded
last year.
