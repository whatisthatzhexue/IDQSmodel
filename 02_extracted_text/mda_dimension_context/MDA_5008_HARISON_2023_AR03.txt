[MDA_P003]
Harrisons' income is mainly derived from the marketing, sales, warehousing and distribution of consumer,
building materials and engineering products, fine wines, agricultural and industrial chemicals, and the
operation of shipping/logistics, travel agencies and retailing. These businesses are conducted as follows:
[MDA_P004]
a. In East Malaysia, Harrisons Sabah Sdn. Bhd. and Harrisons Sarawak Sdn. Bhd. are involved in the
marketing, sales, warehousing and distribution of fast-moving consumer goods, building materials,
engineering products and agricultural chemicals as well as the operation of shipping and travel
agencies; and
[MDA_P005]
b. In Peninsular Malaysia, Harrisons Peninsular Sdn. Bhd. and Harrisons Marketing & Services Sdn. Bhd.
are involved in the marketing, sales, warehousing and distribution of building materials, industrial and
agricultural chemicals, and import and distribution of fine wines as well as the freight forwarding and
shipping.
[MDA_P006]
Presently, Harrisons has over 400 principals, and distributes approximately 13,000 product items to
over 15,000 accounts spread all over Malaysia. The Group operates a total network of 27 branches and
47 warehouses strategically located throughout Malaysia (13 in Peninsular, 9 in Sabah and 5 in
Sarawak) and is supported by over 1,900 employees.
[MDA_P009]
Harrisons’ objective is to focus on its core strength of marketing and distribution, and to maintain its
position as one of the leading distributors in Malaysia. The Group will continue to expand the sales of the
Fast-Moving Consumer Goods and Building Materials by securing new agencies of good quality products
and to grow its customer base in East and West Malaysia.
[MDA_P011]
Revenue
[MDA_P023]
Revenue increased from RM2.17 billion in FY2022 to RM2.26 billion in FY2023. The increase is mainly due
to increase in cement sales in the Building Materials and Engineering Products Division. Cement sales was
driven by tight supplies, rising demands and price increases in Peninsular during FY2023.
[MDA_P024]
Sales of our Fast-Moving Consumer Goods declined slightly by 0.34% due to decrease in sales of certain
agencies and rationalisation of certain products range/agencies of our Fast Moving-Consumer Goods
products. Sales of our Industrial and Agriculture Chemical products decreased by 10.18% as overall
demand and selling price soften in FY2023.
[MDA_P027]
Sales in the Retailing Segment increased by 8.41% as selling price of the Famous Amos Cookies in
Singapore increased following increase in ingredient costs.
[MDA_P028]
Revenue for the Shipping and Others Segment decreased by 18.45% due to lower commissions earned as
container handling and freight commissions declined following overall decline in shipping activities in
Sabah.
[MDA_P029]
Gross Profit
[MDA_P030]
Gross Profit increased from RM254.15 million in FY2022 to RM263.52 million in FY2023. Gross Profit
Margin in FY2023 was flat at 11.68% compared to FY2022. Our sales of FMCG was down by 0.3% but our
Building Materials sales grew by 30%. The Gross Profit Margin of our cement is lower than that of our
FMCG.
[MDA_P031]
Profit Before Taxation
[MDA_P032]
The Group Profit Before Taxation for FY2023 of RM89.52 million as compared to RM89.88 million.
Administrative expenses increased by RM9.22 million for FY2023 as compared to FY2022 as there was a
reversal of allowance for loss of trade receivables of RM5.4 million in FY2022. Interest expense increased
by RM1.77 million as Overnight Policy Rate (OPR) increased 0.25% for the year.
[MDA_P033]
Profit After Taxation
[MDA_P034]
Profit After Taxation increased slightly from RM67.98 million in FY2022 to RM68.80 million in FY2023,
reflecting an increase of 1.20%.

HARRISONS HOLDINGS (MALAYSIA) BERHAD
ANNUAL REPORT 2023
MANAGEMENT DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS (CONTINUED)
The Trade and Distribution Segment comprise mainly of the Fast-Moving Consumer Products businesses
in Sabah and Sarawak and Building Materials and Engineering Products in Peninsular. This segment
continued to perform well in FY2023, exceeding last year’s Profit Before Tax by RM2.1 million (2.71%). The sales of Building Materials and Engineering Products which contributed 19.36% of total revenue
recorded a growth of 26.31% in FY2023. The significant increase is mainly due to strong demand for
cement which pushed up selling prices during the year. The Profit Before Tax for the small Retail Segment increased from last year of RM5.48 million to RM6.16
million this year by RM682,000. Sales of the Famous Amos cookies grew by RM3.9 million or 11.55% for
FY2023. The Shipping and Others Segment Profit Before Tax is RM3.55 million for FY2023 as compared to RM6.69
million in FY2022.
