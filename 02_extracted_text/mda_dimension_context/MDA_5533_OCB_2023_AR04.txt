REVIEW OF THE FINANCIAL RESULTS AND FINANCIAL CONDITIONS (cont’d)
The Group reported a profit before taxation of RM12.7 million for FYE 2023 as opposed to a loss before taxation
of RM2.0 million in the FYE 2022 because of higher profit reported from Consumer Foods and Bedding Products
Divisions. The Consumer Foods Division posted a profit before taxation of RM11.7 million for FYE 2023 as compared to a
loss before taxation of RM2.2 million in the FYE 2022 mainly due to higher products margin because of increase
in selling price and stabilization of raw materials cost during the year. The main raw materials price such as flour,
palm oil and soy bean oil have decreased in the range of 13% to 24% during the year. The Bedding Products Division reported a profit before taxation of RM6.9 million for FYE 2023 as against a profit
before taxation of RM5.5 million in the FYE 2022 because of reopening of economy after Covid-19 restriction. Domestic projects have seen an uptake in activities after the long lockdown. Project sales has increased to RM18.3
million in the FYE 2023 as compared to RM13.6 million reported in the immediate preceding year.
[MDA_P011]
Noodles’ sales reported a 58% increase to RM95.9 million as compared to RM60.7 million in the FYE 2022. These were mainly due to a new OEM business and increase customers coverage because of self-distribution. OEM noodles business registered RM29.9 million as compared to RM15.2 million in the FYE 2022. The Division is
positive on the prospect of this business. Sales of in-house brands noodles also reported an increase of RM20.5
million to RM66.0 million in the FYE 2023. The salad dressing segment also grew by RM4.2 million or 10% mainly contributed by the increase in selling price
and more people are eating out as the economy continue to open-up. The sales of trading segment (creamer) increased by RM3.7 million or 7% due to a higher demand from the export
market. This segment has achieved RM55.9 million sales for FYE 2023 as compared to RM52.2 million in the
immediate preceding financial year. The sales of seasonings segment, on the other hand, saw a decrease by RM0.3 million or 25% in the FYE 2023
attributable to a lower repeat orders from the overseas market for the Ready to Cook Curry Paste range.

Consumer Foods Division
The Semenyih and Sibu noodle plants have a current combined capacity of 12.0 million pieces of noodles per shift
per month. For the full year 2023, the Semenyih noodle plant utilised 93% of its daily shift capacity while Sibu plant
ran at full capacity. The sales of instant noodles surged 58% compared to last year. Domestic market made up 87% of total sales in
this category. Sales of IBUMIE was again strongly powered by the East Malaysian market which was up 21% year-
on-year, partly driven by an increase in volume and price. The Division also started production for an OEM label brand of instant noodles which contributed 31% to the
overall turnover of this product category in Year 2023. Beside the major improvement from existing OEM customers
business, the Division also managed to secure additional OEM customer during the year. MANAGEMENT DISCUSSION

BUSINESS OUTLOOK 2024
Malaysia’s Gross Domestic Product (“GDP”) is expected to grow strongly at 4.5 percent to 5.0 percent in 2024,
primarily on the back of a recovery in the manufacturing sector and driven by the expected expansion of its
domestic consumption that is underpinned by a resilient labour market, and potential recovery in global trades
amid expectation that the United States would avoid a recession.
