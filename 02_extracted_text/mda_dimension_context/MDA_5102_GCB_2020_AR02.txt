ANNUAL REPORT 2020
Besides that, GCB planned to further expand its foothold into Europe. This was made possible through the acquisition of a
17.8-acre land in Glemsford, UK, the site of the former Philips Avent factory, for RM43.8 million in October 2020. The Group
aims to produce value-added liquid cocoa products and confectionery ingredients to serve the UK market by converting
the existing factory into melting and value-added processing facilities. The facilities are targeted to commission in the
second quarter of 2022. As the Group builds up an increasing network of clientele from various parts of the world, our trading subsidiary in Singapore
currently plays an important role to serve as a regional hub to sell our cocoa ingredients globally. In fulfilling our clients’ quality requirements, all our factories have undergone comprehensive international certification
processes and have been certified with HACCP and FSSC 22000, in addition to having passed the Sedex Members Ethical
Trade Audit. This highlights the Group’s focus on maintaining stringent quality and meeting ethical standards of labour.

OPERATIONAL HIGHLIGHTS
Production Highlights
FY2020 was marked by the challenging landscape for our business operations, stemming from several market and supply
chain issues posed by the effects of the COVID-19 pandemic. For one, our operations in Malaysia were disrupted by the lockdown imposed by the Malaysian Government from mid-March
till end-April 2020. While we were granted approvals to maintain operations due to our essential goods category, various
other hurdles arose such as disruptions to trucking and supply activities, and slower response times across other supply
chain participants. In spite of that, we still managed to sustain our grinding level to near maximum capacity of 250,000 MT in Pasir Gudang
and Batam, as we continued to ensure that our cocoa ingredients are readily available to be distributed to meet our clients’
demand. We also managed to register healthy sales of cocoa ingredients in FY2020, albeit at a slightly lower volume from a year
ago. The lower demand for chocolate was a result of closure of travel borders and global lockdowns imposed by numerous
often-travelled-to countries in Europe.
[MDA_P004]
While prices of cocoa butter saw weakness, prices of cocoa solids firmed up as
consumers opted for the cheaper alternative compound chocolate products, of which cocoa powder is the main ingredient. Overall, we maintained a commendable EBITDA of RM349.3 million in FY2020, 5.0% higher compared to RM332.8 million
in the previous year, largely contributed by the one-off gain of RM27.9 million from the disposal of an associate, Fuji Global
Chocolate (M) Sdn Bhd (formerly known as GCB Specialty Chocolates Sdn Bhd). FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE
Group revenue in FY2020 grew 25.3% to RM3.7 billion compared to RM2.9 billion in the last year despite the lower sales
tonnage, with the growth driven mainly by maiden contribution from the sale of industrial chocolate by SCHOKINAG. GCB’s gross profit improved 5.2% to RM376.0 million from RM357.4 million previously, as a result of increased selling price
of cocoa solids and improved yield per tonne. GCB’s pre-tax profit in FY2020 was sustained at RM267.3 million, 0.1% higher
versus RM266.9 million the previous year.
[MDA_P005]
The Group also posted a 2.2% higher net profit attributable to shareholders of RM222.7 million in FY2020, compared to
RM217.9 million in the previous year. The increase was largely from the one-off gain on disposal of an associate, Fuji Global
Chocolate (M) Sdn Bhd, to industrial chocolate producer Fuji Oil Asia Pte Ltd in February 2020, as the latter exercised a call
option to acquire GCB’s 27.75% stake in the associate. As at 31 December 2020, the Group’s total assets stood at RM2.9 billion, increasing 42.8% from RM2.0 billion previously,
due to higher property, plant and equipment from the expansions into Germany, Cote D’Ivoire, and UK, as well as increased
inventory level and trade receivables.
