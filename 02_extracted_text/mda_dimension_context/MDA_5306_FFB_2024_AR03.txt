FARMING
Plans are underway to develop a second farm at Muadzam Shah, which will boost the farm’s capacity from the current
3,100 to approximately 6,000 dairy cows. With the new farm located near the existing one, we are confident of scaling
up activities economically, sharing human capital and resources with our current setup in Muadzam Shah. The new farm
is expected to commence operations in 2025. Sustainable farming is essential for the longevity of our operations, the health of our land and the quality of our products.

UPDATES ON NEW AUSTRALIAN FARM
We have purchased and completed the upgrading of a 300-acre farm property situated next to our processing facility in
Kyabram. Due to its proximity, this new farm strategically fits to the next step expansion as it can absorb all the treated
wastewater from our Kyabram trade waste pond thus adding value for farming purposes. The new farm will continue the
AFS breed development for the Group’s Malaysian farms, which was previously done at another 350-acre farm situated
further away at Echucha Road, Ardmona, which has since been disposed. We have equipped the new farm with a new
rotary milking station and with the added milking capacity we will be able to increase the herd size to enable more milk
production from our own farms in Australia.

MANUFACTURING
With increasing demand for our dairy products,
alongside the success of new products such as Farm
Fresh Grow and Yarra by Farm Fresh, we have
enhanced our manufacturing capacity to support our
growth. At Muadzam Shah, we have installed two new UHT
processing lines and a second filling and packaging
line for 1-litre ambient category products, boosting
our capacity by 22.7 million litres to 103.7 million
litres. Combined with the operationalisation of our
new processing facility at Taiping in June 2023, our
total capacity for finished goods reached 193.7
million litres as of 31 March 2024, reflecting a 20%
increase over the past year. Looking ahead, we are on track to complete our new
manufacturing hub at Enstek in 2025, a significant development for our next phase of growth. Part of our Enstek facility
will house our production of CPG ice cream. Beyond Malaysia, our new production facility at San Simon in the Central Luzon region of the Philippines is set to
commence operations in the third quarter of 2024. This facility will produce a wide range of Farm Fresh products for
the Greater Manila market, including chilled products, UHT products and growing up milk powder.

EXPANDING OUR PRODUCT PORTFOLIO
Driven by consistent and strategic innovation to meet evolving market expectations and expand across the dairy value
chain, we successfully commercialised 16 new products in FY2024, increasing our total number of SKUs to 192. A major highlight was the introduction of a powder variant of our successful Farm Fresh Grow growing up milk, originally
launched in FY2023. By creating a powder form of our nutritious milk, free from hidden or unnecessary ingredients, we
have significantly increased the accessibility of Farm Fresh Grow to meet the rising demand for quality growing up milk. Other key launches during the year included Almond and Oat Barista Milk variants, Natural Farm Yoghurt, 100% Australia
Organic Milk, Chocolate Mint Milk, Riceberry Milk and Cooking Cream. Looking ahead, we are on track to enter several new product categories in the upcoming financial year.
