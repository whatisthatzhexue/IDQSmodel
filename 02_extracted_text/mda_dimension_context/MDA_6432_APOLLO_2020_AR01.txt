MANAGEMENT DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS
[MDA_P005]
(ii) Hap Huat Food Industries Sdn Bhd is principally engaged in distributing and marketing of compound
chocolates, chocolate confectionery products and layer cakes in local market.
[MDA_P007]
The aim of the Group is to always fulfil the customer needs and requirements by using the latest equipment
and technology. In order to achieve this, the Group uses only the finest natural ingredients sourced from a
select panel of suppliers and employs state-of-art technology in manufacturing its products.
[MDA_P012]
The Group registered a turnover of RM174.93 million for the financial year ended 30 April 2020, a decrease of
7.37% as compared to RM188.84 million in previous financial year ended 30 April 2019. 72% of the turnover
was contributed by local market and there were no material changes on the sales proportion from previous
financial year.
[MDA_P014]
The profit after taxation had decreased by 18.26% to RM14.24 million from RM17.43 million as recorded in the
previous financial year. The current financial year’s profit margin was improved by 1.46% from 24.77% to
26.23%. The lower turnover and other income coupled with a higher administrative expense had resulted in a
lower profit.
[MDA_P019]
Total assets had decreased from RM266.29 million as at last financial year end to RM263.55 million as at
current financial year end. A decrease of RM2.74 million in total current assets was mainly contributed by the
decrease in receivable and inventories.

[MDA_P028]
In this new era, consumers tend to consume healthy foods as compared to the olden days. The Group is
aware of the shift in consumer preferences and understand that consumers nowadays prefer to consume
healthier products with less sugar and sweetness. The Group will assess the risk and consider whether to
produce healthier products in the near future to prevent potential loss of health-conscious customers.
[MDA_P036]
BUSINESS PROSPECT
[MDA_P038]
In view of inflation on key raw materials’ prices and the volatility of Ringgit Malaysia against foreign currencies,
raw materials’ prices are expected to be unstable in the forthcoming year. Coupled with the uncertainties in
the global economy due to the Covid-19 pandemic, the Group expects that the business performance in
forthcoming year will become more challenging. In order to maintain its profitability, the Group will focus its
efforts to closely monitor the prices of raw materials and to review its business strategies when deem
necessary.
[MDA_P041]
The Board of Directors recommend a first and final dividend of 20 sen under the single tier system (tax
exempt) for the financial year ended 30 April 2020. The dividend rate will subject to the shareholders’ approval
at the forthcoming Annual General Meeting to be held on 28 October 2020. The dividend will be paid on 12
January 2021 once it is approved.
