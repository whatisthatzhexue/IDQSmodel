[MDA_P003]
AGB is an investment holding company with its subsidiaries involved in Tin Cans Manufacturing
(“Tin Cans”) segment and the Food and Beverage (“F&B”) segment. The Tin Cans segment involves
manufacturing of various tins, cans and other containers and providing tinplates printing services. The
F&B segment involves manufacturing and selling of milk and other related dairy products, as well as
packing and trading of all types of dairy products.
[MDA_P004]
For Tin Cans segment, most of the customers are locally based, with minor market share in Singapore.
The customer base consists of various industries mainly in biscuit, paint and chemical, edible oil and
food processing industries. As for the tinplates printing services, most of the printing services are
exported within South-East Asia market as well as local market.
[MDA_P005]
Whilst for F&B segment, approximately 80% of the dairy products are exported, mainly to West
Africa, South-East Asia Region and American Continent. The dairy products comprise of sweetened
condensed milk, evaporated milk as well as milk powder packed in both bulk and consumer packs.
2.
REVIEW OF FINANCIAL RESULTS AND FINANCIAL CONDITION
[MDA_P006]
For FYE 2022, the Group achieved revenue of RM549.82 million as compared to RM499.86 million in the
previous financial year, an increase of RM49.96 million. Profit before tax decreased by RM8.63 million to
RM42.80 million as compared to RM51.43 million in the previous financial year.
[MDA_P007]
For Tin Cans segment, the revenue decreased by RM3.38 million to RM124.13 million from RM127.51 million
mainly due to lower sales demand in current year-to-date.
[MDA_P008]
As for the F&B segment, the revenue increased by RM53.35 million to RM425.70 million from RM372.35
million mainly due to the increase in the sales demand from the customers and partly due to the increase in
selling prices.
[MDA_P009]
Profit before tax of Tin Cans segment increased by RM1.38 million to RM26.38 million from RM25.00 million
is mainly due to lower operating expenses incurred during the year.
[MDA_P010]
For F&B segment, the profit before tax decreased by RM9.63 million to RM18.46 million from RM28.09
million and is mainly attributable to the higher cost of goods sold caused by the increase in raw material
costs. Although the selling prices were adjusted, the full increase in costs could not be completely passed
down to the customers, especially in the domestic market. In addition, the earlier price commitment to our
customers and the continual increase in the raw material prices contributed to the decrease in margin in the
current year-to-date.
[MDA_P013]
In FYE 2022, the total borrowings increased by RM166.05 million to RM253.96 million from RM87.91 million
mainly due to financings through term loan for the acquisition of land. As a result, the gearing ratio increased
by 39.96% to 63.05% from 23.09%.
[MDA_P014]
On the investor’s perspective, the return on equity decreased by 1.49% to 8.34% from 9.83%. The return
on total assets decreased by 2.39% to 4.66% from 7.05%. Both the decreases of the returns are due to the
decrease in profitability in FYE 2022.
[MDA_P015]
Interest coverage ratio is approximately 23 times in FYE 2022 as compared to 110 times in the previous
financial year, which reflect the Group’s ability to pay off its debts against current year’s profit.
[MDA_P016]
As for share information, the earnings per share (“EPS”) is 10.85 sen for the FYE 2022 as compared to 12.00
sen in the previous financial year. The net assets per share (“NAPS”) increased by 7 sen to RM1.31 for FYE
2022 against RM1.24 in the previous financial year. The price earnings (“PE”) ratio is 12.44 for FYE 2022 as
compared to 13.42 in the previous financial year. The share price in FYE 2022 and in the previous financial
year was at RM1.35 and RM1.61 respectively.
[MDA_P018]
The main factors that affected the operating activities in the Tin Cans segment are shortage of labour and
increase in raw materials prices.
[MDA_P019]
Raw material prices directly affect the profitability and to remain competitive, we have always strived to
provide the most competitive prices to our customers. The Management is actively monitoring the raw
materials cost and engages with the customers on the selling price adjustments. The Management is always
vigilant in monitoring the cost of manufacturing to stay competitive in the industry and ensure that this
segment remains its profitability.
[MDA_P020]
For F&B segment, the main factors that affected the operating activities were volatility in raw materials prices
and freight costs.
[MDA_P022]
Due to the high percentage of outputs that are exported, freight cost is another factor that affects the
operations of the F&B segment. During the first half of FYE 2022, the pandemic-related delays and closures
of ports led to high freight costs. During this period, our subsidiaries in the F&B segment managed the
situation by engaging with multiple shipping agents in order to get the best quotes and the earliest possible
delivery date to suit the customer’s requirement. During the second half of FYE 2022, the freight costs started
to decline and this trend eased some cost pressure for the F&B segment.
[MDA_P025]
The Group’s revenue and operating results could be adversely affected by many factors which include,
amongst others, the costs of raw materials, costs of labour as well as maintenance costs of equipment
and machineries.
