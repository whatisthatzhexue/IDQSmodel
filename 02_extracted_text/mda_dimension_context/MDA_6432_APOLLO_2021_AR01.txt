MANAGEMENT DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS
[MDA_P005]
(ii) Hap Huat Food Industries Sdn Bhd is principally engaged in distributing and marketing of compound
chocolates, chocolate confectionery products and layer cakes in local market.
[MDA_P007]
The aim of the Group is to always fulfil the customer needs and requirements by using the latest equipment and
technology. In order to achieve the aim, the Group uses only the finest natural ingredients sourced from a select
panel of suppliers and employs state-of-art technology in manufacturing its products.
[MDA_P012]
The Group registered a turnover of RM190.39 million for the financial year ended 30 April 2021, an increase of 8.84%
as compared to RM174.93 million in previous financial year ended 30 April 2020. 75.96% of the turnover was
contributed by local market and there were no material changes on the sales proportion from previous financial
year. During the financial year, the increase of sales demand in local market has improved the overall turnover of
the Group.
[MDA_P014]
The profit after taxation had increased by 21.77% to RM17.34 million from RM14.24 million as recorded in the
previous financial year. The increase in profit after taxation during the financial year was mainly due to reversal of
impairment loss on Property, Plant and Equipment and fair value gain on other investments.
[MDA_P020]
Total assets had decreased from RM263.55 million as at last financial year end to RM260.61 million as at current
financial year end mainly due to higher dividend paid during the financial year.
