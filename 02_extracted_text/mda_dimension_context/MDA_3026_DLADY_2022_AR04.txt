DUAL DILEMMA & DAIRY LEADERSHIP
The dynamics of business have changed over the
last three years. In 2022, the market continued to
perform but with uncertainty. There was no stability
in price movements and trade behaviour. Supply chain
disruptions affected incoming raw materials and their
prices. As a result, supply chain and procurement
from business continuity and resilience perspectives
were in the limelight replacing the traditional lead role of
commercial. Local and international political and policy
contexts made markets even more fragile. Regulatory
landscape kept evolving, especially in relation to
responsible business conduct and sustainability. Against
this backdrop, in our position and approach, we remained
cautiously optimistic.
RAMJEET KAUR VIRIK
