AGB was incorporated in Malaysia on 22nd November 2000. It was subsequently listed on the Second
Board of Kuala Lumpur Stock Exchange in 2003 and transferred to the Main Board of Bursa Malaysia
Securities Berhad in 2006.
[MDA_P004]
AGB is an investment holding company with its subsidiaries engaging in Tin Cans Manufacturing (“Tin
Cans”) and the Food and Beverage (“F&B”) segments. The Tin Cans segment involves manufacturing
of various tins, cans and other containers and providing tinplates printing services. The F&B segment
involves manufacturing and selling of milk and other related dairy products, as well as packing and
trading of all types of dairy products.
[MDA_P005]
For Tin Cans segment, most of the customers are locally based, with minor market share in Singapore.
The customer base consists of various industries mainly in biscuit, paint and chemical, edible oil and
food processing industries. As for the tinplates printing services, most of the printing services are
exported within South-East Asia market as well as local market.
[MDA_P006]
Whilst for F&B segment, approximately 80% of the dairy products are exported, mainly to West
Africa, South-East Asia Region and American Continent. The dairy products comprise of sweetened
condensed milk, evaporated milk, UHT full cream plus flavoured milk as well as milk powder packed in
both bulk and consumer packs.

3.
REVIEW OF OPERATING ACTIVITIES
[MDA_P019]
The main factors that affected the operating activities in the Tin Cans segment are fluctuation in raw materials
prices and machineries maintenance.
[MDA_P020]
Raw material prices directly affect the profitability of the Group. To remain competitive, we have always
strived to provide the most competitive prices to our customers. The Management is actively monitoring the
raw materials cost and engages with the customers on the selling price adjustments. The Management is
always vigilant in monitoring the cost of manufacturing to stay competitive in the industry and ensure that
this segment maintains its profitability.
[MDA_P021]
Maintenance is required for the existing machinery. The unique and scarcity of the spare parts is a challenge
to the maintenance team when fixing the machineries of older models. To resolve these issues, the older
model machineries are upgraded as necessary.
