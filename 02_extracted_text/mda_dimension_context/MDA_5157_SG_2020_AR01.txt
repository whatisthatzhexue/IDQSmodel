Our Group’s COGS increased by RM20.6 million or 29.1% from RM70.5 million in FYE 2019 to RM91.1 million in FYE 2020. The reason on the
increase in COGS was mainly due to the inventories written off and rework of slow moving trading goods and low margin products. This
causes the increased in material conversion cost i.e. increase in processing cost to convert existing goods to other products.
