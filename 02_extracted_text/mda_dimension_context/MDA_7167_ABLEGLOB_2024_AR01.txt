[MDA_P001]
Statement on Management Discussion and Analysis (“MD&A”) provides an insight of the financial
performance of Able Global Berhad (“AGB”) and its subsidiaries (“the Group”) for the financial year
ended 31 December 2024 (“FYE 2024”). This MD&A also contains information on the Group’s overview
of business and operations, current financial position, reviewing of operating activities, assessing the
operational risks faced by the Group and discussing the future prospect of the Group.

[MDA_P003]
In FYE 2024, the total borrowings decreased by RM15.75 million to RM254.20 million from RM269.95 million
mainly due to repayment of long-term financing, contributing positively to the gearing ratio. The gearing ratio
decreased by 8.34% from 61.00% to 52.66% mainly due to continued growth in shareholders’ equity and the
decrease in total borrowings, reflecting a stable financial position.
[MDA_P004]
From the investors’ perspective, the return on equity increased by 2.68% to 14.50% from 11.82%. The return
on total assets increased by 1.88% to 8.40% from 6.52%. Both the increases of the returns are due to the
increase in profitability in FYE 2024.
[MDA_P010]
Maintenance is required for the existing machinery. The unique and scarcity of the spare parts is a challenge
to the maintenance team when fixing the machineries of older models. To resolve these issues, the older
model machineries are upgraded as necessary.
[MDA_P012]
The prices of dairy and sugar remain volatile and it will affect the manufacturing costs significantly
which poses a challenge to the Group. Management remains highly vigilant in monitoring the cost of
manufacturing, exploring alternative sourcing options, and optimizing inventory management to maintain
cost competitiveness.. As such, the management is in constant contacts and dialogues with the suppliers
and traders to gauge the situation of the market in order to make the procurement decisions.

The Group will continue to adopt effective measures such as prudent management and efficient
operating procedures to mitigate these factors that will affect the Group’s business.
[MDA_P035]
For Tin Cans segment, the business remains challenging due to an increasingly competitive market. The
cost of raw material stability is dependent on the outcome of the anti-dumping petition by Perstima, the local
tinplate manufacturer. The final determination by MITI is expected in May 2025 and that decision will affect
the industry as a whole.
[MDA_P036]
On the other hand, the demand for F&B segment remains healthy although the market is highly competitive.
Most of the raw material prices have remained quite stable in this quarter under review. Although the
commodities prices and foreign exchange are volatile due to the current global weather patterns and
economic situations, we expect this segment to continue to be profitable.
[MDA_P039]
Barring any unforeseen circumstances, the Board believes that the overall prospects and future financial
performance of the Group is expected to be favourable in the mid-term.
[MDA_P040]
The Group, although without a dividend policy, intends to declare dividends depending on the Group’s profits
generated during each financial year, by taking into consideration the expansion plan and future investments
on capital expenditure. For FYE 2024, the total dividends of 7.50 sen (2023: 6.50 sen) per ordinary share
had been declared and paid to the shareholders amounting to RM23.07 million (2023: RM19.99 million) at a
dividend yield of 3.89% (2023: 4.33%).
