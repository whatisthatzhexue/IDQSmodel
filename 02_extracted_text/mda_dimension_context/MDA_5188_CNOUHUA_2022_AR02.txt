b) Profit(Loss) after Taxation
Loss for FY 2022 was RMB 25.940 million as compared with FY 2021's loss of RMB 4.263 million.The higher loss
figure in FY 2022 was mainly due to Inventories written down, which amount to RMB15.396 million, and
Impairment for PPE of RMB6.388 million also contributed to the higher loss figure. Eliminate the
aforementioned factor the operating loss in FY2022 was comparable to FY 2021, Both year's operating result
was badly affected by the prolonged impact of the COVID-19 pandemic
a) Revenue
For FY 2022, China Ouhua, which is principally involved in importing and sales of grape wines with its sole
manufacturing plant located in Yantai City, Shandong Province, China recorded a decline in revenue of
7.01% or RMB 1.102 million from RMB 15.721 million in FY 2021 to RMB 14.619 million in current financial
year. Principal target market of China Ouhua's wine products is throughout China. For FY 2022, the sales
recorded was solely through sales of red wine. Given that the wine market in China remains highly competitive
and due to the prolonged impact of the COVID-19 pandemic, Management had made many efforts with the
objective to improve the sales revenue.
[MDA_P004]
Although the Company has enhanced the sales of imported wine, the
total sales revenue did not improve. .

c) Liquidity and Capital Resources
As at FY 2022, China Ouhua had cash and cash equivalent amounting to RMB 10.611 million with no external
borrowing. Total current assets was RMB 148.373 million and total current liability was RMB5.174 million.
Therefore, China Ouhua is confident that its existing financial standing is sufficient to finance its working capital
requirements for the next 24 months.
Moving forward, in the next financial year, the Management will continue to work hard to improve the sales
revenue. The Management of the Company is confident to lead the Company to a new era by diversifying
into importation of overseas wine to market in new areas and online business.
