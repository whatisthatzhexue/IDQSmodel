[MDA_P011]
Revenue
[MDA_P012]
The Group registered a turnover of RM190.39 million for the financial year ended 30 April 2021, an increase of 8.84%
as compared to RM174.93 million in previous financial year ended 30 April 2020. 75.96% of the turnover was
contributed by local market and there were no material changes on the sales proportion from previous financial
year. During the financial year, the increase of sales demand in local market has improved the overall turnover of
the Group.
[MDA_P013]
Profit for the Financial Year
[MDA_P014]
The profit after taxation had increased by 21.77% to RM17.34 million from RM14.24 million as recorded in the
previous financial year. The increase in profit after taxation during the financial year was mainly due to reversal of
impairment loss on Property, Plant and Equipment and fair value gain on other investments.
[MDA_P020]
Total assets had decreased from RM263.55 million as at last financial year end to RM260.61 million as at current
financial year end mainly due to higher dividend paid during the financial year.
[MDA_P024]
(i) Fluctuation of Raw Material Prices
[MDA_P025]
The fluctuation of raw materials’ prices will have an impact on the Group’s profitability. The Group has taken steps
in reducing the risk of price fluctuation by negotiating better terms with suppliers. Under the supervision of the
Directors, Procurement Department monitors the prices closely and assures that the quality of raw materials is not
compromised in line with lower purchase price.
[MDA_P031]
Deterioration in quality of the product would bring negative impact to the Group’s public image and reputation. To
prevent this from happening, the Group has implemented quality management systems and quality assurance
procedures via HALAL and ISO accreditation. The Group also purchases top quality raw materials from reputable and
reliable suppliers.
[MDA_P036]
Due to the evolving situation of Covid-19 pandemic, the Group is mindful and will defer any major capital
commitment to avoid any adverse effect on the financial situation of the Group and to safeguard the interest of the
Group. However, the Group will monitor the situation closely and make the necessary decisions as and when the
situation is conducive.
[MDA_P039]
In view of inflation on key raw materials’ prices and the volatility of Ringgit Malaysia against foreign currencies, raw
materials’ prices are expected to be unstable in the forthcoming year. Coupled with the uncertainties in the global
economy due to the Covid-19 pandemic, the Group expects that the forthcoming year will continue to be
challenging. In order to maintain its profitability, the Group will focus its efforts to closely monitor the prices of raw
materials and to review its business strategies when deem necessary.
[MDA_P040]
The Board expects a challenging year for the Group and will adopt a vigilant and prudent stance. The Group will
strive to ensure that it continues to achieve satisfactory results by implementing prudent measures and improving
operational efficiency so as to sustain the current profit margin without compromising the products’ quality. To
improve its stream of revenue, the Group will put more efforts in focusing and enhancing its brands recognition and
products’ varieties for new business opportunities.
