ANNUAL REPORT 2023
REVIEW OF THE FINANCIAL RESULTS AND FINANCIAL CONDITIONS (cont’d)
Bedding Products Division
The Bedding Products Division registered a higher revenue of RM68.8 million and a profit before taxation of RM6.9
million in the FYE 2023 compared to a revenue of RM67.1 million and a profit before taxation of RM5.5 million in the
FYE 2022. The higher profit before taxation was mainly attributable to the improvement in sales and product margin. The Division showed an improvement in its domestic project sales to RM18.3 million in the FYE 2023 as compared
to RM13.6 million in the immediate preceding financial year. The increase was mainly due to increase in activities
in the domestic scene after the reopening of Covid-19 lockdown. The domestic retail market recorded lower sales of 4% to RM50.1 million after the surge in demand in the previous
year because of the reopening of the economy and the effect of the pent-up demand. Revenue from export sales dropped to RM0.8 million in the FYE 2023 as compared to RM1.4 million reported for
FYE 2022 due to the Covid-19 pandemic impact on the overseas’ hospitality segment.

Consumer Foods Division
The Semenyih and Sibu noodle plants have a current combined capacity of 12.0 million pieces of noodles per shift
per month. For the full year 2023, the Semenyih noodle plant utilised 93% of its daily shift capacity while Sibu plant
ran at full capacity. The sales of instant noodles surged 58% compared to last year. Domestic market made up 87% of total sales in
this category. Sales of IBUMIE was again strongly powered by the East Malaysian market which was up 21% year-
on-year, partly driven by an increase in volume and price. The Division also started production for an OEM label brand of instant noodles which contributed 31% to the
overall turnover of this product category in Year 2023. Beside the major improvement from existing OEM customers
