Dear Shareholders,
In 2022, Malaysia’s economy strengthened with the
reopening of international borders and the transition
to the endemic phase of COVID-19. This had positively
impacted various industries, trade and investment-
related services. However, world economic activities
were adversely affected by monetary policy tightening,
surging inflation, fiscal restraints, and the continuing
Russian-Ukraine war. Despite the global and domestic economic challenges, the PPB Group had achieved
a commendable performance in 2022. All key segments contributed positively to the
increase in revenue and profitability of the Group, coupled with a higher contribution
from Wilmar International Limited. Economic
