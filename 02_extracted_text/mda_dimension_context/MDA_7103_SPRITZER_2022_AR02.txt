Revenue, Results and Dividend
The Group recorded its highest annual revenue of
RM433.3 million in the financial year ended December
31, 2022, a significant increase of 31% from the previous
year’s revenue of RM331.0 million, mainly due to the
increase in sales volume and better selling prices.
[MDA_P003]
The
current year revenue is also 16% higher than our pre-
pandemic annual revenue of RM374.4 million recorded
in year 2019. The revenue growth is mainly attributed to
our much higher sales recorded throughout the year after
movement restrictions were relaxed and the reopening of
all economic sectors following the Malaysia’s transition to
endemic phase of COVID-19 in April 2022. Consequent to
the higher revenue recorded, the profit before tax of the
Group for the year ended December 31, 2022 was higher
by 49% at RM45.2 million (2021: RM30.3 million) mainly
due to economies of scale and better sales mix. Similarly,
the profit after tax has also increased significantly by 53%
to RM37.0 million (2021: RM24.2 million). In line with
the higher earnings, the Group’s earnings per share and
diluted earnings per share for the year stood at 17.6 sen
and 17.0 sen (2021: 11.5 sen and 11.2 sen) respectively. The pre-tax profit margin has risen from 9.2% to 10.4%
mainly due to economies of scale and favourable sales
mix achieved in the current year. Similarly, the current
year’s net margin has also risen from 7.3% to 8.5%.
[MDA_P004]
During the year, the Company has adopted a Dividend
Policy which targets a dividend pay-out ratio of at least
35% of its audited consolidated profit after taxation
attributable to shareholders for each financial year, after
excluding non-operating income that is capital in nature. In line with this Dividend Policy and after considering the
cash flow requirement of the Group, the directors have
proposed a first and final dividend of 6.25 sen (2021: 4.50
sen) per ordinary share in respect of the financial year
ended December 31, 2022. The current dividend pay-out
ratio is at 36% (2021: 39%). Management Discussion and Analysis

The Group’s cash flows has remained healthy and we continued to generate positive cash flows from our business activities. In year 2022, the Group generated a net cash of RM40.7 million (2021: RM35.6 million) from its operating activities. Our
net earnings continued to be supported by a corresponding operating cash flows.
[MDA_P006]
The cash outflows for the purchase of
plant, property and equipment in 2022 amounted to RM103.8 million (2021: RM24.8 million) which included the payment
for the purchase of land in Taiping and Yong Peng and the installation of a new raw water treatment plant in Taiping. The
Company also paid the first and final dividend of 4.5 sen per share in respect of the financial year ended December 31,
2021, amounting to RM9.4 million to its shareholders on June 23, 2022. During the year, the Company also spent RM2.2
million for the purchase of its own shares. Despite the substantial cash outflows for the purchase of the land in Taiping and
Yong Peng, the Group was able to sustain its net cash position throughout 2022. Financial Position and Gearing
The financial position of the Group has been further strengthened with the net profit of RM37.0 million recorded during the
year. Our total assets have increased by RM43.0 million or 8% from RM557.9 million on December 31, 2021 to RM601.0
million on December 31, 2022. Our total liabilities have increased by RM12.3 million or 13% from RM95.2 million on
December 31, 2021 to RM107.5 million on December 31, 2022.
