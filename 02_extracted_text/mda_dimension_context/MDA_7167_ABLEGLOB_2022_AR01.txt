MANAGEMENT DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS
The Statement on Management Discussion and Analysis (“MD&A”) provides an insight of
the financial performance of Able Global Berhad (“AGB”) and its subsidiaries (“the Group”)
for the financial year ended 31 December 2022 (“FYE 2022”). This MD&A also contains
information on the Group’s overview of business and operations, current financial position,
reviewing of operating activities, assessing the operational risks faced by the Group and
discussing the future prospect of the Group.
1.
OVERVIEW OF BUSINESS AND OPERATIONS
A.
Business and Operations
[MDA_P004]
For Tin Cans segment, most of the customers are locally based, with minor market share in Singapore.
The customer base consists of various industries mainly in biscuit, paint and chemical, edible oil and
food processing industries. As for the tinplates printing services, most of the printing services are
exported within South-East Asia market as well as local market.
[MDA_P006]
For FYE 2022, the Group achieved revenue of RM549.82 million as compared to RM499.86 million in the
previous financial year, an increase of RM49.96 million. Profit before tax decreased by RM8.63 million to
RM42.80 million as compared to RM51.43 million in the previous financial year.
[MDA_P007]
For Tin Cans segment, the revenue decreased by RM3.38 million to RM124.13 million from RM127.51 million
mainly due to lower sales demand in current year-to-date.
[MDA_P008]
As for the F&B segment, the revenue increased by RM53.35 million to RM425.70 million from RM372.35
million mainly due to the increase in the sales demand from the customers and partly due to the increase in
selling prices.
[MDA_P010]
For F&B segment, the profit before tax decreased by RM9.63 million to RM18.46 million from RM28.09
million and is mainly attributable to the higher cost of goods sold caused by the increase in raw material
costs. Although the selling prices were adjusted, the full increase in costs could not be completely passed
down to the customers, especially in the domestic market. In addition, the earlier price commitment to our
customers and the continual increase in the raw material prices contributed to the decrease in margin in the
current year-to-date.
[MDA_P021]
The prices of dairy and sugar remain volatiles and it will affect the manufacturing costs significantly. This
posed a challenge to the Management in inventory control and procurement management. As such, the
management is in constant contacts and dialogues with the suppliers and traders to gauge the situation of
the market in order to make the procurement decisions.

A.
Business Risk
[MDA_P042]
For Tin Cans segment, demand is expected to grow marginally in this matured and stable industry but will
remain challenging in the short-term. Raw material costs are less volatile but still at a high level. With raw
materials cost at such high levels, it is creating a challenging business environment and will put additional
pressure on the profit margin in the short term.
[MDA_P046]
Barring any unforeseen circumstances, the Board believes that the overall prospects and future financial
performance of the Group is expected to be favourable in the mid-term.
