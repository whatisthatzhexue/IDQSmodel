and Analysis

customers. INDUSTRY OVERVIEW
With the recent changes taking place in the presence of globalisation, sustainability goals and health consciousness,
businesses are closely being evaluated based on their operational procedures, adaptability and environmental
impact. World travels, the internet, purchasing power, awareness on nutrition and fast-paced lifestyle are also
crucial indicators for the food industry. The multiple big trends marked a significant shift and become the catalysts
in reshaping the industry in this region. It is thus vital for companies such as SDS Group Berhad and its subsidiaries
(“SDS” or the “Group”) to seize the opportunities in today’s dynamic market. Whilst activities and the economy have returned to normalcy, SDS has been at the forefront to benefit from
reopening of dine-in restaurants as well as more sales opportunities from all other revenue streams post pandemic.
[MDA_P009]
The diversified business model tackling the retail and wholesale segments has proven to be working well for the
Group in ensuring continued supply of its products to Malaysian customers. The results were also a strong indication
of its financial discipline and agility to fast decision-making in capitalising on any opportunities.
[MDA_P018]
Total assets recorded an increase by 13.1% to RM161.9 million during FY2023 mainly due to higher capital
expenditures, trade receivables and cash and bank balances from the increase in revenue and net profit. Total
borrowings on the other hand have reduced significantly by 72.2% from RM17.4 million to RM4.9 million in FY2023
mainly due to early repayment of term loan and lease liabilities under hire purchase arrangement.
[MDA_P026]
Concurrently, the Group expanded its R&D team and facilities to be ahead in meeting the demand for its products.
The use of warmer display was its latest feature and has garnered good response. Some of the Group’s bakery
products were sold in warm temperature to improve their attractiveness. Due to the good response, SDS has since
created new products series specially for warmer display. Other outcome coming from product research include
ready-to-eat, ready-to-drink, and frozen products as well as sauces. New special menus were also launched during
festive period together with some new bakery products namely Chocolate Tuxedo Cheese Cake, Fudgy Choco
Velvet, Snowy Almond Croissant and more.
