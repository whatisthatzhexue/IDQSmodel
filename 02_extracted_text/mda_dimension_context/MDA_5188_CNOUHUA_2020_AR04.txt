a) Revenue
For FY 2020, China Ouhua, which is principally involved in importing and sales of grape wines with its sole
manufacturing plant which is located in Yantai City, Shandong Province, China recorded a revenue decline of 6.12%
or RMB 1.337 million from RMB 21.836 million in FY 2019 to RMB 20.499 million in current financial year. Principal target market of China Ouhua’s wine products is throughout China. For FY 2020, the sales breakdown
between red wine and white wine were 100% and 0.00% respectively. The management made many efforts with
the objective to improve the sales revenue, given the competition of wine market in China remains highly
competitive and prolonged impact of the COVID-19 pandemic. The Company has enhanced the sales of imported
wine and realised some effect. b) Profit(Loss) after Taxation
Loss for FY 2020 was RMB 16.736 million as compared with FY 2019's profit of RMB 0.313 million. The
increased in losses was mainly due to the impairment of inventories, which amounted to RMB12.898
million. c) Liquidity and Capital Resources
As at FY 2020, China Ouhua had cash and cash equivalent amounting to RMB 8.49 million with no
external borrowing.
