MANAGEMENT DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS
Overview of Business and Objectives
HB Global Limited (“HBGL”) is principally an investment
holding company and currently the Group has one
active wholly owned subsidiary as below:
100% owned Subsidiary:
(i) Shandong Hengbao Foodstuffs Co., Ltd. (“SHF”) –
the main operating plant situated in China
Our Group specializes in the processing, packaging,
and production of a wide array of ready-to-serve
foods, frozen vegetables, and other culinary products
through our subsidiaries. Over the years, we have
established ourselves as a leading gourmet convenient
food specialist in China, equipped with advanced
food processing technologies such as cleaning,
slicing/cutting, blanching, boiling, frying, vacuum
frying, steaming, smoking, stewing, and barbequing. We produce a diverse range of food materials and
quick-frozen products, primarily catering to the needs
of our customers and distributors. Additionally, we
operate as an original equipment manufacturer (OEM)
for third-party brands.
[MDA_P003]
Recognizing that customer
preferences evolve, we continuously strive to monitor
and refine our offerings to meet their changing needs. By prioritizing quality, the Group aims to fortify its
market position and cultivate enduring relationships
with its customers. Operation Overview
HBGL Group’s revenue had increased by RMB3.9
million or approximately 4.9% from RMB76.1 million in
financial year ended 31 December 2022 (“FYE2022”) to
RMB80.0 million in financial year ended 31 December
2023 (“FYE2023”). The increase was mainly due to
increase in sales of China subsidiary as during FYE2023,
there was recovery from disruption in the production
and supply chain due to lockdown in certain areas of
China which have been affected with rising Covid 19
cases. The Group had a decrease in net loss before
taxation to RMB47.9 million in FYE2023 as compared to
RMB171.0 million in FYE2022. Dear Valued Shareholders,

Financial Review
In the FYE2023, the Group’s revenue slightly increase
by RMB 3.9 million or approximately 4.9% compared
to the previous financial year, mainly attributable to
increase sales by the China subsidiary. This shows the
recovery from disruptions in the production and supply
chain caused by lockdowns in certain areas of China
that were affected by the rising number of Covid-19
cases. In addition, other income in FYE 2023 amounted
to RMB7.9 million, which was considerably lower than
the RMB14.1 million recorded in FYE 2022. Furthermore, the Group’s other operating expenses
decreased from RMB137.3 million to RMB28.5 million,
mainly due to higher impairment loss provided on
property, plant and equipment and write off of
intangible assets and prepayments of land use rights
in FYE 2022. As a result, the Group incurred a net
loss before taxation of RMB45.8 million in FYE 2023
compared to RMB167.7 million in FYE 2022, mainly due
to significantly lower other operating expenses in FYE
2023. Financial Position
As of December 31, 2023, the Company’s net assets
amounted to approximately RMB162.4 million.
