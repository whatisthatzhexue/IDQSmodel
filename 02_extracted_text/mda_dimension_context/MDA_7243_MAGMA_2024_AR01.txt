MANAGEMENT DISCUSSION & ANALYSIS
(Overview of Group Business)
Magma Group Berhad (“.BHNB” or “the Group”) is actively evolving from a traditional hospitality company into an
innovative, experience-focused property and lifestyle group. Anchored in Malaysia’s hotel industry, Magma manages
a portfolio of distinctive assets under the WOLO and Impiana brands, delivering culturally immersive hospitality across
strategic urban and regional locations. As of the financial year ended 31 December 2024 (“FYE2024”), the Group’s portfolio includes WOLO Kuala Lumpur,
Impiana Hotel Ipoh, Impiana Hotel Senai, and an associate stake in Impiana KLCC Hotel. The full consolidation of
WOLO Kuala Lumpur under Magma Bintang Sdn Bhd has notably enhanced the Group’s hospitality performance,
fueling robust segmental growth. Magma’s strategic focus on high-impact locations reflects its commitment to delivering exceptional guest experiences. Through innovative offerings and targeted market positioning, the Group consistently sets industry standards for
service, quality, and operational excellence.
[MDA_P002]
The Group’s foray into real estate leverages its hospitality strengths to deliver elevated living experiences and long-
term value. As a cornerstone of its growth strategy, property development is set to play a significant role in Magma’s
future trajectory. Looking ahead to 2025, Magma is well-positioned to seize emerging opportunities in the dynamic hospitality
and tourism sectors. With a strong asset base, proven operational excellence, and an unwavering focus on guest
satisfaction, the Group is poised to lead with innovation and sustainable growth. Review of Financial Performance

For the FYE2024, Magma recorded revenue of RM14.93 million, a 3.2% increase from RM14.46 million in financial year
ended 31 December 2023 (“FYE2023”). This growth reflects the Group’s continued efforts to strengthen operational
efficiency and adapt to evolving market conditions. Gross Profit (“GP”) declined by 0.9% to RM16.39 million (FYE2023: RM16.54 million). However, the Gross Profit Margin
(“GPM”) contracting from 114.4% to 109.8%, primarily due to higher input and operating costs, driven by inflation and
industry-wide cost pressures. Administrative expenses increased significantly by 75.7%, reaching RM21.83 million (FYE2023: RM12.42 million). This
rise was mainly due to strategic restructuring initiatives, increased staffing costs, and inflation-related adjustments.

FYE2024 represented a significant period of transition for Magma, the implementation of targeted restructuring
initiatives focused on cost optimization and operational efficiency. While these transformative efforts temporarily
impacted the Group’s financial performance, resulting in a loss after tax (LAT) of RM45.67 million, they have
established a stronger operational foundation for sustainable growth. In spite of the broader challenges, the Hotel
Operations segment continued to exhibit robust performance, underscoring its strategic importance to the Group’s
future trajectory. Looking forward, Magma will concentrate on expanding its hotel-based operations and refining management services
to capitalize on growth opportunities, while recalibrating its property strategy to align with market realities. The Group
remains committed to sustainable value creation and operational excellence in a dynamic business environment.
