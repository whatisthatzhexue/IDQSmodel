MANAGEMENT DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS
[MDA_P006]
Presently, Harrisons has over 400 principals, and distributes approximately 13,000 product items to
over 15,000 accounts spread all over Malaysia. The Group operates a total network of 27 branches and
47 warehouses strategically located throughout Malaysia (13 in Peninsular, 9 in Sabah and 5 in
Sarawak) and is supported by over 1,900 employees.
[MDA_P023]
Revenue increased from RM2.17 billion in FY2022 to RM2.26 billion in FY2023. The increase is mainly due
to increase in cement sales in the Building Materials and Engineering Products Division. Cement sales was
driven by tight supplies, rising demands and price increases in Peninsular during FY2023.
[MDA_P027]
Sales in the Retailing Segment increased by 8.41% as selling price of the Famous Amos Cookies in
Singapore increased following increase in ingredient costs.
[MDA_P030]
Gross Profit increased from RM254.15 million in FY2022 to RM263.52 million in FY2023. Gross Profit
Margin in FY2023 was flat at 11.68% compared to FY2022. Our sales of FMCG was down by 0.3% but our
Building Materials sales grew by 30%. The Gross Profit Margin of our cement is lower than that of our
FMCG.

MANAGEMENT DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS (CONTINUED)
The Trade and Distribution Segment comprise mainly of the Fast-Moving Consumer Products businesses
in Sabah and Sarawak and Building Materials and Engineering Products in Peninsular. This segment
continued to perform well in FY2023, exceeding last year’s Profit Before Tax by RM2.1 million (2.71%). The sales of Building Materials and Engineering Products which contributed 19.36% of total revenue
recorded a growth of 26.31% in FY2023. The significant increase is mainly due to strong demand for
cement which pushed up selling prices during the year. The Profit Before Tax for the small Retail Segment increased from last year of RM5.48 million to RM6.16
million this year by RM682,000. Sales of the Famous Amos cookies grew by RM3.9 million or 11.55% for
FY2023. The Shipping and Others Segment Profit Before Tax is RM3.55 million for FY2023 as compared to RM6.69
million in FY2022.

Strategies and Future Profit Growth
The distribution business of our Fast-Moving Consumer Goods becomes more challenging in days ahead
as more distribution players emerge to compete in the East Malaysia market. We will continue to focus on
serving our customers better by improving staff competencies to ensure high service standards are offered
to our customers and principals. We will also focus and strategise to secure new agencies and brands to
complement our diversified agencies portfolio. We have completed our Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) to manage and integrate important parts of
our business for Sabah successfully in FY2022. We have commenced implementing our ERP systems in
Sarawak in FY2023 and expects to roll out in FY2024. This technology upgrades will enhance efficiencies
in our operations to serve our customers and principals effectively. Prospects
We expect slow global growth, price of goods to increase and local affordability to impact the domestic
economy in 2024. We also appreciate that the Fast-Moving Consumer Products is more resilient and less
impacted by moderate price increase.
