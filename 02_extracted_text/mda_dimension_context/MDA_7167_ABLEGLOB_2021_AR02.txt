AGB was incorporated in Malaysia on 22nd November 2000. It was subsequently listed on the Second
Board of Kuala Lumpur Stock Exchange in 2003 and transferred to the Main Board of Bursa Malaysia
Securities Berhad in 2006.
[MDA_P003]
AGB is an investment holding company with its subsidiaries involved in Tin Manufacturing (“Tin Cans”)
segment and the Food and Beverage (“F&B”) segment. The Tin Cans segment involves manufacturing
of various tins, cans and other containers and providing tinplate printing services. The F&B segment
involves manufacturing and selling of milk and other related dairy products, as well as packing and
trading of all types of dairy products.
[MDA_P004]
For Tin Cans segment, most of the customers are locally based, with minor market share in Singapore.
The customer base consists of various industries mainly in biscuit, paint and chemical, edible oil
and food processing industries. As for the tinplate printing services, most of the printing services are
exported within South-East Asia market as well as local market.
[MDA_P005]
Whilst for F&B segment, approximately 80% of the dairy products are exported, mainly to West
Africa, South-East Asia Region and American Continent. The dairy products comprise of sweetened
condensed milk, evaporated milk as well as milk powder packed in both bulk and consumer packs.
2.
REVIEW OF FINANCIAL RESULTS AND FINANCIAL CONDITION
[MDA_P006]
In year 2021, the Group’s business operations continue to be disrupted by the COVID-19 pandemic with Full
Movement Control Order (“FMCO”) in June 2021 and various restrictions and regulations to curb the spread
of COVID-19. During this period, output was reduced due to the limitation of production workforce to 60%
in accordance to the Government guidelines and factory operations stoppages due to COVID-19 pandemic,
thus limiting our sales to the group of companies.
[MDA_P007]
For FYE 2021, the Group achieved revenue of RM499.86 million as compared to RM502.26 million in the
previous financial year, a decreased of RM2.4 million. Profit before tax decreased by RM1.79 million to
RM51.43 million as compared to RM53.22 million in the previous financial year.
[MDA_P008]
For Tin Cans segment, the revenue increased by RM12.13 million to RM127.51 million from RM115.38 million
as a result of higher sales and the effect from the selling prices adjustment to tin cans during FYE 2021.
[MDA_P009]
As for the F&B segment, the revenue decreased by RM14.53 million to RM372.35 million from RM386.88
million mainly due to the decrease in production output from the lockdown imposed by the government and
factory operations stoppages due to Covid-19.
[MDA_P010]
Profit before tax of Tin Cans segment increased by RM11.86 million to RM25.00 million from RM13.14 million.
The adjustment in selling prices to Tin Cans customers in the FYE 2021 contributed to the normalisation
of the profit margin and therefore, a higher profit as compared to the exceptionally low profit in previous
financial year. The exceptionally lower profit before tax in previous financial year was mainly due to lower
revenue resulted from the lower demand across all industries caused by the initial outbreak of COVID-19
pandemic and the increase in costs during MCO1.0 period.

For F&B segment, the profit before tax decreased by RM13.17 million to RM28.09 million from RM41.80

Furthermore, the F&B segment shared the losses of equity accounted joint ventures amount to RM3.86
million in FYE 2021 as both joint ventures commenced the operations in Quarter 3 of FYE 2021.
[MDA_P014]
The Group’s total assets in FYE 2021 increased by RM61.03 million to RM531.40 million from RM470.37
million in the previous financial year. The increase in total assets was mainly arose from the higher inventories
value in FYE 2021 due to an increase in the raw material cost. The Group also plan for higher stock level
to ensure sufficient stock and prevent disruption to the business operation during the COVID-19 pandemic
period.
[MDA_P015]
In FYE 2021, the total borrowings increased by RM45.25 million to RM87.86 million from RM42.61 million
mainly due to short-term trade financings through trust receipts and revolving credits for the purchase of raw
materials. As a result, the gearing ratio increased by 11.15% to 23.07% from 11.92%.
[MDA_P016]

to lower revenue and increase in cost of goods sold in FYE 2021. The return on total assets decreased
by 1.42% to 7.05% from 8.47% mainly due to lower production output and limitation of workforce in the
production during the FMCO period resulted in lower revenue.
