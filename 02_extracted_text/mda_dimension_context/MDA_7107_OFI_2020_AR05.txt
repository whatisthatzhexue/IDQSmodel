MANAGEMENT DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS
OVERVIEW OF THE GROUPS’ BUSINESS AND OPERATIONS
Oriental Food Industries Holdings Berhad ("the Company” or “the Group”) has made its mark in the snack food and
confectionery manufacturing industry today as one of the companies holding the leading position in the said industry
in Malaysia.
The Group remains focused on placing the needs and interests of its customers first by manufacturing an assortment
of snack food and confectionery products that focuses on quality. Generally, these products can be divided into four
(5) broad categories, which are snack food, wafer, biscuits, potato snacks and bakery products.
The Group has various product brand names, most of which are commercially strong and generating the required
sales and profit both locally and oversea. Super Ring, Jacker, Rota and Oriental are currently well-known household
brand names in Malaysia.

DIVIDEND
The Group continuously declares a dividend payout for every quarter for the past years. The total dividend declared
during the financial year 2020 was 1.5 sen (2019: 2.2 sen) per share. GROUP’s PROSPECTS
The Group is in its final stage of expansion and currently pending for some minor machine acquisition to complete
its new production lines. These new lines are expected to contribute positively to the growth and profitability of
the Group. The management views the current year contraction in revenue is just a temporary setback due to the
pandemic and slowdown in the global economy. The Group still expects a positive contribution from the investment
in various product lines in years to come from the new and existing market segment due to the active promotional
activities taken. The Board expects that the performance of the Group will be satisfactory for the next financial year ending 31 March
2021, despite facing various challenges of competitive markets.
