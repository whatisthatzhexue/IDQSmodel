[NEWS_P001] KUALA LUMPUR: Rex Industry Bhd is changing its name to ETA Group Bhd, effective Dec 8, 2025. Bursa Malaysia, in a statement, said following the change, the company’s stock short name will be updated from REX to ETA, while its warrants will be traded under the new short name ETA-WA, replacing REX-WA. However, Bursa Malaysia noted that the company’s stock codes will remain unchanged.
