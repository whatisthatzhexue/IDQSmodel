[NEWS_P001] KUALA LUMPUR: Last-minute selling pressure pushed the FBM KLCI lower on Wednesday, snapping a two-day winning streak amid mixed regional markets. At closing, the benchmark index closed down 2.03 points, or 0.13% to 1,588.21 with 19 of the component stocks finishing lower. The index has risen to an intraday high of 1,591.04 Market breadth turned negative, with losers outnumbering gainers by 647 to 376. With nearly twice as many stocks declining as advancing, bearish sentiment persisted as selling pressure continued to weigh on the broader market.

[NEWS_P002] About 2.55 billion shares, valued at RM2.5bil, changed hands. Rakuten Trade Sdn Bhd vice-president of equity research, Thong Pak Leng, said the FBM KLCI closed marginally lower due to profit-taking after a recent strong rally. Meanwhile, key regional indices finished mixed, following a heavy sell-off in tech stocks on the Nasdaq overnight. Thong described today’s profit-taking as a healthy correction, allowing the benchmark index to digest the recent strong uptrend.

[NEWS_P003] “The FBM KLCI is holding well above the key 1,585 support mark, which sets the stage for further gains. “Barring any external shocks, we expect the benchmark index to trade within the 1,570 to 1,600 range through the week,” he said. Among the decliners, PETRONAS Dagangan slid 44 sen to RM22.50, Kuala Lumpur Kepong lost 32 sen to RM19.20, PPB Group fell 27 sen to RM8.62 and Heineken declined 26 sen to RM22.10. Nestle jumped RM1.50 to RM89, Far East added 46 sen to RM4.18, PETRONAS Chemicals gained 46 sen to RM4.18 and Riverview rose 20 sen to RM3.05.

[NEWS_P004] In terms of fund flows, foreign investors and local retailers were net sellers on Tuesday offloading equities worth RM75mil and RM9mil respectively. Local institutions emerged as net buyers with total purchases valued at RM84mil. Meanwhile, the ringgit was trading at 4.2248 against the US dollar, down 0.03%, while it edged up 0.16% against the Singapore dollar to 3.2866.
