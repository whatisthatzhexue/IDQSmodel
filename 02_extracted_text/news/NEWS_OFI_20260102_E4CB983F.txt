[NEWS_P001]
KUALA LUMPUR: Their Majesties Sultan Ibrahim, King of Malaysia, and Raja Zarith Sofiah, Queen of Malaysia, have graciously extended New Year greetings to all Malaysians.

[NEWS_P002]
In a post on Sultan Ibrahim's Facebook page, Their Majesties expressed their hope that the new year would bring continuous peace and prosperity to the nation, reported Bernama.

[NEWS_P003]
Sultan Ibrahim also called upon the people to further strengthen unity and mutual respect for lasting harmony.

[NEWS_P004]
“Their Majesties also pray that Malaysia will always be under the protection of Allah SWT and be blessed with enduring peace, progress and well-being,” the post read.
