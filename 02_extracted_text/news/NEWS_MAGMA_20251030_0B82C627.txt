[NEWS_P001]
LUCENA CITY: Taal Volcano in Batangas province recorded 61 seismic activities within 24 hours, the Philippine Institute of Volcanology and Seismology (Phivolcs) reported on Thursday (Oct 30).

[NEWS_P002]
In its morning bulletin, Phivolcs said 41 volcanic earthquakes were recorded. The quakes were accompanied by 20 volcanic tremors that lasted two to 30 minutes.

[NEWS_P003]
The 61 seismic events in a single day were the highest number recorded this month.

[NEWS_P004]
Taal Volcano has been rattled by almost daily earthquakes this month, with no seismic activity noted on Oct 1, 5, 20, and 23, according to data from Phivolcs.

[NEWS_P005]
From Oct 1 to 30, the agency recorded 336 volcanic earthquakes and 56 tremor episodes, a significant rise from September’s 189 quakes and 129 tremors.

[NEWS_P006]
Phivolcs explained that volcanic earthquakes are triggered by magmatic or magma-related processes beneath or near an active volcano, unlike tectonic quakes caused by fault movements. - Philippine Daily Inquirer/ANN
