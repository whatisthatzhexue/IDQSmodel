Philippines raises Mayon Volcano alert level amid signs of increased unrest

[LEAD]
MANILA: (Bernama-Xinhua) The Philippines raised the alert level of Mayon volcano in Albay province on Luzon island on Thursday (Jan 1) following signs of increased volcanic activity, reported Xinhua.

[NEWS_P001]
MANILA: (Bernama-Xinhua) The Philippines raised the alert level of Mayon volcano in Albay province on Luzon island on Thursday (Jan 1) following signs of increased volcanic activity, reported Xinhua.

[NEWS_P002]
The Philippine Institute of Volcanology and Seismology (PHIVOLCS) said the escalation from alert level 1 (low-level unrest) to alert level 2 (increasing or moderate level of unrest) indicates that magma is moving beneath the volcano, increasing the likelihood of sudden explosive activity.

[NEWS_P003]
"This means that there is current unrest driven by shallow magmatic processes that could lead to hazardous magmatic eruption," the institute said in an advisory.

[NEWS_P004]
"The public is strongly advised to be vigilant and refrain from entering the six-kilometre-radius Permanent Danger Zone," added the institute, stressing that the restriction is crucial to minimising risks from explosions and related hazards.

[NEWS_P005]
Mayon is the country's most active volcano and has a long history of eruptive activity. - Bernama-Xinhua
