#        (factual accuracy, sourcing transparency, balance, information
#        increment, headline fit), per team discussion in plans/dimension.txt.
#
# ----------------------------------------------------------------------
# SHARED SCALE ANCHORS
# ----------------------------------------------------------------------

1 = severely insufficient
2 = weak
3 = adequate
4 = good
5 = excellent

# ----------------------------------------------------------------------
# DIMENSION DEFINITIONS (name / weight / definition)
# ----------------------------------------------------------------------

N01  name: Factual accuracy and internal consistency        weight: 0.30
     definition: Whether company names, dates, events, amounts, percentages, people,
                  timeline, and causal statements are clear, internally consistent,
                  and not obviously exaggerated.

N02  name: Source transparency and verifiability        weight: 0.25
     definition: Whether the article identifies sources, quotations, evidence chains,
                  citations, or other traceable clues.

N03  name: Balance and objectivity        weight: 0.15
     definition: Whether the article avoids one-sided claims, emotional wording,
                  promotional exaggeration, or commentary replacing facts.

N04  name: Relevance and information increment        weight: 0.20
     definition: Whether the article adds useful company-research information rather
                  than repeating announcements or low-relevance material.

N05  name: Expression clarity and headline fit        weight: 0.10
     definition: Whether title, lead, and body are aligned and clearly written without clickbait.
