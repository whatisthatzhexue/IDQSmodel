# ----------------------------------------------------------------------
# SHARED SCALE ANCHORS
# ----------------------------------------------------------------------

1 = severely insufficient
2 = weak
3 = adequate
4 = good
5 = excellent

# ----------------------------------------------------------------------
# DIMENSION DEFINITIONS (name / weight / definition / anchors)
# ----------------------------------------------------------------------

AR01  name: MD&A content completeness        weight: 0.15
      definition: Coverage of operating review, financial performance, business segments,
                   market changes, drivers, risks or challenges, and outlook within the
                   provided MD&A text only.
      1: Extremely short or slogan-like MD&A with almost no substantive operating or financial content.
      2: Basic operating description but little financial performance, business explanation, or change reason.
      3: Basic operating and financial performance are covered, but business, market, risk, or outlook content is incomplete.
      4: Operating, financial, business change, market background, and some risk or outlook information are covered.
      5: Systematic and traceable coverage of performance, business, market, risk, outlook, and management explanation.

AR02  name: MD&A internal numeric consistency and traceability        weight: 0.25
      definition: Clarity, mathematical self-consistency, directional consistency, and logical
                   traceability of financial expressions inside the provided MD&A text.
      hard_limit: This is not financial statement verification and must not claim audited
                   financial statements match.
      required_wording: The numbers are internally consistent within the provided MD&A text.
      1: Financial numbers are confusing, directions conflict, percentages are clearly wrong, or key numbers create major misleading risk.
      2: Some numbers are present, but context is weak and some calculation or explanation problems are visible.
      3: Revenue, profit, or cost changes are basically described with no major contradiction, but traceability is limited.
      4: Financial numbers, percentages, and directions are mostly clear; calculable items are broadly correct.
      5: Financial performance is highly clear and internally self-consistent, with strong explanations for amounts, percentage changes, segments, and drivers.

AR03  name: Operating analysis specificity        weight: 0.30
      definition: Specific explanation of revenue, cost, profit, segments, products, regions,
                   volume, price, raw materials, currency, demand, and other operating drivers.
      1: Mostly slogan-like language with almost no specific operating explanation.
      2: Some operating description but little explanation of drivers.
      3: Major operating changes are explained, but product, region, cost, or volume detail is limited.
      4: Revenue, profit, cost, or business changes are explained with identifiable main drivers.
      5: Operating results are explained in data-backed and causal terms across products, regions, costs, demand, price, or other drivers.

AR04  name: MD&A internal risk and forward-looking disclosure        weight: 0.20
      definition: Disclosure of challenges, risks, uncertainty, management outlook, and mitigation
                   within the provided MD&A text only.
      1: No substantive risk or outlook content, or only empty slogans.
      2: Challenges or risks are mentioned but are highly generic.
      3: Main risks or outlook are listed, but specificity, quantification, or mitigation detail is limited.
      4: Risks or challenges are reasonably specific and include impact or management responses.
      5: Risks are specific, important, traceable, and include impact, mitigation, and forward-looking uncertainty.

AR05  name: MD&A readability and structural clarity        weight: 0.10
      definition: Whether structure, paragraphs, language, headings, tables, and logic help
                   readers understand the MD&A.
      1: Disorganized, jargon-heavy, promotional, and hard to understand.
      2: Weak readability with repetition, slogans, or long unfocused paragraphs.
      3: Basically readable but structure and emphasis are only average.
      4: Clear structure, concise language, and helpful headings or paragraphs.
      5: Highly clear, logical, concise, and easy to navigate.
