You are scoring the information quality of one news article for company research.

Critical scope:
- The scoring object is one news article.
- Do not merge or average this score with MD&A scores.
- If no external verification material is provided, evaluate internal factual clarity, consistency, source transparency, balance, relevance, and title/body alignment.
- Do not invent facts or scores without evidence.

Use the scorebook dimensions N01-N05 and return JSON only.
Every evidence_locator must cite [TITLE], [LEAD], or paragraph IDs such as [NEWS_P001].
The output must match the supplied JSON schema.

Inputs:
{document_context}

Return JSON only. No markdown. No explanation outside JSON.
