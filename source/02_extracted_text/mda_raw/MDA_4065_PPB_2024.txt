Group Outlook and Prospects for 2025 Looking to 2025, while the global environment continue to pose

[MDA_P007]
challenges with some possible downside risks from slowdown of trading partners and business restrictions, growth of the Malaysian economy will continue to be bolstered by robust expansion in investment activity, resilient household spending and expansion in exports supported by Malaysia’s strong economic fundamentals. Inflation is also expected to remain manageable. The economy will benefit from these cascading effects. In light of these projections, the Group will remain steadfast in executing our strategic business plans, capitalising on emerging opportunities while proactively addressing potential challenges. With deep market insights into our various business segments, we are well-positioned to navigate the evolving market landscape and align our efforts accordingly to drive sustainable growth. Despite ongoing challenges in the global grain market,

[MDA_P008]
including adverse weather conditions in key grain-producing regions, evolving government policies, shifting trade dynamics and economic pressures, the Grains & Agribusiness segment remains confident in our ability to navigate and manage these complexities. By leveraging our expertise in grain sourcing and technical know-how, we will continue to deliver high-quality

[MDA_P009]
products to our customers. We expect performance of the segment to be satisfactory in 2025. The Consumer Products segment will continue to expand our product range and strengthen our market presence to enhance distribution efficiency amid rising operating costs. With a well-established distribution network, robust logistics infrastructure, and resilient household spending in Malaysia,
