REX INDUSTRY BERHAD
Annual Report 2021
Changes in the composition of the Group
There were no changes in the composition of the Group for the financial year ended 30 June 2021. Corporate Exercise
The Company undertook a fund-raising exercise during the financial year for a renounceable rights issue of new ordinary
shares at the issue price of RM0.10 per rights share, on the basis of 1 rights share for every 1 existing share held. With
246,626,468 Right Shares subscribed, the Company raised proceeds of approximately RM24.66 million. It was listed and
quoted on the Main Market of Bursa Malaysia Securities Berhad on 15 December 2020. The proceeds raised from the Rights Issue will be utilised firstly in priority to defray the expenses related to the Rights
Issue, and secondly towards repayment of bank borrowings, thirdly towards upgrade of machineries and lastly toward the
working capital required for the day-to-day operations of the Group. Dividend
The Company has recommended a first and final dividend of 0.2 sen per ordinary share amounting to RM986,505 in respect
of the financial year ended 30 June 2021.

[MDA_P009]
Outlook and Prospects
The Group’s business will remain challenging and volatile due to the uncertainty caused by Covid-19 pandemic. We are,
however, encouraged by our performance in the last quarter of FYE 2021. We are cautiously confident of building on this
positive momentum into FYE 2022. With the aggressive rate of vaccination programs being rolled out in Malaysia, along with a streamlined product portfolio, our
proven strategies and plans and great teamwork, the group remains optimistic on further strengthening our market position. Covid-19 has resulted in more frugal consumers and a shift in purchase sites and habits. At-home consumption, e-commerce,
modern trade and budget RM2.00 shops are gaining firmer traction. Budget RM2.00 shops will see greater relevance going
forward but while it offers a good avenue to expand our business into, eroding margins may pose a challenge. The group sees improvements for its margins going forward as we continue to improve our production efficiency, product
mix and grow our topline.

[MDA_P010]
Since 2018, REX has reduced its products by 40% to focus more on higher margin products,
products that were less profitable but required lots of resources to produce were outsourced, headcount were significantly
rationalized by investing in automation. The challenges brought about by the pandemic has made the management team take a closer look at its distribution
channels, inventory management and cash conversion cycle. This has driven us to make significant changes to our goods
return policies and how we handle our distributors. This approach has a positive effect as inventory write-offs and doubtful
debts have reduced significantly. We remain proactive in addressing the challenges ahead and are cautiously optimistic of retaining a positive performance
in the new FYE 2022. MANAGEMENT DISCUSSION

Significant Event
The Covid-19 pandemic will remain the most significant event for the new Financial Year. It appears that Malaysians will
have to live with the virus and exercise self-responsibility as the Government prepares its people for the “endemic” phase
of Covid-19 by the end of October 2021. In the “endemic” phase, sectors that had to be closed due to Covid-19 containment efforts will eventually be allowed to
open with new Covid-19 norms being practised. Whilst the business environment is expected to remain challenging and volatile, the Group with its streamlined product
portfolio, great teamwork and astute execution of strategies and plans is confident to surmount the challenges ahead and
capitalize from school re-opening and HORECA segment starting out again. Acknowledgements
I take this opportunity to thank my fellow Board members, our shareholders, customers and business partners for their
continued support, trust and unwavering confidence over the past years. To all our dedicated staff and management team, thank you for your commitment and leading the company forward.
