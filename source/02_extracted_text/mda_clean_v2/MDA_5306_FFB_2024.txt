[MDA_P007] MANUFACTURING With increasing demand for our dairy products, alongside the success of new products such as Farm Fresh Grow and Yarra by Farm Fresh, we have enhanced our manufacturing capacity to support our growth. At Muadzam Shah, we have installed two new UHT processing lines and a second filling and packaging line for 1-litre ambient category products, boosting our capacity by 22.7 million litres to 103.7 million litres. Combined with the operationalisation of our new processing facility at Taiping in June 2023, our total capacity for finished goods reached 193.7 million litres as of 31 March 2024, reflecting a 20% increase over the past year. Looking ahead, we are on track to complete our new manufacturing hub at Enstek in 2025, a significant development for our next phase of growth. Part of our Enstek facility will house our production of CPG ice cream. Beyond Malaysia, our new production facility at San Simon in the Central Luzon region of the Philippines is set to commence operations in the third quarter of 2024. This facility will produce a wide range of Farm Fresh products for the Greater Manila market, including chilled products, UHT products and growing up milk powder.
