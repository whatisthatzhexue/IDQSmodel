[MDA_P003] Apollo Food Holdings Berhad is principally an investment holding company and currently it has two (2) wholly owned subsidiaries as below:

[SECTION: Business Review]

[MDA_P003]
[MDA_P004] (i) Apollo Food Industries (M) Sdn Bhd is principally engaged in manufacturing and trading in compound chocolates, chocolate confectionery products and layer cakes in overseas market.

[SECTION: Business Review]

[MDA_P004]
[MDA_P005] (ii) Hap Huat Food Industries Sdn Bhd is principally engaged in distributing and marketing of compound chocolates, chocolate confectionery products and layer cakes in local market.

[SECTION: Business Review]

[MDA_P005]
[MDA_P006] Over the years, the Group has recognised itself as one of the leading manufacturers of chocolate confectionery products and layer cakes in Malaysia.

[SECTION: Business Review]

[MDA_P006]
[MDA_P007] The aim of the Group is to always fulfil the customer needs and requirements by using the latest equipment and technology. To accomplish this goal, the Group uses only the finest natural ingredients sourced from a select panel of suppliers and employs state-of-art technology in manufacturing its products.

[SECTION: Business Review]

[MDA_P007]
[MDA_P008] The Group firmly believes that quality sells. Holding this belief, the Group strives to ensure that the following principles are taken to improve its market position:

[SECTION: Business Review]

[MDA_P008]
[MDA_P009] • Implement and maintain quality management system and continually improve its effectiveness; • Produce products using premium raw and packaging materials; • Use world class wafer and layer cake manufacturing machinery from Europe and constantly upgrade to improve the quality of products and competitiveness of the Group; • Enhance customer satisfaction by meeting customer requirements; • Recognize customers’ needs by introducing individual packaging; and • Implement quality assurance procedures such as HALAL, GMP and ISO accreditations.

[MDA_P011] Revenue

[SECTION: Financial Review]

[MDA_P010]
[MDA_P012] The Group recorded revenue of RM257.11 million for the financial year ended 30 April 2023, an increase of 36.56% as compared to RM188.27 million in the previous financial year ended 30 April 2022. The reopening of Malaysia’s borders has increased the demand of the Group’s products which result in higher sales. A large proportion of revenue was contributed by local market, accounting for 68.38% of total revenue. There were no material changes on the sales proportion from previous financial year.

[SECTION: Financial Review]

[MDA_P011]
[MDA_P014] The profit after taxation had increased by 217.45% from RM9.97 million as recorded in the previous financial year to RM31.65 million due to an increase in sales.

[SECTION: Financial Review]

[MDA_P012]
[MDA_P015] The Group’s earnings per share increased from 12.46 sen to 39.56 sen.

[SECTION: Risk and Outlook]

[MDA_P017]
[MDA_P023] The Group is currently facing a few risk factors that might affect the Group’s profitability. Those risks include the following:

[SECTION: Risk and Outlook]

[MDA_P018]
[MDA_P024] (i) Fluctuation of Raw Material Prices

[SECTION: Risk and Outlook]

[MDA_P019]
[MDA_P025] Any fluctuation of raw materials’ prices will have a direct impact on the Group’s gross profit margin. The Group has taken measures to reduce the risk of price fluctuation by working closely with suppliers to get reasonable price without compromising the quality of raw material. The Group will monitor the price closely and assures that the quality of raw materials is maintained.

[SECTION: Risk and Outlook]

[MDA_P020]
[MDA_P026] (ii) Fluctuation of Foreign Currency Exchange Rate

[SECTION: Risk and Outlook]

[MDA_P021]
[MDA_P027] Significant fluctuation of foreign currency exchange rate will impact on the Group’s cash flow movement and profitability. The movement of the foreign currency exchange rate is closely monitored by the Group’s management.

[SECTION: Risk and Outlook]

[MDA_P022]
[MDA_P028] (iii) Changes in Consumer’s Preferences

[SECTION: Risk and Outlook]

[MDA_P023]
[MDA_P029] Consumers tend to consume healthy food as compared to the past. The Group is aware of the shift in consumer’s preferences and will consider producing healthier products to meet the requirement of such health-conscious customers.

[SECTION: Risk and Outlook]

[MDA_P024]
[MDA_P030] (iv) Quality of the Products

[SECTION: Risk and Outlook]

[MDA_P025]
[MDA_P031] Poor quality products can harm the Group’s image and reputation. The Group has implemented quality management systems and quality assurance procedures via HALAL, GMP and ISO accreditations and sources raw materials from reputable and reliable suppliers to maintain the quality of the product.

[SECTION: Market Review]

[MDA_P026]
[MDA_P033] A large proportion of the Group’s workforce is relied on foreign workers to overcome the difficulties in employing local employees. Any future changes to the policies imposed by the Malaysia Government on employment of foreign workers will affect the Group’s production and consequently, the profitability of the Group.

[SECTION: Dividends]

[MDA_P036]
