The Board and Management team have been vigilant and continue to take the necessary measures to mitigate the potential threats that may be disrupting the smooth running of the business. SDS employed risk management process of risk identification, impact assessment, profiling matrix and the mitigation plans, based on the general principles of the risk management framework. The Group were aware of intensified competition and challenging environment given inflationary pressure, high food cost, labour shortage as well as increased awareness on carbon footprint. Some of the mitigation action plans included product diversification to keep up with the customers’ changing preferences and taste. Other measures involved in detailed analysis of products disposals for retail outlets in order to reduce cost.

[MDA_P028]
