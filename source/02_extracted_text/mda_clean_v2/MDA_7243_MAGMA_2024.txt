[MDA_P001] MANAGEMENT DISCUSSION & ANALYSIS (Overview of Group Business) Magma Group Berhad (“.BHNB” or “the Group”) is actively evolving from a traditional hospitality company into an innovative, experience-focused property and lifestyle group. Anchored in Malaysia’s hotel industry, Magma manages a portfolio of distinctive assets under the WOLO and Impiana brands, delivering culturally immersive hospitality across strategic urban and regional locations. As of the financial year ended 31 December 2024 (“FYE2024”), the Group’s portfolio includes WOLO Kuala Lumpur, Impiana Hotel Ipoh, Impiana Hotel Senai, and an associate stake in Impiana KLCC Hotel. The full consolidation of WOLO Kuala Lumpur under Magma Bintang Sdn Bhd has notably enhanced the Group’s hospitality performance, fueling robust segmental growth. Magma’s strategic focus on high-impact locations reflects its commitment to delivering exceptional guest experiences. Through innovative offerings and targeted market positioning, the Group consistently sets industry standards for service, quality, and operational excellence.
