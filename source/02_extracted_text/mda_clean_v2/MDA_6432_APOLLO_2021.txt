[SECTION: Business Review]

[MDA_P006] Over the years, the Group has established itself as one of the leading manufacturer of chocolate confectionery products and layer cakes in Malaysia.

[SECTION: Business Review]

[MDA_P006]
[MDA_P007] The aim of the Group is to always fulfil the customer needs and requirements by using the latest equipment and technology. In order to achieve the aim, the Group uses only the finest natural ingredients sourced from a select panel of suppliers and employs state-of-art technology in manufacturing its products.

[SECTION: Business Review]

[MDA_P007]
[MDA_P008] The Group firmly believes that quality sells. Holding this belief, the Group strives to ensure that following principles are taken to improve its market position:

[SECTION: Business Review]

[MDA_P008]
[MDA_P009] • Implement and maintain quality management system and continually improve its effectiveness • Produce products with top quality of raw and packaging materials • Use world class wafer and layer cake manufacturing machinery from Europe and constantly upgrade to improve the quality and competitiveness • Enhance customer satisfaction by meeting customer requirements • Recognize customers’ needs by introducing individual packaging • Implement quality assurance procedures such as HALAL and ISO accreditation

[MDA_P011] Revenue

[SECTION: Financial Review]

[MDA_P010]
[MDA_P012] The Group registered a turnover of RM190.39 million for the financial year ended 30 April 2021, an increase of 8.84% as compared to RM174.93 million in previous financial year ended 30 April 2020. 75.96% of the turnover was contributed by local market and there were no material changes on the sales proportion from previous financial year. During the financial year, the increase of sales demand in local market has improved the overall turnover of the Group.

[SECTION: Financial Review]

[MDA_P011]
[MDA_P014] The profit after taxation had increased by 21.77% to RM17.34 million from RM14.24 million as recorded in the previous financial year. The increase in profit after taxation during the financial year was mainly due to reversal of impairment loss on Property, Plant and Equipment and fair value gain on other investments.

[SECTION: Financial Review]

[MDA_P012]
[MDA_P015] Consequently, the Group’s earnings per share had increased from 17.81 sen to 21.67 sen over the financial year.

[SECTION: Risk and Outlook]

[MDA_P017]
[MDA_P023] The Group is currently facing a few risk factors that might affect the Group’s profitability. Those risks include the following:

[SECTION: Risk and Outlook]

[MDA_P018]
[MDA_P024] (i) Fluctuation of Raw Material Prices

[SECTION: Risk and Outlook]

[MDA_P019]
[MDA_P025] The fluctuation of raw materials’ prices will have an impact on the Group’s profitability. The Group has taken steps in reducing the risk of price fluctuation by negotiating better terms with suppliers. Under the supervision of the Directors, Procurement Department monitors the prices closely and assures that the quality of raw materials is not compromised in line with lower purchase price.

[SECTION: Risk and Outlook]

[MDA_P020]
[MDA_P026] (ii) Fluctuation of Foreign Currency Exchange Rate

[SECTION: Risk and Outlook]

[MDA_P021]
[MDA_P027] Significant fluctuation of foreign currency exchange rate will impact the Group’s cash flow movement and profitability. The movement of the foreign currency exchange rate is closely monitored by the Group’s management.

[SECTION: Risk and Outlook]

[MDA_P022]
[MDA_P028] (iii) Changes in Consumer’s Preferences

[SECTION: Risk and Outlook]

[MDA_P023]
[MDA_P029] In this new era, consumers tend to consume healthy foods as compared to the olden days. The Group is aware of the shift in consumer preferences and understand that consumers nowadays prefer to consume healthier products with less sugar and sweetness. The Group will assess the risk and consider whether to produce healthier products in the near future to prevent potential loss of health-conscious customers.

[SECTION: Risk and Outlook]

[MDA_P024]
[MDA_P030] (iv) Quality of the Products

[SECTION: Risk and Outlook]

[MDA_P025]
[MDA_P031] Deterioration in quality of the product would bring negative impact to the Group’s public image and reputation. To prevent this from happening, the Group has implemented quality management systems and quality assurance procedures via HALAL and ISO accreditation. The Group also purchases top quality raw materials from reputable and reliable suppliers.

[SECTION: Business Review]

[MDA_P031]
[MDA_P039] In view of inflation on key raw materials’ prices and the volatility of Ringgit Malaysia against foreign currencies, raw materials’ prices are expected to be unstable in the forthcoming year. Coupled with the uncertainties in the global economy due to the Covid-19 pandemic, the Group expects that the forthcoming year will continue to be challenging. In order to maintain its profitability, the Group will focus its efforts to closely monitor the prices of raw materials and to review its business strategies when deem necessary.

[SECTION: Business Review]

[MDA_P032]
[MDA_P040] The Board expects a challenging year for the Group and will adopt a vigilant and prudent stance. The Group will strive to ensure that it continues to achieve satisfactory results by implementing prudent measures and improving operational efficiency so as to sustain the current profit margin without compromising the products’ quality. To improve its stream of revenue, the Group will put more efforts in focusing and enhancing its brands recognition and products’ varieties for new business opportunities.
