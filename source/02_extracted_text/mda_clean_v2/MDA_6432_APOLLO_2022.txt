[MDA_P011] Revenue

[SECTION: Financial Review]

[MDA_P010]
[MDA_P012] The Group registered a turnover of RM188.27 million for the financial year ended 30 April 2022, a decrease of 1.11% as compared to RM190.39 million in previous financial year ended 30 April 2021. 73.88% of the turnover was contributed by local market and there were no material changes on the sales proportion from previous financial year.

[SECTION: Financial Review]

[MDA_P011]
[MDA_P014] The profit after taxation had decreased by 42.50% to RM9.97 million from RM17.34 million as recorded in the previous financial year. The decrease in profit after taxation during the financial year was mainly due to lower gross profit margin as a result of higher raw material costs as compared to previous financial year.

[SECTION: Financial Review]

[MDA_P012]
[MDA_P015] Consequently, the Group’s earnings per share had decreased from 21.67 sen to 12.46 sen.

[SECTION: Risk and Outlook]

[MDA_P018]
[MDA_P024] (i) Fluctuation of Raw Material Prices

[SECTION: Risk and Outlook]

[MDA_P019]
[MDA_P025] The fluctuation of raw materials’ prices will have an impact on the Group’s profitability. The Group has taken steps in reducing the risk of price fluctuation by negotiating better terms with suppliers. Under the supervision of the Directors, Purchasing Department monitors the prices closely and assures that the quality of raw materials is maintained despite the lower purchase price.

[SECTION: Risk and Outlook]

[MDA_P020]

[MDA_P021]
