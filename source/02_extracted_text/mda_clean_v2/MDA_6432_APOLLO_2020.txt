[SECTION: Business Review]

[MDA_P006]

[SECTION: Business Review]

[MDA_P007]
[MDA_P008] The Group firmly believes that quality sells. Holding this belief, the Group strives to ensure that following principles are taken to improve its market position:

[SECTION: Business Review]

[MDA_P008]
[MDA_P009]  Implement and maintain quality management system and continually improve its effectiveness  Produce products with top quality of raw and packaging materials  Use world class wafer and layer cake manufacturing machinery from Europe and constantly upgrade to improve the quality and competitiveness  Enhance customer satisfaction by meeting customer requirements  Recognize customers’ needs by introducing individual packaging  Implement quality assurance procedures such as HALAL and ISO accreditation

[MDA_P011] Revenue

[SECTION: Financial Review]

[MDA_P010]
[MDA_P012] The Group registered a turnover of RM174.93 million for the financial year ended 30 April 2020, a decrease of 7.37% as compared to RM188.84 million in previous financial year ended 30 April 2019. 72% of the turnover was contributed by local market and there were no material changes on the sales proportion from previous financial year.

[SECTION: Financial Review]

[MDA_P011]
[MDA_P014] The profit after taxation had decreased by 18.26% to RM14.24 million from RM17.43 million as recorded in the previous financial year. The current financial year’s profit margin was improved by 1.46% from 24.77% to 26.23%. The lower turnover and other income coupled with a higher administrative expense had resulted in a lower profit.

[SECTION: Financial Review]

[MDA_P012]
[MDA_P015] Consequently, the Group’s earnings per share had decreased from 21.78 sen to 17.81 sen over the financial year.

[SECTION: Risk and Outlook]

[MDA_P017]
[MDA_P022] The Group is currently facing a few risk factors that might affect the Group’s profitability. Those risks include the following:

[SECTION: Risk and Outlook]

[MDA_P018]
[MDA_P023] (i) Fluctuation of Raw Material Prices

[SECTION: Risk and Outlook]

[MDA_P019]
[MDA_P024] The fluctuation of raw materials’ prices will have an impact on the Group’s profitability. The Group has taken steps in reducing the risk of price fluctuation by negotiating better terms with suppliers. Under the supervision of the Directors, Procurement Department monitors the prices closely and assures that the quality of raw materials is not compromised in line with lower purchase price.

[SECTION: Risk and Outlook]

[MDA_P020]
[MDA_P025] (ii) Fluctuation of Foreign Currency Exchange Rate

[SECTION: Risk and Outlook]

[MDA_P021]
[MDA_P026] Significant fluctuation of foreign currency exchange rate will impact the Group’s cash flow movement and profitability. The movement of the foreign currency exchange rate is closely monitored by the Group’s management.

[SECTION: Risk and Outlook]

[MDA_P022]
[MDA_P027] (iii) Changes in Consumer’s Preferences

[SECTION: Risk and Outlook]

[MDA_P023]
[MDA_P028] In this new era, consumers tend to consume healthy foods as compared to the olden days. The Group is aware of the shift in consumer preferences and understand that consumers nowadays prefer to consume healthier products with less sugar and sweetness. The Group will assess the risk and consider whether to produce healthier products in the near future to prevent potential loss of health-conscious customers.

[SECTION: Risk and Outlook]

[MDA_P024]
[MDA_P029] (iv) Quality of the Products

[SECTION: Risk and Outlook]

[MDA_P025]
[MDA_P030] Deterioration in quality of the product would bring negative impact to the Group’s public image and reputation. To prevent this from happening, the Group has implemented quality management systems and quality assurance procedures via HALAL and ISO accreditation. The Group also purchases top quality raw materials from reputable and reliable suppliers.

[MDA_P034] The pre-requisites for the Group to remain competitive in this challenging market environment include continuous improvement in the Group's production planning, application of stringent quality control on the products manufactured. The Group also invests in the newer and modern production machineries.

[SECTION: Business Review]

[MDA_P029]
[MDA_P035] The Group’s management plan to fully automate certain of its production line as its initial step. Currently, the planning is still in progress. If the automation is proven to be efficient, the Group will continue its next step to fully automate the remaining production line. Simultaneously, the Group needs to provide internal training for its staff on technical know-how in using, operating and maintaining the automated machines.

[SECTION: Business Review]

[MDA_P030]
[MDA_P037] After the Covid-19 pandemic outbreak and the Movement Control Order (“MCO”) was imposed by the Malaysia Government starting mid-March 2020 to curb its spread, many commercial activities were haulted. The Covid-19 pandemic affects the operation and business of the Group as it affects the demands of customers.

[SECTION: Business Review]

[MDA_P031]
[MDA_P038] In view of inflation on key raw materials’ prices and the volatility of Ringgit Malaysia against foreign currencies, raw materials’ prices are expected to be unstable in the forthcoming year. Coupled with the uncertainties in the global economy due to the Covid-19 pandemic, the Group expects that the business performance in forthcoming year will become more challenging. In order to maintain its profitability, the Group will focus its efforts to closely monitor the prices of raw materials and to review its business strategies when deem necessary.

[SECTION: Business Review]

[MDA_P032]
[MDA_P039] The Board expects a forthcoming challenging year for the Group and will adopt a vigilant and prudent stance. The Group will strive to ensure that it continues to achieve satisfactory results by implementing prudent measures and improving operational efficiency so as to sustain the current margin without compromising the products’ quality. To improve its stream of revenue, the Group will put more efforts in focusing and enhancing its brands recognition and products’ varieties for new business opportunities.

[SECTION: Dividends]

[MDA_P033]
[MDA_P041] The Board of Directors recommend a first and final dividend of 20 sen under the single tier system (tax exempt) for the financial year ended 30 April 2020. The dividend rate will subject to the shareholders’ approval at the forthcoming Annual General Meeting to be held on 28 October 2020. The dividend will be paid on 12 January 2021 once it is approved.
