The Company has enhanced the sales of imported wine and realised some effect. b) Profit(Loss) after Taxation Loss for FY 2020 was RMB 16.736 million as compared with FY 2019's profit of RMB 0.313 million. The increased in losses was mainly due to the impairment of inventories, which amounted to RMB12.898 million. c) Liquidity and Capital Resources As at FY 2020, China Ouhua had cash and cash equivalent amounting to RMB 8.49 million with no external borrowing.
