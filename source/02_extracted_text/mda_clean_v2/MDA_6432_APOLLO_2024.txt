[MDA_P009]  Implement and maintain quality management system and continually improve its effectiveness;  Produce products using premium raw and packaging materials;  Use world class wafer and layer cake manufacturing machinery from Europe and constantly upgrade to improve the quality of products and competitiveness of the Group;  Enhance customer satisfaction by meeting customer requirements;  Recognise customers’ needs by introducing individual packaging; and  Implement quality assurance procedures such as HALAL, GMP and ISO accreditations.

[MDA_P011] Revenue

[SECTION: Financial Review]

[MDA_P010]
[MDA_P012] The Group recorded revenue of RM255.27 million for the financial year ended 30 April 2024, a decrease of 0.67% as compared to RM257.00 million in the previous financial year ended 30 April 2023. A large proportion of revenue was contributed by local market, accounting for 74.16% of total revenue. There were no material changes on the sales proportion from previous financial year.

[SECTION: Financial Review]

[MDA_P011]
[MDA_P014] The profit after taxation had increased by 71.03% from RM31.65 million as recorded in the previous financial year to RM54.13 million. During the financial year, the Group recorded a one-off gain (net of tax) on the disposal of investment properties of RM18.66 million, accounting for 34.47% of profit after taxation. Excluding the one-off gain, the Group still recorded a commendable 12.08% year-on-year growth in profit after tax on the back of improved gross profit margins.

[SECTION: Financial Review]

[MDA_P012]
[MDA_P015] The Group’s earnings per share increased from 39.56 sen to 67.66 sen for the financial year.

[SECTION: Risk and Outlook]

[MDA_P018]
[MDA_P024] (i) Fluctuation of Raw Material Prices

[SECTION: Risk and Outlook]

[MDA_P019]
[MDA_P025] Any fluctuation of raw material prices will have a direct impact on the Group’s gross profit margin. The Group has taken measures to reduce the risk of price fluctuation by working closely with suppliers to get reasonable price without compromising quality. The Group will monitor the price closely and assures that the quality of raw materials is maintained.

[SECTION: Risk and Outlook]

[MDA_P020]
[MDA_P026] (ii) Fluctuation of Foreign Currency Exchange Rate

[SECTION: Risk and Outlook]

[MDA_P021]
[MDA_P027] Significant fluctuation of foreign currency exchange rate will impact on the Group’s cash flow movement and profitability. The movement of the foreign currency exchange rate is closely monitored by the Group’s management.

[MDA_P022]
[MDA_P028] (iii) Changes in Consumer’s Preferences

[SECTION: Risk and Outlook]

[MDA_P023]

Focus remains on increasing efficiency and leveraging on the Group’s strong brand to develop new export market and customer segments. Additionally, the Group will proactively improve it’s distribution and marketing efforts to achieve deeper market penetration while still maintaining stringent quality control on the Group’s products.

[MDA_P040] We remain cautious on the outlook for financial year ending April 2025 for both domestic and international markets due to uncertainty in global macroeconomics, trade relations, geopolitical events as well as rising inflationary environment in which the Group operates in.

[MDA_P032]
[MDA_P041] Price fluctuation of key raw materials supply chain coupled with the weakened Malaysia Ringgit remains a key challenge for the Group. Having said that, the Group remains committed to maintaining satisfactory results by continuing to enhance operating efficiency and implementing cautious measures without compromising product and service quality.
