Through our wholly-owned subsidiary, San Soon Seng Food Industries Sdn Bhd ("SSSFI"), 3A operates as a leading manufacturer of food and beverage ingredients. Together, as a cohesive entity ("the Group"), 3A and SSSFI remain committed to manufacturing excellence, with a core focus on producing high-quality food and ingredients. Leveraging our collective expertise, the Group has successfully extended our reach beyond the confines of Asia where we now have an established presence on the international stage.
