Operating Environment The Malaysian economy grew moderately in 2023 amid a challenging external environment due to weak exports, geopolitical tensions and tighter monetary policies. Bank Negara projected our economic growth to improve in year 2024 mainly driven by continuous domestic demand expansion and improvement in external demand. There are downside risks to growth arising from weaker-than-expected external demand, escalation of geopolitical conflicts and lower production of commodity due to adverse weather conditions which could impact the cost of our raw materials and energy. Financial Review In the financial year ended 31 March 2024 (“FYE 31.03.2024”), the Company achieved a revenue of RM636.4 million, increased by 5.4% as compared to the revenue of previous financial year of RM603.7 million. Sales volume in the current financial year decreased mainly from AJI-NO-MOTO® against the sales volume in the previous financial year. However, this reduction was compensated by the increase in prices of AJI-NO-MOTO® AND "TENCHO".

[SECTION: Unknown]

SEGMENT INFORMATION Consumer Business Revenue in Consumer Business was RM484.2 million as compared to the revenue of RM462.1 million in the previous financial year. Despite lower sales volume, the revenue in the Consumer Business improved due to better selling price of AJI-NO-MOTO® and foreign exchange gain from export sales. Profit was 43.9 million, 13.3 times of the profit of RM3.3 million in the previous financial year.
