OVERVIEW OF THE GROUP’S BUSINESS AND OBJECTIVES

[MDA_P003]
Apollo Food Holdings Berhad is principally an investment holding company and currently it has two wholly owned
subsidiaries as below:

[MDA_P004]
(i) Apollo Food Industries (M) Sdn Bhd is principally engaged in manufacturing and trading in compound
chocolates, chocolate confectionery products and layer cakes in overseas markets.

[MDA_P005]
(ii) Hap Huat Food Industries Sdn Bhd is principally engaged in distributing and marketing of compound
chocolates, chocolate confectionery products and layer cakes in local market.

[MDA_P006]
Over the years, the Group has established itself as one of the leading manufacturer of chocolate confectionery
products and layer cakes in Malaysia.

[MDA_P007]
The aim of the Group is to always fulfil the customer needs and requirements by using the latest equipment and
technology. In order to achieve the aim, the Group uses only the finest natural ingredients sourced from a select
panel of suppliers and employs state-of-art technology in manufacturing its products.

[MDA_P008]
The Group firmly believes that quality sells. Holding this belief, the Group strives to ensure that following principles
are taken to improve its market position:

[MDA_P009]
•
Implement and maintain quality management system and continually improve its effectiveness
•
Produce products with top quality of raw and packaging materials
•
Use world class wafer and layer cake manufacturing machinery from Europe and constantly upgrade to improve
the quality and competitiveness
•
Enhance customer satisfaction by meeting customer requirements
•
Recognize customers’ needs by introducing individual packaging
•
Implement quality assurance procedures such as HALAL and ISO accreditation

FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE

[MDA_P011]
Revenue

[MDA_P012]
The Group registered a turnover of RM188.27 million for the financial year ended 30 April 2022, a decrease of 1.11%
as compared to RM190.39 million in previous financial year ended 30 April 2021. 73.88% of the turnover was
contributed by local market and there were no material changes on the sales proportion from previous financial
year.

[MDA_P013]
Profit for the Financial Year

[MDA_P014]
The profit after taxation had decreased by 42.50% to RM9.97 million from RM17.34 million as recorded in the
previous financial year. The decrease in profit after taxation during the financial year was mainly due to lower gross
profit margin as a result of higher raw material costs as compared to previous financial year.

[MDA_P015]
Consequently, the Group’s earnings per share had decreased from 21.67 sen to 12.46 sen.

RISK OVERVIEW

[MDA_P023]
The Group is currently facing a few risk factors that might affect the Group’s profitability. Those risks include the
following:

[MDA_P024]
(i) Fluctuation of Raw Material Prices

[MDA_P025]
The fluctuation of raw materials’ prices will have an impact on the Group’s profitability. The Group has taken steps
in reducing the risk of price fluctuation by negotiating better terms with suppliers. Under the supervision of the
Directors, Purchasing Department monitors the prices closely and assures that the quality of raw materials is
maintained despite the lower purchase price.

[MDA_P026]
(ii) Fluctuation of Foreign Currency Exchange Rate

[MDA_P027]
Significant fluctuation of foreign currency exchange rate will impact the Group’s cash flow movement and
profitability. The movement of the foreign currency exchange rate is closely monitored by the Group’s management.

[MDA_P028]
(iii) Changes in Consumer’s Preferences

[MDA_P029]
Consumers tend to consume healthy food as compared to the past. The Group is aware of the shift in consumer
preferences and will consider producing products to meet the requirement of such health-conscious customers.

[MDA_P030]
(iv) Quality of the Products

[MDA_P031]
The Group has implemented quality management systems and quality assurance procedures via HALAL and ISO
accreditation and sources raw materials from reputable and reliable suppliers to maintain the quality of the product.

OPERATION REVIEW

[MDA_P035]
The pre-requisites for the Group to remain competitive in this challenging market environment include continuous
improvement in the Group's production planning, application of stringent quality control on the products
manufactured.

[MDA_P036]
The Group is continuously monitoring and upgrading its production lines for better efficiency.

[MDA_P037]
BUSINESS PROSPECT

[MDA_P038]
Despite the political unrest in certain regions and the slowing of growth in the emerging markets, the markets in
which the Group operates remain relatively stable.

[MDA_P039]
With the stabilisation of key raw materials supply chain and prices, the revenue and results of the Group are
expected to improve in the forthcoming financial year.

[MDA_P040]
The Group will strive to ensure that it continues to achieve satisfactory results by implementing prudent measures
and improving operational efficiency so as to sustain the profit margin without compromising the product quality.

[MDA_P041]
DIVIDEND POLICY

[MDA_P042]
The Company does not have a fixed dividend policy. The Board of Directors are pleased to recommend a First and
Final Dividend of 15 sen under the single tier system (tax exempt) for the financial year ended 30 April 2022. The
dividend rate will subject to the shareholders’ approval at the forthcoming Annual General Meeting to be held on
27 October 2022. The dividend will be paid on 11 January 2023 once it is approved.
