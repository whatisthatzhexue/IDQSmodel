[TITLE]
Body of second drowning victim in Dungun found

[LEAD]
DUNGUN: The body of Mohd Fazir Shafie, 37, the second victim of a drowning incident at Pantai Sura Tengah near here, was found last night.

[NEWS_P001]
DUNGUN: The body of Mohd Fazir Shafie, 37, the second victim of a drowning incident at Pantai Sura Tengah near here, was found last night.

[NEWS_P002]
Dungun police chief, Supt Maizura Abdul Kadir, said rescuers discovered his body at 12.35am, about a kilometre from the location where he was last seen.

[NEWS_P003]
"His body was sent to Sultanah Nur Zahirah Hospital in Kuala Terengganu for an autopsy, " he said when contacted.

[NEWS_P004]
The incident occurred at 10:27 am yesterday when Mohd Fazir attempted to rescue his sister, 39-year-old Nor Baizura, from drowning. Both were swept away by strong currents.

[NEWS_P005]
Nor Baizura’s body was recovered at 10.54am yesterday.

[NEWS_P006]
The siblings had returned to their village for the holidays and were having a picnic with 13 family members.

[NEWS_P007]
Nor Baizura, a housewife, lived in Johor Baru, while Mohd Fazir worked as a lorry driver in Kuantan. - Bernama
