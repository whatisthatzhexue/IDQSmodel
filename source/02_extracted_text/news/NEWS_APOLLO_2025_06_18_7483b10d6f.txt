[NEWS_P001] PETALING JAYA: Apollo Food Holdings Bhd aims to continue the rationalisation of its manufacturing facilities and the execution of its capacity expansion plans in the financial year ending April 30, 2026 (FY26). In the fourth quarter ended March 30, 2025 (4Q25), Apollo’s net profit jumped 50.1% to RM9.4mil, or 11.82 sen per share, bringing FY25 profit to RM40.2mil or 50.22 sen per share. Revenue in 4Q25 rose 28.1% to RM73.3mil, bringing its full year revenue to a record of RM298.4mil.

[NEWS_P002] Apollo Food said that excluding a non-operational gain of RM18.6mil from the disposal of properties in the previous financial year, its FY25 net profit rose 13% year-on-year, marking the highest operational net profit in the group’s history. The company declared a second interim dividend of 20 sen per share payable on July 23.
