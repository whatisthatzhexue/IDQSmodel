[NEWS_P001] KUALA LUMPUR: The FBM KLCI advanced at midday, supported by persistent buying in heavyweight stocks while tracking its regional peers. At lunch break, the FBM KLCI rose 7.95 points, or 0.49% to 1,642.86 after hitting an intra-morning high of 1,644.44. In the broader market, gainers outnumbered losers with 485 stocks ending higher and 424 stocks finishing lower. Volume was 1.5 billion shares valued at RM982.3mil. Among the gainers, Nestle rose 40 sen to RM105.40, Allianz added 18 sen to RM20.34, Kuchai Development gained 17 sen to RM2.16 and Hong Leong Financial Group climbed 16 sen to RM19.28.

[NEWS_P002] Malaysian Pacific Industries lost 12 sen to RM26.30, United Plantations slid eight sen to RM26.02, HeiTech Padu fell eight sen to RM4.16 and Gamuda declined eight sen to RM8.10. Pertama Digital rebounded after several days of downtrend. The counter jumped 30.61%, or 7.5 sen to 32 sen with 86.39 million shares done. Among the banks, CIMB rose 12 sen to RM8.15, Maybank added four sen to RM10.54 and RHB Bank gained three sen to RM6.23. Public Bank has suspended from Oct 9 pending the release of a material announcement.

[NEWS_P003] Malacca Securities expects investors to position themselves ahead of Budget 2025 by trading in e-invoicing, construction, property, building materials, utilities, and consumer-related stocks. “Also, the ongoing rally in crude palm oil prices may lead to a positive trading sentiment in plantation stocks. Moreover, with the ringgit showing a weakening bias compared to last week, sentiment could shift toward export-related stocks,” it added. Meanwhile, TA Securities said the local market is likely to remain in consolidation due to weak buying momentum and the absence of strong domestic catalysts, as investors anticipate key U.S.

[NEWS_P004] inflation data and major bank earnings. The research house noted that the immediate index support remains at 1,625, with stronger support levels at 1,620 and 1,600. “Immediate resistance remains at 1,660, followed by the recent highs of 1,675 and 1,684, and then 1,695, Dec 2020 high, as tougher resistance levels,” it added.
