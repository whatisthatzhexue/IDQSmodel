[NEWS_P001] KUALA LUMPUR: Bursa Malaysia finished higher today as bargain-hunting activity increased following six consecutive sessions of decline. At closing, the KLCI was down 4.61 points, or 0.29%, to 1,615.08. The market traded within a range of 9.35 points, with an intra-day high of 1,619.05 and a low of 1,609.70 during the session. Winners and losers were closely balanced, with 490 gainers against 497 losers and 511 that were flat. About 2.48 billion shares, valued at RM2.26bil, changed hands.

[NEWS_P002] Among the gainers, BLD Plantation jumped 52 sen to RM11.24, Sunmow rose 35 sen to RM1.65, Malaysian Pacific Industries added 24 sen to RM26.46 and Bursa Malaysia gained 22 sen to RM8.91. Nestle plunged RM3 to RM100, F&N lost 32 sen to RM30.90, Hong Leong Industries fell 20 sen to RM13.40 and Dutch Lady declined 18 sen to RM30.20. ACE Market debutant OB Holdings added one sen, or 4.17% to 25 sen with 63.9 million shares traded. Meanwhile, data from Bursa Malaysia shows that foreign investors continued selling equities on Monday, with a total disposal of RM118 mil.

[NEWS_P003] Local institutions and retailers acquired equities worth RM88mil and RM30mil, respectively. On the forex market, the ringgit was down 0.21% against the US dollar to 4.3715. The local currency has depreciated by 0.1% against the Singapore dollar at 3.3021 and down by 0.32% against the pound sterling at 5.6771. US West Texas Intermediate crude rose 46 cents to US$67.84 and Brent added 44 cents to US$71.86 per barrel. Elsewhere in the region, MSCI's Asia ex-Japan stock index was lower by 0.13%.

[NEWS_P004] Japan’s Nikkei 225 rose 0.77% to 38,903.68 while South Korea’s Kospi closed up 0.21% at 2,617.80. Hong Kong’s Hang Seng index was up 0.5% at 20,701.14. China’s CSI300 Index closed down 1% to 3,924.65 while the Shanghai Composite Index fell 1.08% to 3,286.41.
