MARKET OVERVIEW
Despite global concerns about the economic
impact of the Russia-Ukraine conflict, the
global demand for chocolate remained
strong, as demonstrated by the increase in
grinding worldwide. According to regional
cocoa associations, total grinding in FY2022
rose to 3.5 million MT from 3.4 million MT in
the previous year. The reopening of global borders, increased air travel,
and tourism also contributed to the growth in chocolate
consumption in FY2022. The chocolate market is expected
to continue its growth trend, with Euromonitor International
forecasting it to reach a global retail sales value of $128
billion by the end of 2023, with a cumulative average
growth rate of 1.9% in volume over the next three years to
2025. There are, however, various macroeconomic factors that
created a challenging market environment for GCB in
2022. The European and UK region has experienced rising
energy costs, which caused by the energy crisis as a result
of Russia-Ukraine conflict. Global inflationary pressure have been causing interest
rate hike. The company foresees that interest rates may
continue to rise due to the uncertain economic outlook.

OUTLOOK
While the Group cannot control external factors, we
will strive to navigate through the headwinds to ensure
that our operations remain focused on seizing business
opportunities, meeting customer demand, and generating
sustainable profits to support our growth objectives. Côte d'Ivoire presence brings us ‘close to source’ and
‘close to market’
GCB's expansion into Côte d'Ivoire, the world's largest
cocoa bean supplying country, has brought us closer to
the source of cocoa beans and enables us to implement
sustainability projects commencing from the procurement
of cocoa beans. This expansion into a strategic location
will improved our cost-effectiveness in reaching out to
key chocolate-consuming countries in Europe and the UK,
where most of our customers are based. The new facility in
Côte d'Ivoire will be a boost for the Group's earnings and
will catapult our strength as a global player in the chocolate
supply chain.
