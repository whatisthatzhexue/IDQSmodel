The Consumer Foods Division operates as subsidiaries under Ibufood Corporation Sdn. Bhd. (“Ibufood Group”). The Ibufood Group is a major producer of premium quality instant noodles, mayonnaise, seasoning-powder,
seasoning-oil, sauces and food ingredients. It is represented by several brand names. IBUMIE is the highly recognisable brand for its instant noodles which
comes in several different flavours. The TELLY brand represents the food seasoning segment offering premium quality blended seasoning-powder,
seasoning-oil, sauces, mayonnaise, specialty spices, soup, soup stocks, and marinades. The TELLY mayonnaise
has grown over the years to become one of the market leaders in the Food Service sector.

The Group recorded a revenue of RM298.6 million and a profit before taxation of RM12.7 million in the FYE 2023 as
compared to a revenue of RM254.9 million and a loss before taxation of RM2.0 million reported in the immediate
preceding financial year. Both Consumer Foods and Bedding Products Divisions reported higher sales of 27% and 3% respectively for
FYE 2023.
[MDA_P007]
The higher sales from Consumer Foods Division was mainly due to the higher demand for its products
especially noodles, mayonnaise and creamers. Sales of its noodles, mayonnaise and creamers in FYE 2023
increased to RM95.9 million (FYE 2022: RM60.7 million), RM46.7 million (FYE 2022: RM42.5 million) and RM55.9
million (FYE 2022: RM52.2 million) respectively. The increase in noodles sales was mainly due to the newly-
secured OEM business. The higher sales performance from the Bedding Products Division resulted from increase in activities from the local projects
scene. Domestic project sales increased to RM18.3 million in the FYE 2023 from RM13.6 million in the FYE 2022. Building Materials Division reported a drop in sales by 1%. Sales for retail segment decreased by 54% to RM2.5 million
from RM5.5 million in the immediate preceding year. The drop in retail sales was mainly due to the discontinuation
of retail sales from one of its agency brands. The Division also wind down its lock-set and ironmongery business as
part of its down-sizing strategy.
[MDA_P008]
However, its project sales increased by 10% to RM27.8 million in the FYE 2023 from
RM25.2 million previously, mainly due to increasing activities in the construction industry.

REVIEW OF THE FINANCIAL RESULTS AND FINANCIAL CONDITIONS (cont’d)
The Group reported a profit before taxation of RM12.7 million for FYE 2023 as opposed to a loss before taxation
of RM2.0 million in the FYE 2022 because of higher profit reported from Consumer Foods and Bedding Products
Divisions. The Consumer Foods Division posted a profit before taxation of RM11.7 million for FYE 2023 as compared to a
loss before taxation of RM2.2 million in the FYE 2022 mainly due to higher products margin because of increase
in selling price and stabilization of raw materials cost during the year. The main raw materials price such as flour,
palm oil and soy bean oil have decreased in the range of 13% to 24% during the year. The Bedding Products Division reported a profit before taxation of RM6.9 million for FYE 2023 as against a profit
before taxation of RM5.5 million in the FYE 2022 because of reopening of economy after Covid-19 restriction. Domestic projects have seen an uptake in activities after the long lockdown. Project sales has increased to RM18.3
million in the FYE 2023 as compared to RM13.6 million reported in the immediate preceding year.

sales dropped 4% to RM50.1 million as compared to RM52.1 million in the FYE 2022. Building Materials Division posted a higher loss before taxation of RM4.8 million in the FYE 2023 as against to a
