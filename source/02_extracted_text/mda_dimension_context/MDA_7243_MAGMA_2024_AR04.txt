ended 31 December 2023 (“FYE2023”). This growth reflects the Group’s continued efforts to strengthen operational
efficiency and adapt to evolving market conditions. Gross Profit (“GP”) declined by 0.9% to RM16.39 million (FYE2023: RM16.54 million). However, the Gross Profit Margin
(“GPM”) contracting from 114.4% to 109.8%, primarily due to higher input and operating costs, driven by inflation and
industry-wide cost pressures. Administrative expenses increased significantly by 75.7%, reaching RM21.83 million (FYE2023: RM12.42 million). This
rise was mainly due to strategic restructuring initiatives, increased staffing costs, and inflation-related adjustments.
