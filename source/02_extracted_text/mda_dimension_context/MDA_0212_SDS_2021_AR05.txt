Dear valued shareholders, it is with great pleasure that we present to you the Annual
Report and Audited Financial Statements of SDS Group Berhad (“SDS Group” or
the “Group”) for the financial year ended 31 March 2021 (“FY2021”). OVERVIEW OF THE GROUP’S BUSINESS
Business Venture and Journey
SDS Group has embarked on its business as a manufacturer and retailer of bakery products in 1984. SDS
Group continues to develop varieties of new creative products to build customers’ excitement and interest in
its products in the market till today. The Group strives to enrich and satisfy customers’ needs and preferences
through its high-quality products at a reasonable price. It was an astonishing achievement and business journey for SDS Group as it started its home-grown
business in Johor region with three subsidiary brands namely, retail brand, ‘SDS’, and wholesale brands,
‘Top Baker’ and ‘Daily’s’. Throughout the years, SDS Group has grown rapidly through business expansion
of its manufacturing and distribution facilities, and retail outlets in the Food and Beverages (F&B) industry.

KEY RISK FACTORS
The Group is cognisant of the importance that the investors and shareholders need to understand the types of risks that it is exposed
to and how these risks may impact the Group’s business and its operating performance during this pandemic period. The Management is consistently identifying anticipated or known risks that the Group is exposed to in which these risks will have a
material effect on the Group’s operations, performance, financial condition and liquidity. The Group’s risk management framework
covers risk identification, assessment and control activities such as monitoring, analysis, communication, internal control, action and
strategies. A continuous discussion of the plans or strategies is in place to make sure actions are taken to mitigate such risks.
[MDA_P037]
The Group is confident that with the effective
implementation of sound business plans and strategies, the Group will emerge
stronger in its industry to withstand any vulnerability. For retail outlets, the Group
will continue to expand in the central region by setting up new outlets. The Group
is also upgrading its online store and platform to offer a wide range of products to

continue to expand its geographical footprint and logistic fleets across Malaysia
