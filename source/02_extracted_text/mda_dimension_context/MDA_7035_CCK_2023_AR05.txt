export markets. PT Bonanza’s key products are frozen raw shrimp, frozen cooked shrimp and frozen Nobashi Ebi
shrimp. As evidenced by the performance of the prawn segment in FY2023, the acquisition has augmented our seafood
business and increased the size and scale of our export-oriented prawn products. Specifically, the prawn segment
benefitted from a full year of contribution from PT Bonanza which boosted export volumes to Japan, Taiwan. Thailand
and The People’s Republic of China. Manufacturing operations in Indonesia continued to benefit from an overall increase in production capacity due to the
addition of Pontianak’s facility that was commissioned in January 2021.
