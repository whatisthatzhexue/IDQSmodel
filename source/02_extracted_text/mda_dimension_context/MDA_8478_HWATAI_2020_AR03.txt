ANNUAL REPORT 2020 I HWA TAI INDUSTRIES BERHAD
FINANCIAL AND OPERATIONS REVIEWS (Continued)
There was a slight increase in the Group’s gross profit margin at approximately 30% in FYE 2020 as compared with
29% in FYE2019. The slight increase of margin was mainly due to no major fluctuation prices of sugar, flour and
packaging materials, which are major cost components for our products. Moreover, there was no major movement
of foreign currency exchange (FOREX) on our export sales which mainly transacted in US Dollar in FYE2020 as
compared to FYE2019. The Group had incurred total expenses of RM21.45 million in FYE2020, almost on par as compared with RM21.80
million in FYE2019, a marginal decrease of 1.63%. The expenses were mainly from advertising and promotional
expenses, staff cost and impairment loss on trade receivable. These expenses were also formed partly from selling
and distribution expenses, administration expenses and other expenses.
[MDA_P003]
Other income of RM0.50 million in FYE2020 and RM0.94 million in FYE2019 was primarily derived from reversal of
impairment loss for associate and interests earned on fixed deposits placed at financial institutions for both of the
financial years. The Group had fully provided the impairment loss on the remaining cost of investment in our China associated
company, Shan Dong Yingerle Hwa Tai Food Industry Co. Ltd in financial year ended 31 December 2018 (“FYE2018”). The impairment loss arose from prolonged decline in value of investment and significant financial difficulty in the
associated company. Thus, there will be no material impact on the Group’s results irrespective of the performance
of the associate neither in FYE2020 or coming years. Should there be any evidence which indicates that the
performance of the associated company is or will be better than expected, the Group will expect a write back of
these impairments.
[MDA_P004]
In compliance with the disclosure of Malaysian Financial Reporting Standard, a share of loss of RM344,000 in this
associate was recorded and the impact was mitigated by a reversal of partial impairment loss of RM342,000 for
this associate in FYE2020 which the impairment loss for its remaining cost of investments was fully provided in
FYE2018. FINANCIAL POSITION

so as to keep up with technological changes where continuous capital expenditure investments are vital to improve
efficiency and productivity of our factory. BUSINESS STRATEGICS AND RISK FACTORS
In view of market competition and ever-growing demands from consumers, our Research and Development
division has continuously focused on product innovation, creativeness on developing healthier choices products
for consumers, market research in improving our current products to suit consumers preferences without
compromising on quality. We have been continuously expanding our customer network by penetration to new markets locally and
internationally, developing and launching new products and also improve our product quality and variety. In line
with the new norm and e-commerce phenomenon, the Group also is working on various online shopping initiatives
to drive sales. Apart from the above, the Group has continuously invested in factory upgrading and machineries to improve the
production efficiency for cost savings.
[MDA_P008]
Our factory has been renovated and designed according to food manufacturing
standards in order to reduce the risk of contamination and facilitate the production of safe finished products. Old factory roof system is removed and reinstalled with new insulated roof system for better manufacturing &
warehousing environment. The project of conversion of LPG (Liquefied Petroleum Gas) to Natural Gas, as a source
of burning energy for the production plant which will result in cost savings by approximately 50% was completed
in FYE2018. As a player in the food industry, the Group is exposed to the risk associated with product quality and standard
as product safety and quality are of paramount importance to the Group. The risk may have financial impact as
well as reputation impact. Comprehensive policies and procedures established on processing, plant & machinery
sanitization as well as employee personal hygiene to prevent contamination. Also the products traceability and
compliance with stringent quality system are in place too. STATEMENT OF MANAGEMENT DISCUSSION & ANALYSIS
