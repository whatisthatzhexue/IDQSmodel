[MDA_P012]
The Group registered a turnover of RM190.39 million for the financial year ended 30 April 2021, an increase of 8.84%

contributed by local market and there were no material changes on the sales proportion from previous financial
year. During the financial year, the increase of sales demand in local market has improved the overall turnover of
the Group.
[MDA_P013]
Profit for the Financial Year
[MDA_P014]

previous financial year. The increase in profit after taxation during the financial year was mainly due to reversal of
impairment loss on Property, Plant and Equipment and fair value gain on other investments.

Consequently, the Group’s earnings per share had increased from 17.81 sen to 21.67 sen over the financial year.

RISK OVERVIEW
[MDA_P026]
(ii) Fluctuation of Foreign Currency Exchange Rate
[MDA_P027]
Significant fluctuation of foreign currency exchange rate will impact the Group’s cash flow movement and
profitability. The movement of the foreign currency exchange rate is closely monitored by the Group’s management.
[MDA_P028]
(iii) Changes in Consumer’s Preferences
[MDA_P039]
In view of inflation on key raw materials’ prices and the volatility of Ringgit Malaysia against foreign currencies, raw
materials’ prices are expected to be unstable in the forthcoming year. Coupled with the uncertainties in the global
economy due to the Covid-19 pandemic, the Group expects that the forthcoming year will continue to be
challenging. In order to maintain its profitability, the Group will focus its efforts to closely monitor the prices of raw
materials and to review its business strategies when deem necessary.
[MDA_P040]
The Board expects a challenging year for the Group and will adopt a vigilant and prudent stance. The Group will
strive to ensure that it continues to achieve satisfactory results by implementing prudent measures and improving
operational efficiency so as to sustain the current profit margin without compromising the products’ quality. To
improve its stream of revenue, the Group will put more efforts in focusing and enhancing its brands recognition and
products’ varieties for new business opportunities.

DIVIDEND POLICY
[MDA_P042]
The Company has not formalised a dividend policy. During the financial year, the Board of Directors approved a First
Interim Dividend of 10 sen per ordinary share under the single tier system (tax exempt) for the financial year ending
30 April 2021 that was paid on 8 January 2021.
[MDA_P043]
The Board of Directors are pleased to recommend a Final Dividend of 25 sen under the single tier system (tax
exempt) for the financial year ended 30 April 2021. The dividend rate will subject to the shareholders’ approval at
