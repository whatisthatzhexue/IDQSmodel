Statement on Management Discussion and Analysis (“MD&A”) provides an insight of the financial performance
of Able Global Berhad (“AGB”) (Formerly known as Johore Tin Berhad) and its subsidiaries (“the Group”) for
the financial year ended 31 December 2021 (“FYE 2021”). This MD&A also contains information on the Group’s
overview of business and operations, current financial position, reviewing of operating activities, assessing the
operational risks faced by the Group and discussing the future prospect of the Group.
1.
OVERVIEW OF BUSINESS AND OPERATIONS
A.
Business and Operations
[MDA_P013]
For F&B segment, the profit before tax decreased by RM13.17 million to RM28.09 million from RM41.80
million is in line with the decrease in revenue and due to the increase in cost of goods sold in FYE 2021.
Furthermore, the F&B segment shared the losses of equity accounted joint ventures amount to RM3.86
million in FYE 2021 as both joint ventures commenced the operations in Quarter 3 of FYE 2021.
[MDA_P026]
Another factor that affects the operating activities of F&B segment is freight cost. The ongoing pandemic-
related delays and closures of ports lead to higher freight costs and transit time volatile. The subsidiaries
of F&B segment experience higher freight cost and delay in shipments. As there is no certainty when this
situation is expected to normalise, the subsidiaries in F&B segment are trying to cope with the situation by
engaging multiple shipping agents in order to get the best quote and the earliest possible delivery date to
suit the customer’s requirement.
4.
REVIEW OF OPERATIONAL RISKS
[MDA_P039]
The Group’s continued success will depend upon, to a certain extent, the skills, experiences, abilities
and continued efforts of the key management personnel. The loss of key management personnel in the
Group may have an adverse impact on the performance of the Group.
