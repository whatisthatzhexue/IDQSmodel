a) Revenue
For FY 2023, China Ouhua, which is principally involved in importing and sales of grape wines with its sole
manufacturing plant located in Yantai City, Shandong Province, China recorded an increase in revenue of
6.74% or RMB 0.985 million from RMB 14.619 million in FY 2022 to RMB 15.604million in current financial
year. Principal target market of China Ouhua's wine products is throughout China. For FY 2023, the sales
recorded was solely through sales of red wine. Given that the wine market in China remains highly competitive
and none of red wine and white wine was favoured by the consumers as before , Management had made
many efforts with the objective to improve the sales revenue.
