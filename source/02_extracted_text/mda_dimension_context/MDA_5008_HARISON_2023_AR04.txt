Strategies and Future Profit Growth
The distribution business of our Fast-Moving Consumer Goods becomes more challenging in days ahead
as more distribution players emerge to compete in the East Malaysia market. We will continue to focus on
serving our customers better by improving staff competencies to ensure high service standards are offered
to our customers and principals. We will also focus and strategise to secure new agencies and brands to
complement our diversified agencies portfolio. We have completed our Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) to manage and integrate important parts of
our business for Sabah successfully in FY2022. We have commenced implementing our ERP systems in
Sarawak in FY2023 and expects to roll out in FY2024. This technology upgrades will enhance efficiencies
in our operations to serve our customers and principals effectively. Prospects
We expect slow global growth, price of goods to increase and local affordability to impact the domestic
economy in 2024. We also appreciate that the Fast-Moving Consumer Products is more resilient and less
impacted by moderate price increase.
