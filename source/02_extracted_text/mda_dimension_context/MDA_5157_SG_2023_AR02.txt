The loss in EBITDA of RM1.4 million was mainly due to the increase in labour costs and electricity costs in FYE 2023 by RM2.2 million as
compared to EBITDA of RM1.2 million in FYE 2022. However, the impact of costs increase was mitigated by continuing cost reduction
programme implemented. Furthermore, this loss also impacted by the fair value loss of other investments amounting to RM4.8 million in
total. The loss is not incurred any outflow of actual fund. The actual EBITDA excluded this fair value loss on other investments should be
RM3.4 million as compare to of RM8.5 million in FYE 2022.
[MDA_P005]
Therefore, due to the above recognition of fair value loss, for FYE 2023, the Group recorded LAT of RM7.4 million as compared to LAT of
RM4.4 million in FYE 2022. The increase in LAT mainly due increase in labour costs due to implementation of minimum wages, increase in
utilities charges and fair value loss charge out for other investments in FY 2023. However, the increase in LAT was mitigated by the effort of
the management team which continues to implement cost efficiency and reduction programmes and the good strategy by management
to continued concentrate on the FPP instead of HORECA in FYE 2023. Even though the Group recorded LAT of RM7.4 million during FYE 2023, the Group was still able to generated net operating cash inflow of
RM20.4 million that is further discussed in the analysis on cash flow. Analysis of Expenses
Our Group’s COGS increased by RM3.2 million or 4.09% from RM79.1 million in FYE 2022 to RM82.3 million in FYE 2023. The reason for the
increase in COGS was mainly due increase in labour costs and utilities cost as discuss in above. Furthermore, this also impact GP margin
reduce to 13.9% in FY2023 as compared with 18.4% in FY2022.
