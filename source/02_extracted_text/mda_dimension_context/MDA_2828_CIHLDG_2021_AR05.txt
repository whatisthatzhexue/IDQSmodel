Movement Control Order (MCO 2.0 and MCO 3.0) and subsequent Phase One of National Recovery Plan were fully enforced
since Q3FY21. This has dampened many domestic economic activities. The Group’s Tapware and Sanitary ware Division (“DOE Group”) was not allowed to operate or did not operate at full capacity

Operating Activities
