MANAGEMENT DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS
Overview of Business and Objectives
HB Global Limited (“HBGL”) is principally an investment
holding company and currently the Group has one
active wholly owned subsidiary as below:
100% owned Subsidiary:
(i) Shandong Hengbao Foodstuffs Co., Ltd. (“SHF”) –
the main operating plant situated in China
Our Group specializes in the processing, packaging,
and production of a wide array of ready-to-serve
foods, frozen vegetables, and other culinary products
through our subsidiaries. Over the years, we have
established ourselves as a leading gourmet convenient
food specialist in China, equipped with advanced
food processing technologies such as cleaning,
slicing/cutting, blanching, boiling, frying, vacuum
frying, steaming, smoking, stewing, and barbequing. We produce a diverse range of food materials and
quick-frozen products, primarily catering to the needs
of our customers and distributors. Additionally, we
operate as an original equipment manufacturer (OEM)
for third-party brands.
[MDA_P002]
Our products are marketed
both under our customers’ brands and our proprietary
brands, including “Geleifu” (Green Food), “Hengbao
Food,” “Qing Long Tan” (Asparagus Tea), and “The
Four Seasons Farm”. Our product portfolio includes over 1,000 items, which
are exported to more than 20 countries, including
Japan, the USA, Singapore, Australia, and Korea. Our
products are accessible to a variety of users, from
hypermarkets and tourist markets to food stalls and
residential homes. At the heart of our operations is the principle of
providing high-quality, affordable food products
with unique flavors. Our commitment to technical
excellence and stringent quality control standards
has solidified our reputation as a premier food
manufacturer. We maintain a comprehensive quality
monitoring and control system, and our facilities are
certified with international standards such as ISO 9001,
ISO 22000, HACCP, FDA, and BRC. The Group believes that maintaining high quality is
crucial for attracting and retaining customers. To
support this belief, we are dedicated to enhancing
the quality of our products, services, and processes,
adopting industry best practices, and ensuring
customer satisfaction.
[MDA_P003]
Recognizing that customer
preferences evolve, we continuously strive to monitor
and refine our offerings to meet their changing needs. By prioritizing quality, the Group aims to fortify its
market position and cultivate enduring relationships
with its customers. Operation Overview
HBGL Group’s revenue had increased by RMB3.9
million or approximately 4.9% from RMB76.1 million in
financial year ended 31 December 2022 (“FYE2022”) to
RMB80.0 million in financial year ended 31 December
2023 (“FYE2023”). The increase was mainly due to
increase in sales of China subsidiary as during FYE2023,
there was recovery from disruption in the production
and supply chain due to lockdown in certain areas of
China which have been affected with rising Covid 19
cases. The Group had a decrease in net loss before
taxation to RMB47.9 million in FYE2023 as compared to
RMB171.0 million in FYE2022. Dear Valued Shareholders,

MANAGEMENT DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS
(cont’d)
In order to maintain competitiveness in the current
market conditions, it is essential for the Group to
focus on improving its production planning and
implementing rigorous quality control measures on
its manufactured products. The Group is continuously
monitoring and enhancing its production lines to
ensure optimal efficiency. HBGL
expenses
are
classified
into
five
main
components: costs of sales, selling and distribution
expenses, administrative expenses, other expenses,
and finance costs. In the year under review, costs of
sales constituted the largest portion of the Group’s
expenditure, accounting for 54.9% of total expenses. Given the potential risks posed by currency fluctuations
and changing consumer behavior, HBGL has put in
place effective contingency plans to mitigate such
risks. To minimize costs, management has implemented
a tight control operating model, while also leveraging
technology to enhance operational efficiency.
