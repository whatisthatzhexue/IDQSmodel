MANAGEMENT DISCUSSION
AND ANALYSIS
Dear valued shareholders, it is with great pleasure that we present to you the Annual
Report and Audited Financial Statements of SDS Group Berhad (“SDS Group” or
the “Group”) for the financial year ended 31 March 2021 (“FY2021”). OVERVIEW OF THE GROUP’S BUSINESS
Business Venture and Journey
SDS Group has embarked on its business as a manufacturer and retailer of bakery products in 1984. SDS
Group continues to develop varieties of new creative products to build customers’ excitement and interest in
its products in the market till today. The Group strives to enrich and satisfy customers’ needs and preferences
through its high-quality products at a reasonable price. It was an astonishing achievement and business journey for SDS Group as it started its home-grown
business in Johor region with three subsidiary brands namely, retail brand, ‘SDS’, and wholesale brands,
‘Top Baker’ and ‘Daily’s’. Throughout the years, SDS Group has grown rapidly through business expansion
of its manufacturing and distribution facilities, and retail outlets in the Food and Beverages (F&B) industry.
[MDA_P002]
Thereafter, the Group has successfully listed on the ACE Market of Bursa Malaysia Securities Berhad on
7 October 2019. The Group provides an exceptional in-store experience by offering bakery and confectionary products which
are mostly sold in a ‘grab-and-go’ manner and a wide variety of F&B choices ranging from international
cuisines such as western and Japanese food to local cuisine. In addition, SDS Group also offers festive
cookies, hampers, mooncakes, baby full-month and wedding gift set to cater to customers’ needs and
preferences during the festive seasons. The Group’s venture into the wholesale trade of bakery products
further enables it to reach out to its customers through a wider sales channel such as convenience stores
and hypermarkets. Today, the Group strives to remain buoyant in its competitive market especially during this pandemic period. The Group aims to expand its geographical footprint particularly in the Central and Northern Region of
Peninsular Malaysia. Being one of the market leaders in the F&B industry, the Group continues to develop
business strategies to market its products and expand its operation and presence throughout the Country
and overseas.
