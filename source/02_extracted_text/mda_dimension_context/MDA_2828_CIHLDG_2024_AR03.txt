REVIEW OF FINANCIAL RESULTS AND FINANCIAL CONDITION
Revenue
The Group recorded a decrease in revenue to RM3.90 billion for the financial year ended 30 June 2024 (“FY2024”) as against
RM5.35 billion recorded in the preceding financial year ended 30 June 2023 (“FY2023”). This decrease of 27% was driven by lower demand from Africa region, mainly West Africa, and Middle East region. In Africa, this
was due to the Coup d’état that occurred in Niger in July 2023 which escalated into a political crisis affecting neighbouring countries
in West Africa. Demand from the Middle East weakened as a result of the crisis in the Red Sea region which led to a sharp increase
in freight costs affecting shipments to Middle East and East Africa ports. As our primary operations is the export of fast moving consumer goods, the weakened MYR relative to USD throughout FY2024 resulted
in favourable average foreign exchange rates to cushion the demand challenges in the financial year under review. The opening of
MYR/USD commenced the financial year at MYR 4.60 per 1 USD and peaked at MYR 4.77 in April 2024, ending at MYR 4.71 for
June 2024.
[MDA_P006]
The overall average for the year stood at RM4.70/USD. This is a 4.47% increase as against the previous financial year
average of MYR 4.495 to 1 USD. The Group has successfully navigated global challenges by expanding its product range and diversifying its sales mix, which has
minimised revenue losses and reduced reliance on a single product, showcasing the team’s adaptability and strategic thinking. Profit After Tax (“PAT”) And Expenses
The Group’s PAT decrease of 30% to RM 117.17 million for the current year from RM166.51 million in the previous year is in line
with the decrease in revenue due to market challenges mentioned above. While absolute PAT saw a decrease during the year, the
overall PAT and Gross Profit margin still remain strong at 3.01% and 6.49%, respectively for the Group compared to 6.66% and
3.11% in FY2023. Operating expenses decreased from RM165.78 million to RM 113.71 million during the year, a 31% decrease compared to the
previous year. The decrease was due to lower realised/unrealised gain from derivatives arising from hedged foreign exchange
contracts of our sales proceeds.
[MDA_P007]
This is consistent with the more stable currency fluctuation and lower overall volume throughout the year
under review compared to the previous financial year. Finance costs decreased by 8% from RM17.08 million to RM15.71 million in the reporting period. This is mainly due to lower utilisation
of trade facilities aimed at funding the necessary working capital to fulfil sales orders. As a result, the finance cost also increased to
0.4% compared to 0.32% in the previous year due to the lower revenue. Assets: Trade Receivables
Trade receivables decreased by 46% from RM706.23 million to RM383.62 million, with the average outstanding period shortening
from 48 days in FY2023 to 36 days in FY2024. The drop in revenue resulted in a RM322 million reduction in receivables. Although this may not be seen as entirely favourable, the receivable turnover days have improved significantly, reducing by 12
days due to the lower overall receivables from customers compared to the previous reporting period. Assets: Inventories
There was an increase in inventories by 4%, from RM71.68 million in the previous financial year to RM74.56 million.
