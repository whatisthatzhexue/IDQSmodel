CHINA OUHUA WINERY HOLDINGS LIMITED
Overview of Performance
Prospects
[MDA_P002]
Annual Report 2021
[MDA_P003]
a) Revenue
For FY 2021, China Ouhua, which is principally involved in importing and sales of grape wines with its sole
manufacturing plant located in Yantai City, Shandong Province, China recorded a revenue decline of
23.31% or RMB 4.778 million from RMB 20.499 million in FY 2020 to RMB 15.721 million in current financial
year. Principal target market of China Ouhua’s wine products is throughout China. For FY 2021, the sales
breakdown between red wine and white wine were 100% and 0.00% respectively. Given that the wine market in
China remains highly competitive and due to the prolonged impact of the COVID-19 pandemic, Management
had made many efforts with the objective to improve the sales revenue. Although the Company has enhanced
the sales of imported wine, the total sales revenue did not improve. b) Profit(Loss) after Taxation
Loss for FY 2021 was RMB 4.263 million as compared with FY 2020's loss of RMB 16.736 million.The higher loss
figure in FY 2020 was mainly due to the impairment of inventories, which amounted to RMB12.898 million, and
by comparison for FY 2021 the impairment loss was RMB0.399 million.
[MDA_P004]
Notwithstanding the lower impairment loss recorded for FY2021, the operating results were still badly affected
by the prolonged impact of the COVID-19 pandemic. c) Liquidity and Capital Resources
As at FY 2021, China Ouhua had cash and cash equivalent amounting to RMB 23.83 million with no external
borrowing. Total current assets was RMB 165.28 million and total current liability was RMB5.49 million. Therefore, China Ouhua is confident that its existing financial standing is sufficient to finance its working capital
requirements for the next 24 months. Moving forward, in the next financial year, the Management will continue to work hard to improve the sales
revenue. In view of the prolonged COVID-19 pandemic, the Company will be facing challenging times ahead. The Management of the Company is confident to lead the Company to a new era by diversifying into
importation of overseas wine to market in new areas and online business.
