MANAGEMENT DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS
The Statement on Management Discussion and Analysis (“MD&A”) provides an insight of
the financial performance of Able Global Berhad (“AGB”) and its subsidiaries (“the Group”)
for the financial year ended 31 December 2022 (“FYE 2022”). This MD&A also contains
information on the Group’s overview of business and operations, current financial position,
reviewing of operating activities, assessing the operational risks faced by the Group and
discussing the future prospect of the Group.
